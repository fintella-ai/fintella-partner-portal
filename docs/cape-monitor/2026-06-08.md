## 1. ⚠️ MAJOR: DOJ Appeals CIT Refund Order for Finally-Liquidated Entries (June 2, 2026)

**What changed**: On May 29, 2026, CBP filed a motion asserting it lacks legal authority to refund "finally liquidated" entries (entries whose protest window has expired, i.e., >180 days post-liquidation) without an importer-specific court judgment. On **June 2, 2026**, the DOJ formally appealed to the U.S. Court of Appeals for the Federal Circuit.

**What this means for brokers**:
- Entries the calculator flags as `excluded_expired` / `filingMethod: "litigation"` now face a contested legal landscape
- CBP is asserting it will not voluntarily process refunds for finally-liquidated entries even in future CAPE phases, despite prior FAQ guidance to the contrary
- The appeal targets the CIT's universal injunction (Euro-Notions Florida, Inc. v. United States) that ordered refunds for all importers including non-litigants
- A hearing was scheduled for June 9, 2026 with CBP Commissioner Rodney S. Scott required to appear in person; CBP contested this requirement

**Implication for the calculator**: The `excluded_expired` reason string was updated to surface this to brokers. The `filingMethod: "litigation"` is unchanged (it remains the only structural path), but advisors must flag the appeal uncertainty to clients.

**What importers with finally-liquidated entries should do**: Consult trade counsel immediately. Consider filing protective actions at the CIT given the two-year statute of limitations for such actions.

**Sources**:
- Sheppard / National Law Review: "From CAPE to Cope? CBP Reverses Position on Universal IEEPA Duty Refunds"
- DWT: "Trump Administration to Appeal Order to Refund IEEPA Duties on Finally Liquidated Entries" (June 2026)
- BDO: "Update on CBP IEEPA Refund Progress and New Orders from the U.S. Court of International Trade"

## 2. New ACE Report: ES-022 (CAPE Entry Summary Report)

**What changed**: CBP added a new ACE report, **ES-022 (CAPE Entry Summary Report)**, effective approximately May 4, 2026 (per CSMS #68536553). This report was not in the original monitoring list.

**What ES-022 shows**:
- Ties CAPE declaration numbers to individual entry summaries
- Shows per-entry refund amounts (principal + interest)
- Blank refund columns = accepted but not yet calculated/disbursed
- The primary report linking a CAPE claim number to entry-level refund status

**Recommended ACE report sequence** (updated):
```
CAPE Tab → ES-022 → ES-701 → REV-603 → REV-615 → REV-613
```

| Report | Purpose |
|--------|---------|
| CAPE Tab | Claim submission status (accepted/rejected/pending) |
| ES-022 | Per-entry refund amounts + claim linkage (NEW) |
| ES-701 | Courtesy Notice of Liquidation (reliquidation confirmation) |
| REV-603 | Trade Refund Report — confirms payment left CBP |
| REV-615 | Trade CAPE Detail Refund Report — CAPE-specific consolidated view |
| REV-613 | ACH Rejected Refunds — flags missing/bad ACH enrollment |

**Source**: CSMS #68536553; C.H. Robinson client advisory (May 2, 2026); Mohawk Global

## 3. Updated Rejection / Acceptance Rate

**What changed**: The previously tracked rejection rate of ~15% (CBS News, early May) appears to have been a snapshot. As of mid-May 2026, per Judge Richard Eaton in court filings:

- **~21% acceptance on first pass** (i.e., ~79% initial rejection rate)
- Of accepted filings, ~3% were already in refund disbursement stage as of early May 2026
- First ACH refunds went out on or around **May 12, 2026**

**Common rejection causes** (updated, per trade publications):
1. HTS classification mismatch (declared HTS ≠ CBP's entry record)
2. Valuation inconsistency (declared value ≠ CBP record)
3. ACH not enrolled for refunds (duty-payment ACH alone is insufficient)
4. Entry number formatting errors
5. ENTRY ON DRAWBACK (entry flagged as on drawback)
6. NO IEEPA HTS ON ENTRY (no Chapter 99 IEEPA HTS code on the entry)
7. STATEMENT PROCESSING NOT COMPLETE
8. PROTEST ON ENTRY / ENTRY SUMMARY UNDER REVIEW

**Note**: The CAPE portal briefly crashed on April 20, 2026 launch day due to high traffic volume.

**Source**: Court filing by Judge Eaton; search synthesis across trade publications
