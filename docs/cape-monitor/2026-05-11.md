## 3. Error taxonomy — Formal error codes confirmed

CBP's "ACE Portal — CAPE Declarations and Error Definitions" document (published April 2026) defines three error categories:

| Category | Scope | Common triggers |
|---|---|---|
| **VAL-F** | File-level (rejects entire declaration) | Wrong encoding, bad line endings, >9,999 rows, duplicate entry numbers, missing header row |
| **VAL-E** | Entry-level (rejects affected entry only) | Entry not in CBP records, outside IEEPA window, no liquidation date, already refunded |
| **VAL-I** | Identity (file or entry level) | IOR, filer code, or ACH payer ID doesn't match active CBP registration |

Specific numbered error codes confirmed in practitioner guidance:

| Code | Meaning | Maps to old name |
|---|---|---|
| 1003 | IEEPA HTS code not present on original entry | NO IEEPA HTS ON ENTRY |
| 1004 | Claimed duty amount exceeds amount on record | (new) |

Previously-known named error codes (ENTRY ON DRAWBACK, PROTEST ON ENTRY, ENTRY SUMMARY UNDER REVIEW, STATEMENT PROCESSING NOT COMPLETE) remain in effect but may have corresponding numeric codes not yet confirmed in public guidance.

**Practical note from trade practitioners:** Excel-generated CSVs can embed invisible formatting characters that trigger VAL-F. Filers should verify CSV in a plain text editor before upload.

## 5. Rejection rate update

CBP filed a declaration with the Court of International Trade (CIT) on April 28, 2026, covering data through April 26, 2026 — six days after launch. Key stats:

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Total CAPE declarations submitted | 75,306 |
| Declarations passing file-level validation (VAL-F) | 47,315 (63%) |
| Declarations failing file-level validation | 27,991 (37%) |
| Total entries in file-valid declarations | 11,222,927 |
| Entries cleared ALL validations | ~1,740,000 (~15.5% of entries in valid files) |
| Entries in active refund processing | ~1,740,000 (~3% of total estimated IEEPA-window entries) |

**Context:** The ~15% entry-level clearance rate reflects the system's first 6 days with high first-time filer volume and a backlog of unprocessed declarations. The actual long-term clearance rate is expected to be higher as processing catches up and filers resubmit corrected declarations.

**Correction to CLAUDE.md baseline:** The previous note of "~15% rejection rate (CBS News)" appears to reflect the **pass rate** (~15% of entries cleared), not rejection rate. The file-level rejection rate is ~37% of declarations. The entry-level rejection-equivalent (entries failing validation) is ~84–85% as of April 26. This figure will improve as CBP processes the queue.

## 6. ACH / payment timeline

- **First ACH payments:** Targeting May 12, 2026 (based on April 20 launch + ~22 processing days)
- **Standard timeline:** 60–90 days from CAPE Declaration acceptance
- **No ACH = no refund:** CBP holds funds until ACH is enrolled; filers who lack ACH on file must add it via the ACE Secure Data Portal → Importer sub-account → ACH Refund Authorization tab
- **REV-613** identifies held refunds due to ACH rejection or missing enrollment
- **No changes** to the ACH enrollment process or requirements detected

## 7. Excluded entries — alternative paths

For entries excluded from CAPE Phase 1 (types 08/09/23/47, historically liquidated entries, entries with pending AD/CVD, surety-paid entries), current practitioner guidance identifies two alternative paths:

1. **Court-ordered refunds** — via the Court of International Trade litigation (V.O.S. Selections et al.) — attorneys required
2. **Protest** — entries within the 180-day protest window may use standard CBP protest mechanism — not limited to 80 days; attorneys typically recommended

These are the `legal_required` routing bucket in the calculator and are not affected by this monitor cycle.
