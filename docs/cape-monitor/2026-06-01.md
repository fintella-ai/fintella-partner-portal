## 5. Rejection rate update

**Known baseline**: ~15% (CBS News, pre/early launch estimate)

**New CBP data** (as of May 11, 2026, per CBP public reporting):
- 126,237 CAPE declarations submitted since April 20 launch
- 86,874 declarations passed file-level validations (~69% pass rate; ~31% file-validation rejection)
- 15,123,221 individual entries accepted for IEEPA duty removal
- 8,338,081 of those entries liquidated/reliquidated without IEEPA duties
- **$35.46 billion** in estimated duty refunds + interest (for the ~8.3M liquidated entries)
- **1,880** consolidated refunds stuck in reject status due to missing ACH bank info — not transmitted to Treasury

Note: one third-party site (customsgenius.com) reported ~79% first-pass rejection, attributing it to HTS mismatches, valuation discrepancies, and COO issues. This conflicts sharply with the CBP data showing 15M entries accepted. That figure is likely mis-attributed or measures a different population. The ~31% file-validation failure rate at the declaration level (86K/126K) is more plausible given known error codes.

**Common rejection codes** (no changes from known list):
- `ENTRY ON DRAWBACK`
- `PROTEST ON ENTRY`
- `ENTRY SUMMARY UNDER REVIEW`
- `NO IEEPA HTS ON ENTRY` — entry has no IEEPA HTS code; most common cause of `excluded_type`
- `STATEMENT PROCESSING NOT COMPLETE`
- New patterns noted in May 2026: HTS classification mismatches, valuation tolerance breaches, COO discrepancies

**No code change.** `EXCLUDED_ENTRY_TYPES` and error logic are current.

## 6. ACH enrollment — active issue

**New finding**: As of May 11, 2026, CBP is actively warning the trade community about ACH enrollment gaps:

- 1,880 refunds are **blocked in reject status** because IOR ACH bank account info is missing or incomplete in ACE
- Treasury cannot release funds until the IOR updates their ACE portal with valid U.S. banking info under the "Importer" sub-account → "ACH Refund Authorization" tab
- First ACH payments reached importers as early as **May 12, 2026** (confirmed by CBP May 4 guidance and Troutman reporting)

**Broker action**: advise IOR clients to verify ACH enrollment in ACE before filing or immediately after acceptance.

**No code change** to `generateCapeCsv()` or `checkEligibility()`. Consider surfacing an ACH enrollment warning in the broker UI as a next-task.
