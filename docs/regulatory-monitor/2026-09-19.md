### 3. CAPE Phase 2 — Reconciliation Entries (June 29, 2026)

**Status:** REVIEWED — no eligibility logic change required  
**Source:** CBP Trade Information Notice (July 23, 2026), CSMS #68340863

Effective June 29, 2026, CAPE began accepting entries of **Type 01, 02, 06** that carry a reconciliation flag but for which the reconciliation entry (Type 09) has NOT yet been filed. Previously these were blocked from CAPE processing.

Key clarifications:
- **Type 09 (reconciliation entries themselves) remain excluded from CAPE.** The `EXCLUDED_ENTRY_TYPES` set `["08", "09", "23", "47"]` in `tariff-calculator.ts` is correct and unchanged.
- **Type 47 drawback entries remain excluded.** No change.
- The June 29 update affects CBP's internal CAPE processing for flagged-but-unfiled reconciliation scenarios. The calculator's `checkEligibility()` function does not currently track the `hasReconFlag / reconFiled` distinction. For the current IEEPA refund period (pre-Feb 23, 2026 entries), this distinction is unlikely to affect most users, but a future enhancement could add a `hasUnfiledRecon?: boolean` flag to `EntryForEligibility` to distinguish the pre-June-29 and post-June-29 CAPE behavior.
- **Drawback (Type 47):** Still excluded from CAPE per the July 23, 2026 CBP notice. No Phase 2 expansion for drawback yet.
