## 2. CAPE Phase 3 launched October 6, 2026 — CALCULATOR UPDATED

CBP deployed CAPE Phase 3 on October 6, 2026 (2 days ago). Phase 3 covers **finally liquidated entries** for importers with active CIT cases who registered with CBP by July 30, 2026.

**What changed in `tariff-calculator.ts`:**
- `FilingMethod` type: added `"cape_phase3"` 
- `EntryForEligibility`: added `isCitPlaintiff?: boolean` field
- `checkEligibility()`: entries liquidated >180 days ago for CIT plaintiffs now route to `cape_phase3` instead of generic `litigation`, with a `needsReview: true` flag (government is appealing the reliquidation authority)
- Updated `excluded_expired` reason string to mention Phase 3 as an option for non-plaintiffs who file suit

**Key eligibility restriction:** Phase 3 is **only** for importers who (a) have their own pending CIT case, AND (b) registered their IOR number with CBP by July 30, 2026. Non-litigants with finally liquidated entries have no confirmed CAPE path — they must file their own CIT suit.

**CIT progress as of Sept 11, 2026:** CBP had accepted ~$134.7B in potential IEEPA refunds; ~$122B disbursed to Treasury.

## 4. Section 122 CIT/CAFC Litigation — MONITORING, NO CODE CHANGE

The CIT struck down Section 122 tariffs on May 7, 2026 (*State of Oregon v. Trump*, 1:26-cv-01472), but the Federal Circuit stayed the ruling on June 11, 2026. Collection continued until the statutory expiry on July 24, 2026. The CAFC appeal on the merits remains pending as of October 2026.

**Impact on calculator:** None currently. Section 122 expired by its own terms. If the Federal Circuit affirms the CIT ruling, Section 122 duty refunds may become available to plaintiffs — but this is a future event. Added litigation status to `S122-01.notes` in seed data.
