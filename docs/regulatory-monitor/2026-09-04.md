## 1. Section 301 Forced-Labor Tariffs — Replaced Section 122 on July 24, 2026

### What changed
The Section 122 10% global surcharge expired at 12:01 AM ET on July 24, 2026 — exactly 150 days from its February 24 effective date, exhausting its statutory maximum. The Trump administration had pre-staged Section 301 forced-labor tariffs to take effect simultaneously with no gap.

**Section 301 structure (effective July 24, 2026):**
- **10% rate** — applied to ~46 economies found to have at least partial forced-labor import protections (includes UK, India, Canada, Mexico, EU, Japan, South Korea, Switzerland, Australia, Brazil, and others)
- **12.5% rate** — applied to ~14 economies with weaker forced-labor enforcement (includes China, Vietnam, and others)
- **Coverage:** ~60 economies total, covering ~99.4% of U.S. imports by value
- **HTS subheadings:** Per-economy Chapter 99 headings `9903.05.20` through `9903.05.84`
- **Exemptions:** USMCA/CAFTA-DR qualifying goods, Section 232 goods, civil aircraft, pharmaceuticals, specified HTS lines per U.S. Note 52 to Subchapter III of Chapter 99
- **CBP guidance:** CSMS #69326983 (issued July 24, 2026) — entry filing instructions including Chapter 99 headings, reporting sequence, and FTZ admission rules

### Impact on the calculator
Section 301 duties are **not eligible for IEEPA refunds**. The tariff refund calculator's eligibility scope (`IEEPA_START_DATE = 2025-02-01`, `IEEPA_END_DATE = 2026-02-23`) is unaffected. The new rate entries are added for context/historical completeness — so the rate database accurately reflects the full tariff timeline — but no eligibility or rate calculation logic changes.

## 2. IRS Q3 2026 Interest Rate Increase (7% Overpayment)

### What changed
The IRS announced that interest rates for Q3 2026 (July 1 – September 30, 2026) increased back to **7% for individual overpayments** (up from 6% in Q2 2026). Rates are set under IRC §6621(b) as 3 percentage points above the federal short-term rate; the short-term rate recovered to 4% for Q3.

**Q3 2026 rates:**
- Individual overpayment: **7%** (compounded daily)
- Corporate overpayment: 6%
- Corporate overpayment (large, >$10K): 4.5%
- Individual underpayment: 7%
- Large-corporation underpayment: 9%

### Impact on the calculator
The `calculateInterest()` function in `tariff-calculator.ts` uses these quarterly rates to compute daily-compounded interest on IEEPA duty refunds per 19 U.S.C. §1505. IEEPA duty deposit dates range from Feb 4, 2025 through Feb 23, 2026. Refund dates depend on when CBP processes the CAPE claim. Adding Q3 2026 ensures interest calculations are accurate for claims processed July–September 2026.

## 3. CAPE Phase 3 Postponed (As of August 21, 2026)

### What changed
CBP's CAPE Phase 3 was originally targeted for launch on **August 20, 2026**. On August 21, 2026, CBP announced the delay "until further notice" to allow for additional ACE system validations.

**What Phase 3 was designed to cover:**
- "Finally liquidated" entries — those liquidated more than 80 days ago that can no longer be automatically processed through the standard CAPE window
- A July 15, 2026 CIT order directed CBP to reliquidate approximately 3,700 finally-liquidated IEEPA cases ahead of Phase 3
- Estimated CAPE progress as of Aug 21, 2026: 272,029 declarations submitted, $132.5 billion in refunds accepted, $106.6 billion completed and sent to Treasury

**Type 47 drawback entries:** Remain excluded from all CAPE phases with no confirmed future inclusion path. Type 47 importers must pursue refunds via §1581(i) litigation or protests.

**Warehouse entries (Types 21/22):** Were originally accepted in Phase 1 but excluded effective July 7, 2026 — any submission on or after that date is rejected with "ENTRY TYPE NOT ALLOWED."

### Impact on the calculator
The current `checkEligibility()` logic correctly routes:
- Unliquidated + liquidated-within-80-days entries → `cape_phase1` ✅ 
- Liquidated 80–180 days ago → `protest` ✅
- Liquidated > 180 days ago → `litigation` ✅ (Phase 3 would have added a CAPE path here, but is postponed — status quo is correct)

No logic change is needed. Comments updated in `tariff-calculator.ts` to alert future developers that Phase 3 is pending and may change the `litigation` bucket.
