## 1. CAPE Phase 3 — Launched October 6, 2026 ✅ CODE UPDATED

**Source:** [CBP Confirms October 6, 2026 Launch of Phase 3 — Thompson Hines SmartTrade](https://www.thompsonhinesmartrade.com/2026/09/cbp-confirms-october-6-2026-launch-of-phase-3-of-the-ieepa-tariff-refund-process/), [CBP IEEPA Duty Refunds page](https://www.cbp.gov/trade/programs-administration/trade-remedies/ieepa-duty-refunds)

**What changed:** CBP launched CAPE Phase 3 on October 6, 2026. Unlike Phases 1 and 2 (automated, self-service for unliquidated and recently-liquidated entries), Phase 3 covers **finally-liquidated entries** that have been subject to **court-ordered reliquidation**. Participation is limited to importers who are **CIT plaintiffs** — not available for general importers self-filing.

As of Oct 6, CBP reported ~$136.6 billion in potential/certified refunds accepted for processing, with ~$126 billion sent to Treasury.

**Why it matters for the calculator:** The existing `excluded_expired` path (entries where the 180-day protest window has closed) previously said "CIT litigation only." Phase 3 means these importers now have a distinct CBP processing pathway available — but only after a court order, not self-service.

**Code change made:**
- Added `"cape_phase3"` to the `FilingMethod` type in `src/lib/tariff-calculator.ts`
- Updated the `excluded_expired` reason message to explain Phase 3 and the class action
- Routing bucket remains `"legal_required"` (correct — CIT involvement is mandatory)

## 2. CIT Class Certification — October 8, 2026 ✅ CODE UPDATED

**Source:** [CIT Slip Op. 26-123 (PDF)](https://www.cit.uscourts.gov/sites/cit/files/26-123.pdf), [Trade Compliance Flash — Miller Chevalier](https://www.millerchevalier.com/publication/trade-compliance-flash-court-international-trade-certifies-class-action-ieepa-tariff-refunds), [Trade Law Daily](https://tradelawdaily.com/article/2026/10/09/cit-judge-allows-lead-ieepa-refunds-case-to-proceed-as-class-action-2610080056?BC=bc_6ac8d86034f7a)

**What changed:** On October 8, 2026, Judge Richard Eaton issued Slip Op. 26-123 in *Freestyle World, Inc. v. United States* (Court No. 26-01088), certifying a **nationwide class** of importers who paid IEEPA duties from February 1, 2025 through February 19, 2026. Exclusions: importers who have already filed their own CIT suits, and certain Brazil/India-related tariff payments.

**Important caveats:**
- Class certification alone does **not** order CBP to reliquidate or issue refunds
- No class-wide reliquidation injunction has issued as of Oct 29, 2026
- Government appeal status unclear; sources disagree on whether one has been filed at the Federal Circuit
- A scheduling order was reportedly due Oct 22, 2026 (not confirmed issued)

**Code change made:** Updated `excluded_expired` reason string to inform importers of the class certification and their potential class membership.

## 3. Section 122 Expiry — Confirmed July 23–24, 2026 ✅ DATA UPDATED

**Source:** [CustomsGenius — 9903.03.02 (ended)](https://www.customsgenius.com/articles/9903-03-02-ended-section-122-surcharge-exemption-explained), [Section 122 Exemption Structure — Global Trade Alert](https://globaltradealert.org/blog/s122-exemption-structure-explained)

**What changed:** The Section 122 10% global surcharge (9903.03.01 through 9903.03.11) expired at the close of July 23, 2026 (codes removed from HTSUS effective July 24, 2026). This was expected (150-day statutory maximum from Feb 24, 2026 = July 23, 2026).

**Important:** As of October 29, 2026, **no replacement tariff authority is in effect**. No new executive order, presidential proclamation, or IEEPA action has been issued to impose new tariffs. The tariff environment for imports is currently the standard Column 1 rates (plus Section 232, Section 301 where applicable).

**Data change made:** Updated `S122-01.notes` to confirm expiry; updated `_metadata.version` to `1.1.0` and `generatedDate` to `2026-10-29`.
