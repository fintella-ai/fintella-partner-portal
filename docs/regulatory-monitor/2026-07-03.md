## Finding 1 — CAPE Phase 2 Launched June 29, 2026 ✅ CHANGE MADE

**Source**: CSMS #69035485 (June 23, 2026); CSMS #69066837 (deployment confirmation June 29, 2026)  
**URL**: https://content.govdelivery.com/accounts/USDHSCBP/bulletins/41d65dd

### What changed

CBP deployed CAPE Phase 2 in the ACE Portal on June 29, 2026. Phase 2 expands eligibility to **entries flagged for reconciliation** — specifically entry types **01, 02, and 06** — provided the associated reconciliation entry (Type 09) has **not yet been filed**.

Previously (Phase 1), these reconciliation-flagged entries were operationally rejected by ACE even though their entry type codes were not in the excluded types set.

### Key rules for reconciliation-flagged entries (Phase 2)

- **Same liquidation window applies**: entries must be unliquidated OR liquidated within 80 days of the CAPE Declaration filing date
- **Filing sequence is mandatory**: submit the CAPE Declaration FIRST; ACE strips the IEEPA duties from the flagged entries; THEN file the Type 09 reconciliation entry. Filing Type 09 before the CAPE Declaration locks those entries out of Phase 2
- **Type 09 already filed = excluded**: entries where the reconciliation entry has already been submitted are NOT covered by Phase 2; a future CAPE phase will address those
- **Type 47 (Drawback) remains excluded**: Phase 2 does not cover drawback entries
- **Type 08 (Duty Deferral) and Type 23 (TIB) remain excluded**

### Code change made

Updated the `EXCLUDED_ENTRY_TYPES` block comment in `src/lib/tariff-calculator.ts` (lines 208–220) to document the Phase 2 expansion and the critical filing sequence requirement. Also updated the `CAPE_PHASE1_LIQUIDATION_WINDOW_DAYS` comment to note that the 80-day window applies to both Phase 1 and Phase 2.

The excluded set itself (`["08", "09", "23", "47"]`) did not change — Type 01/02/06 entries were already passing through the type check; Phase 2 means CBP's ACE system now actually processes them.

## Finding 2 — Section 122 Tariff: CIT Struck Down, CAFC Stayed; Expiring July 24 ✅ CHANGE MADE

**Primary source (CIT ruling)**: Slip Op. 26-47 — https://www.cit.uscourts.gov/sites/cit/files/26-47.pdf  
**CAFC stay**: Federal Circuit, May 12, 2026 (docket not publicly accessible)  
**USTR Section 301**: https://ustr.gov/about/policy-offices/press-office/press-releases/2026/june/ustr-makes-findings-and-proposes-action-60-section-301-investigations-relating-failures-take-action

### What changed — CIT Ruling (May 7, 2026)

In **Oregon v. United States** and **Burlap and Barrel, Inc. v. United States** (consolidated), a divided CIT 3-judge panel (2-1) ruled Proclamation 11012's 10% Section 122 surcharge invalid. The majority held the proclamation failed to identify the type of balance-of-payments deficit required by 19 U.S.C. §2132.

**Practical effect is limited**: permanent injunction applies only to the 3 named plaintiff importers. All other importers remain obligated to pay Section 122 duties.

### CAFC Stay (May 12, 2026)

The government appealed May 8, 2026. The Federal Circuit issued an administrative stay of the CIT injunction on May 12, 2026, and a more durable stay pending appeal was subsequently granted. **Section 122 tariffs continue to be collected for all importers** while the appeal proceeds at the CAFC.

### Expiration: July 24, 2026 (21 days from today)

Section 122 has a statutory 150-day maximum. The surcharge **automatically expires July 24, 2026**. The President cannot extend it unilaterally — only Congress can, and that is considered unlikely given the Senate filibuster threshold.

### Likely replacement: Section 301

USTR announced findings and proposed action in 60+ Section 301 investigations (forced labor practices, 59 countries + EU) on June 2, 2026:
- Proposed rates: 10% for 15 trading partners, 12.5% for 45 others
- Public comment period closed July 6, 2026; public hearing July 7, 2026
- Section 301 tariffs widely expected to take effect on or around July 24, 2026

**If Section 301 tariffs are finalized before July 24**: they would replace the Section 122 surcharge with new coverage that needs new entries in `ieepa-tariff-rates.json`. **The calculator team should watch for a USTR final action press release in the week of July 7–18.** That would trigger a follow-up monitor run and a new seed data entry.

**If no replacement is finalized**: the 10% global surcharge disappears on July 24. MFN rates, existing Section 301 tariffs on Chinese goods, and Section 232 duties (steel, aluminum, copper) remain unchanged.

### Code change made

Updated `S122-01` record in `prisma/seed-data/ieepa-tariff-rates.json`:
- Corrected `executiveOrder` field to include the proclamation number ("Proclamation 11012")
- Expanded `notes` to include the CIT ruling, CAFC stay, expiration countdown, and Section 301 replacement outlook

The `endDate` of `2026-07-23` was already correct.
