## Changes Found

### 1. Section 122 expired July 24, 2026 — replaced by Section 301 forced-labor tariffs

**Source**: [Federal Register 2026-15181](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/07/28/2026-15181/notice-of-actions-in-section-301-investigations-of-acts-policies-and-practices-of-various-economies); [USTR press release](https://ustr.gov/about/policy-offices/press-office/press-releases/2026/july/ustr-takes-action-forced-labor-section-301-investigations); [EY Tax News](https://taxnews.ey.com/news/2026-1607-ustr-finalizes-section-301-forced-labor-tariffs-on-60-economies-additional-tariffs-of-10-percent-or-125-percent-take-effect-24-july-2026)

The Section 122 global surcharge (10%, 9903.03.01) **expired at 12:01 AM EDT July 24, 2026** after 150 days, its statutory maximum. Immediately replaced by new **Section 301 forced-labor tariffs** under USTR authority:

- **Two-tier structure** on 60 economies:
  - **10% (Tier 1)**: economies with qualifying forced-labor import prohibition
  - **12.5% (Tier 2)**: economies without qualifying prohibition (majority of the 60)
- **HTS codes**: 9903.04.xx series
- **Exemptions**: country-specific product exemptions (9903.05.96–9903.06.21) for 13 economies; pharmaceutical exemptions (9903.04.60–9903.04.66, effective Jul 31, 2026)
- **In-transit grace**: goods loaded before Jul 24 and entered before Jul 28 exempt
- **Federal Register**: 2026-15181, published Jul 28, 2026

**Impact on calculator**: These tariffs do NOT create new IEEPA refund claims — entries from July 24, 2026 onward are outside the IEEPA period (Feb 4, 2025 – Feb 23, 2026) and are not eligible for CAPE. The seed data is updated for informational completeness only.

**Code change**: `prisma/seed-data/ieepa-tariff-rates.json`
- Updated `S122-01.supersededBy` from `null` to `"S301-FL-01"`
- Updated `S122-01.notes` to confirm expiration
- Added `S301-FL-01` (10% Tier 1) and `S301-FL-02` (12.5% Tier 2) entries with `rateType: "section301"`

### 2. CAPE Phase 2 launched June 29, 2026 — reconciliation-flagged entries now eligible

**Source**: [CBP CSMS #68340863 update](https://www.cbp.gov/trade/programs-administration/trade-remedies/ieepa-duty-refunds); [CBP CAPE Trade Information Notice Jul 2026](https://www.cbp.gov/sites/default/files/2026-07/26_0723_trade_information_notice_CAPE.pdf); [Holland & Knight](https://www.hklaw.com/en/insights/publications/2026/04/cape-has-arrived-navigating-the-next-phase-of-ieepa-duty-refunds); [Tariffstool](https://www.tariffstool.com/guides/cape-phase-2-launch-date-whats-coming-may-2026)

CAPE Phase 2 launched June 29, 2026, expanding eligibility to **entries flagged for reconciliation (entry types 01, 02, 06)** for which the Type 09 reconciliation entry has not yet been filed. These entries were excluded from Phase 1.

Key rules for Phase 2 reconciliation-flagged entries:
- Entry must be **unliquidated**, OR liquidated **within 80 days** (same window as Phase 1)
- 180-day protest deadline still applies for liquidated entries
- **Type 47 drawback still excluded** (planned for Phase 3)
- **Type 09 entries themselves** (filed reconciliation entries) still excluded
- **Historically liquidated entries** (past 80 days, no protest filed) still Phase 3 — no change

**Code change**: `src/lib/tariff-calculator.ts`
- Added `"cape_phase2"` to `FilingMethod` type
- Added `isReconciliationFlagged?: boolean` field to `EntryForEligibility`
- Added Phase 2 branch in `checkEligibility()` (step 4a) — if `isReconciliationFlagged`, routes to `filingMethod: "cape_phase2"` while still applying the 80-day and 180-day liquidation windows
- Updated `EXCLUDED_ENTRY_TYPES` comment to clarify Type 09 and Phase 2 scope
- Added `section301` to `RateLookupResult.breakdown` type and `lookupCombinedRate` handler
