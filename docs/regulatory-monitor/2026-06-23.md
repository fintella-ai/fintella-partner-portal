### 5. Section 122 Updates — ⚠️ SIGNIFICANT: CIT Ruled Unlawful, CAFC Stay in Place

**IMPORTANT LEGAL STATUS CHANGE — updated in seed data (S122-01 notes):**

| Event | Date | Detail |
|-------|------|--------|
| CIT ruling | May 7, 2026 | 2-1 panel in *Oregon v. United States* + *Burlap and Barrel v. United States* struck down Section 122 tariffs as unlawful |
| CAFC admin stay | May 12, 2026 | CAFC temporarily stayed CIT injunction while considering full stay |
| CIT denied gov't stay | May 20, 2026 | CIT refused to stay its own order pending appeal |
| CAFC full stay | **June 11, 2026** | CAFC granted government's motion for stay pending appeal — injunction paused |

**What this means:**
- Section 122 tariffs are **still being collected** from all importers (including the three plaintiffs) while appeal proceeds
- The injunction was limited to only three plaintiffs: State of Washington, Burlap and Barrel Inc., Basic Fun Inc.
- CAFC "signaled skepticism of CIT's narrow statutory reading" — government's appeal looks likely to succeed on the merits
- Section 122 still expires **July 24, 2026** by statute regardless of appeal outcome — this cannot be extended by the President
- Congress could theoretically extend but is considered unlikely

**Calculator impact:**
- `S122-01.endDate` = `"2026-07-23"` remains correct (statutory expiry)
- `S122-01.rate` = 0.10 remains correct (no rate change)
- Notes updated in seed data to reflect litigation status
- No change to `checkEligibility()` needed — Section 122 tariffs are not IEEPA tariffs; CAPE does not process Section 122 refunds
