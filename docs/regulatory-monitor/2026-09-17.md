## Changes Made

### 1. IRS Q3 2026 Interest Rate Added (ACTIONABLE)

**What changed**: Q3 2026 (July 1 – September 30, 2026) IRS overpayment rate **increased from 6% to 7%** (individual; corporate 6%). Q2 was 6%. This rate applies to interest accruing on IEEPA refund principal that has not yet been paid out. CAPE claims filed in spring 2026 targeting the 45-day processing window may still be open; late-processed claims accrue interest at this higher rate.

**Source**: IRS newsroom — "Interest Rates Increase for Third Quarter of 2026" (Tax Notes); IRS quarterly interest rates page at https://www.irs.gov/payments/quarterly-interest-rates

**What changed**: Section 122 tariff (10% global surcharge, HTS 9903.03.01) expired on **July 24, 2026** as its 150-day statutory maximum ran out. The seed data already had the correct `endDate: "2026-07-23"` but the `supersededBy` field was `null`. Updated notes and set `supersededBy: "S301-01"`.
