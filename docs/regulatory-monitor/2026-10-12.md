### Federal Register 2026-20228: Court-Ordered IEEPA Refunds Worksheet (Oct 5, 2026)

**URL:** https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/10/05/2026-20228/agency-information-collection-activities-court-ordered-refunds-under-the-international-emergency

CBP published an OMB 30-day information collection notice (91 FR 63299) for a **Court-Ordered Refunds Under the IEEPA Worksheet**. Key facts:

- OMB Control No. 1651-0149 (extension of prior 60-day notice from July 8, 2026 — 91 FR 42207)
- Cites *Learning Resources, Inc. v. Trump* (SCOTUS Feb 20, 2026) as the legal basis
- **Specifically cites CIT cases *Atmus Filtration* and *Euro-Notions Florida v. United States*** as establishing the court-ordered refund process for finally-liquidated entries
- Scope: ~$166 billion in IEEPA duties assessed Feb 3, 2025 – Feb 24, 2026 across ~53 million entry summaries
- This worksheet is separate from CAPE — it covers court-ordered reliquidations for entries in the CIT litigation track

**Why this matters for the calculator:**  
Our current code routes entries with expired protest windows (>180 days post-liquidation) to `filingMethod: "litigation"`. This notice confirms CBP is formalizing the worksheet process for that track. If/when CBP publishes the final collection and the worksheet form, a new filing method (e.g., `"court_ordered"` or `"cit_worksheet"`) may be appropriate as a fourth path. **No code change warranted yet** — this is a comment-period notice, not a final rule or live process.

**Monitoring:** Watch federalregister.gov for the final OMB approval notice for collection 1651-0149 (expected within 30 days of Oct 5, i.e., ~Nov 4, 2026).
