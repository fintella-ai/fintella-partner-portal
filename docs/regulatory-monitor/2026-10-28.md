## 4. Court of International Trade — New Rulings ⚠️ NOTABLE

**CIT Slip Op. 26-123 — *Freestyle World v. United States* — Class Certified, October 8, 2026.**

Sources: [Trade Law Daily](https://tradelawdaily.com/article/2026/10/09/cit-judge-allows-lead-ieepa-refunds-case-to-proceed-as-class-action-2610080056), [BDO alert](https://www.bdo.com/insights/tax/cit-certifies-importer-class-seeking-ieepa-duty-refunds-key-considerations-for-importers), [Miller Chevalier](https://www.millermanager.com/publication/trade-compliance-flash-court-international-trade-certifies-class-action-ieepa-tariff), [Mohawk Global](https://mohawkglobal.com/trade-translation/cit-certifies-class-action-for-ieepa-tariff-refunds-what-importers-need-to-know/)

**What it means:**
- CIT has certified a class of all importers who paid IEEPA duties and have NOT filed their own individual suits. This creates a potential path for **finally-liquidated entries** (past the 180-day protest window) to receive refunds without individual litigation.
- As of early October, CBP had accepted ~$136.6 billion in CAPE-eligible potential refunds and transmitted ~$126 billion to Treasury.
- The class certification covers the gap left by Phase 3 (which hasn't formally launched for finally-liquidated entries). The government is appealing the underlying refund order covering finally-liquidated entries to the Federal Circuit.
- **Status:** Not final — subject to Federal Circuit appeal.

**Impact on the calculator:** The existing `"litigation"` `FilingMethod` for entries past the 180-day protest window remains accurate. However, the *Freestyle World* class certification means users routed to `filingMethod: "litigation"` now have an **automatic class action path** and may not need to file individual CIT suits. This is a messaging/UX improvement opportunity for John to consider, not a data or logic bug.

**Recommendation:** John may want to update the `reason` string for the `"excluded_expired"` / `filingMethod: "litigation"` path to mention the *Freestyle World* class action, so users know they don't necessarily need individual legal counsel for finally-liquidated entries. **No rush — this is an enhancement, not a correction.**
