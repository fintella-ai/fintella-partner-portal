### 4. ✅ Section 122 Expired July 24, 2026 + CIT Strike-Down (Stayed) — **SEED NOTES UPDATED**

**Sources:**
- [Section 122 Tariffs Expire — Honigman](https://www.honigman.com/alert-3462)
- [CIT Strikes Down Section 122 Tariffs — Skadden](https://www.skadden.com/insights/publications/2026/05/us-trade-court-strikes-down-section-122-tariffs)
- [Section 301 Replacing Section 122 — Tariffstool](https://www.tariffstool.com/guides/section-301-replacing-section-122)

**Changes:**
1. **Section 122 expired Jul 24, 2026** (150-day statutory cap). The seed already had `endDate: "2026-07-23"` — correct.
2. **CIT May 7, 2026**: 2-1 panel struck down Section 122 (held balance-of-payments conditions didn't meet statutory threshold). CAFC issued administrative stay May 12, 2026 — collection continued through expiry.
3. **Replacement Jul 24, 2026**: New Section 301 forced-labor duties (10–12.5%) on ~60–80 economies replaced Section 122 effective the same minute. No gap. Section 301 has no statutory time cap.

**What was updated:** Updated `S122-01.notes` in `prisma/seed-data/ieepa-tariff-rates.json` to document the CIT ruling, CAFC stay, and Section 301 replacement. Set `supersededBy: "S301-FORCED-LABOR-01"`.

**Note:** Section 301 is not IEEPA — the calculator's IEEPA refund logic is unaffected. No new refundable duties were created by Section 301.
