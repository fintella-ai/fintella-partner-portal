### 2. CBP CSMS — IEEPA / CAPE / Chapter 99 Updates
**Status: Informational update (CAPE Phase 2)**

CAPE Phase 2 launched officially on **June 29, 2026** (CSMS #68396594). Key details:
- Phase 2 expands CAPE to accept entry types **01, 02, and 06** that are flagged for reconciliation but have NOT yet filed their Type 09 reconciliation entry
- The underlying unliquidated entry must be unliquidated, or liquidated no more than 80 days prior
- **Type 47 drawback entries remain excluded** — CAPE Phase 3 (which would cover drawback) has been delayed by CBP to build additional validations
- As of Aug 21, 2026: ~$132.5B in IEEPA refunds accepted for CAPE processing; $106.6B sent to Treasury

**Code impact:** No changes to `EXCLUDED_ENTRY_TYPES` needed. Entry types 01/02/06 are already NOT in the excluded set, so Phase 2 reconciliation-flagged entries route through correctly. Type 47 ("47") remains in the excluded set pending Phase 3. Updated the `FilingMethod` JSDoc in `tariff-calculator.ts` to document this.

### 3. USTR — Trade Policy Announcements
**Status: Informational only (Section 301 successor tariff)**

Section 122 expired July 24, 2026 at its 150-day statutory limit. USTR immediately replaced it with new **Section 301 forced-labor country provisions**:
- 10% or 12.5% on goods from 60 trading partners
- HTS codes: **9903.05.20 through 9903.05.84**
- Effective: 12:01 AM EDT July 24, 2026
- Legal authority: Trade Act of 1974, Section 301 (19 U.S.C. 2411)
- Based on USTR investigations into forced labor practices

**IMPORTANT:** These Section 301 tariffs are NOT refundable under the IEEPA CAPE program. Only IEEPA duties paid Feb 1, 2025–Feb 23, 2026 are recoverable via CAPE. Section 301 tariffs are a prospective program.

**Code impact:** Added `S301-FL-01` entry to `prisma/seed-data/ieepa-tariff-rates.json` to document the succession chain. Updated `S122-01.supersededBy` from `null` to `"S301-FL-01"`. Notes clearly state these are NOT CAPE-eligible.

Source: [Wiley Law: New Forced Labor Tariffs](https://www.wiley.law/alert-New-Forced-Labor-Tariffs-Imposed-on-60-US-Trading-Partners) · [FreightFigures: Section 301 Live July 24](https://www.freightfigures.com/articles/section-301-forced-labor-tariffs-live-july-24-2026)
