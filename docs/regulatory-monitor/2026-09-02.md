## 5. FINDING #1 (ACTIONABLE) — Section 301 Forced-Labor Tariffs Replace Section 122

**Severity: HIGH — Seed data updated in this run**

### What changed
Effective **July 24, 2026 at 12:01 AM ET**, new Section 301 forced-labor tariffs replaced the expired Section 122 global surcharge. USTR determined that 60 major economies use forced or child labor in goods imported into the U.S., triggering a Section 301 (Trade Act of 1974, 19 U.S.C. § 2411) investigation and final determination.

### Rate structure
| HTS Code | Rate | Scope |
|---|---|---|
| 9903.05.20 | 10% | Baseline — most of the 60 covered economies |
| 9903.05.21–9903.05.84 | 12.5% | Higher-risk economies (per USTR final determination) |
| 9903.04.60–9903.04.66 | Exempt | Patented pharmaceutical articles (eff. Jul 31, 2026) |

USTR expanded the exemption list by 471 HTSUS subheadings after public comment (raw materials, supply-chain-critical goods, goods that cannot be domestically sourced in sufficient quantities).

## 6. FINDING #2 (NO CODE CHANGE) — CAPE Reconciliation-Flagged Entry Expansion

**Severity: LOW — No code change required**

Effective **June 29, 2026** (CSMS #69066837), CAPE now accepts consumption entries (types 01, 02, 06) that are **flagged for reconciliation but have not yet had the reconciliation entry (type 09) filed**. This addresses a gap where such entries were being held back.

### What does NOT change
- Type `09` (the reconciliation entry itself) remains excluded from CAPE — consistent with `EXCLUDED_ENTRY_TYPES` in `tariff-calculator.ts`
- Type `47` (drawback) remains excluded
- Entries where the reconciliation entry HAS ALREADY BEEN FILED remain in a future CAPE phase (not yet deployed)

### Assessment
The existing `EXCLUDED_ENTRY_TYPES = new Set(["08", "09", "23", "47"])` is **correct**. The June 29 expansion affects administrative processing of the underlying consumption entries in CAPE — not the entry type exclusion logic. Consumption entry types 01, 02, 06 were already eligible. No `tariff-calculator.ts` change needed.

**Recommend**: John may want to update any user-facing documentation or FAQ that says entries flagged for reconciliation are ineligible — they are now partially eligible (as long as the reconciliation entry is not filed).
