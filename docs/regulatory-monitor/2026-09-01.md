## Backfill Changes Implemented This Run

Three changes were identified from the period **June 29 – July 24, 2026** that had not yet been
applied to the calculator codebase. All three are implemented in this PR.

### 1. CAPE Phase 2 Launched June 29, 2026 ✅ IMPLEMENTED

**What changed:** CBP launched CAPE Phase 2 effective June 29, 2026. Phase 2 accepts
reconciliation-flagged entries (base entry types 01, 02, 06) where the Type-09 reconciliation
entry has *not yet been filed*. Under Phase 1, these entries were not accepted by the CAPE system.

**Eligibility window:** Same 80-day liquidation window as Phase 1 (measured from liquidation date
to date of CAPE filing). Unliquidated reconciliation-flagged entries are accepted with no deadline.

**Still excluded (all phases):**
- Type 09 (reconciliation entries themselves)
- Type 47 (drawback — separate ACE module; CAPE error: "ENTRY ON DRAWBACK")
- Entries where Type-09 has *already* been filed (future Phase — not yet announced)

**Code changes:**
- `src/lib/tariff-calculator.ts` — added `"cape_phase2"` to `FilingMethod` union type
- `src/lib/tariff-calculator.ts` — added `isReconFlagged?: boolean` to `EntryForEligibility`
- `src/lib/tariff-calculator.ts` — added Phase 2 routing block in `checkEligibility()` (step 4b)
- Updated `EXCLUDED_ENTRY_TYPES` JSDoc to document the Phase 1 vs Phase 2 distinction

**Source:** CBP Trade Information Notice (Jul 23, 2026):
https://www.cbp.gov/sites/default/files/2026-07/26_0723_trade_information_notice_CAPE.pdf

### 2. CAPE Phase 3 — Finally-Liquidated Entries (Delayed) ✅ IMPLEMENTED

**What changed:**
- July 15–17, 2026: CIT issued orders directing CBP to reliquidate finally-liquidated entries
  (liquidated > 80 days) across ~3,700 IEEPA cases.
- August 25, 2026: CBP confirmed Phase 3 is **temporarily delayed** while building new
  validations. Phase 3 will apply only to importers who have filed CIT lawsuits.

**Implication for calculator:** The "excluded_expired" path (entries liquidated > 180 days ago)
previously said "CIT litigation only." That is still accurate, but now the *mechanism* is clearer:
importers who filed CIT lawsuits may access Phase 3 when it launches. Importers who did NOT file
suit have no confirmed Phase 3 path.

**Code changes:**
- `src/lib/tariff-calculator.ts` — updated `reason` string in both "excluded_expired" branches
  to mention Phase 3 and the current delay (Aug 25 status).

### 3. Section 301 Forced Labor Tariffs Replace Section 122 (July 24, 2026) ✅ IMPLEMENTED

**What changed:** Section 122 (10% global surcharge, HTS 9903.03.01) expired July 24, 2026 at its
150-day statutory maximum. No congressional extension was passed. Simultaneously (12:01 AM ET July
24), USTR imposed new Section 301 forced-labor tariffs on imports from 60 economies:
- **10%** — countries with operative prohibitions on forced-labor imports
- **12.5%** — countries without such prohibitions

New HTS codes: **9903.05.20–9903.05.84** (country-specific); exemptions at 9903.05.85–9903.06.21
(USMCA/CAFTA-DR goods, civil aircraft, pharmaceuticals, Chapter 98 goods).

**⚠️ Calculator scope note:** Section 301 duties on entries after July 24, 2026 are **NOT
refundable** via the IEEPA CAPE process — they arise under a different legal authority. The
IEEPA refund calculator only covers duties paid on entries dated Feb 1, 2025 – Feb 23, 2026.
This seed entry is informational / for rate lookup UI on post-IEEPA entries.

**Code changes:**
- `prisma/seed-data/ieepa-tariff-rates.json` — updated `S122-01.supersededBy` from `null` to
  `"S301-FL-01"`
- `prisma/seed-data/ieepa-tariff-rates.json` — added new `S301-FL-01` entry (rateType:
  `"section301"`, effectiveDate: `"2026-07-24"`, HTS `9903.05.20`, rate: `0.1`)
