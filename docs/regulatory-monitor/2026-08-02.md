### 2. ⚠️ Section 122 Tariff Expired July 24, 2026 (DATA UPDATED)

**Source:** [Fennemore Law](https://www.fennemorelaw.com/new-u-s-tariffs-replace-expiring-section-122-tariffs/); [Industrial Sage](https://www.industrialsage.com/section-122-tariff-expires-july-2026/); [Swap Academy](https://help.swap-commerce.com/en/articles/16069139-us-tariff-update-24-july-2026-section-301-forced-labor-tariffs-on-imports-from-60-economies)

The Section 122 global 10% surcharge expired at 12:01 AM EDT July 24, 2026 — hitting its 150-day statutory maximum under 19 U.S.C. 2132. The `S122-01` entry in the database had `supersededBy: null`, which was incorrect.

**What replaced it (NOT an IEEPA tariff — not refundable via CAPE):**
Section 301 forced labor tariffs at 10% or 12.5% on 60 trading partners took effect July 24, 2026 simultaneously (USTR action). These are prospective tariffs, not subject to CAPE refunds.

**Fix applied:**
- `prisma/seed-data/ieepa-tariff-rates.json` — updated `S122-01.supersededBy` from `null` to `"S301-FORCED-LABOR-01"` and updated notes to reflect confirmed expiration.

**Note for John:** The `S301-FORCED-LABOR-01` ID is a symbolic reference — the Section 301 forced labor tariffs are a different legal authority and not tracked in the IEEPA rate database. No new entry needed in the rate table since they're not refundable.

### 4. ⚠️ CAPE Phase 3 — CIT Order July 17, 2026 (ELIGIBILITY LOGIC UPDATED)

**Source:** [Thompson Hine SmarTrade](https://www.thompsonhinesmartrade.com/2026/07/cit-orders-cbp-to-process-ieepa-tariff-refunds-for-phase-3-finally-liquidated-entries/); [Diaz Trade Law](https://diaztradelaw.com/ieepa-cape-phase-three-refunds/); [Green Worldwide](https://www.greenworldwide.com/cape-phase-3-for-ieepa-tariff-refunds-on-track-for-end-of-july-as-federal-circuit-appeal-continues/)

CIT Senior Judge Richard Eaton issued an order July 17, 2026 directing CBP to reliquidate, without regard to IEEPA duties, any and all plaintiffs' entries that have been liquidated for more than 80 days. Phase 3 covers ~3,700 cases. **Key constraint: only available to importers who filed a protective action at the CIT.**

Previously, entries liquidated >80 days were classified as `protest` (80-180 days) or `litigation` (>180 days). With Phase 3, CIT filers can bypass both through CAPE.

**Fix applied:**
- `src/lib/tariff-calculator.ts` — added `hasCitFiling?: boolean` to `EntryForEligibility`
- Added `"cape_phase3"` to `FilingMethod` union type
- Updated step 6 in `checkEligibility()`: if `hasCitFiling` is true and entry is finally liquidated (>80 days), returns `filingMethod: "cape_phase3"` with `status: "eligible"` — even past the 180-day protest window
