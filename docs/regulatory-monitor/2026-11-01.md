## 3. USTR — Section 301 Forced Labor Tariffs

**Source:** [EY Tax News — Section 301 Final Rule](https://taxnews.ey.com/news/2026-1607-ustr-finalizes-section-301-forced-labor-tariffs-on-60-economies-additional-tariffs-of-10-percent-or-125-percent-take-effect-24-july-2026)

- **Authority:** Section 301 of the Trade Act of 1974 (19 U.S.C. § 2411)
- **Rate structure:**
  - 10% — economies that have adopted and effectively enforce a forced labor import prohibition (~17–19 countries including Bangladesh, Cambodia, India, Indonesia, Jordan, Malaysia, UK)
  - 12.5% — economies that have NOT adopted such prohibition (~38–41 countries including China, Vietnam, Thailand, Brazil)
- **HTS codes:** 9903.05.20 through 9903.05.84 (one per economy); exemptions under 9903.05.85–9903.06.21
- **Expired codes:** 9903.03.01–9903.03.11 (Section 122) per HTS Revision 13
- **Duration:** Indefinite (no 150-day clock)
- **USMCA:** Canada and Mexico USMCA-qualifying goods exempt

> ⚠️ **NOTE:** Exact per-country HTS subheading assignments not yet populated in seed data. The two entries added (S301-FL-10PCT, S301-FL-12PCT) cover the framework. A dedicated session should populate all 60 country-specific records by cross-referencing USTR final action (FR Doc 2026-15274) and CSMS #69326983.
