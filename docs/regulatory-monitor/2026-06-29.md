## Change 1: CAPE Phase 2 Launched June 29, 2026

**Source:** CBP CSMS #69035485 (published June 23, 2026); confirmed live June 29, 2026  
**Reference:** https://www.cbp.gov/trade/programs-administration/trade-remedies/ieepa-duty-refunds

### What changed

CAPE Phase 2 is now live in the ACE Portal. It adds:

- **Entry types 01, 02, and 06** that are **flagged for reconciliation** AND for which a corresponding Type 09 reconciliation summary has **not yet been filed** are now eligible for CAPE processing.
- Same 80-day liquidation window applies as Phase 1.

### What is still excluded

- **Type 09** (reconciliation summary entries) — still excluded; deferred to a future phase
- **Type 47** (drawback) — still excluded; deferred to a future phase  
- **Type 08** (informal entries), **Type 23** (temporary imports) — remain excluded

### Calculator impact

The `EXCLUDED_ENTRY_TYPES = ["08", "09", "23", "47"]` array is **unchanged** — standard entry types 01/02/06 flagged for reconciliation are already eligible in the calculator (their entry type is 01/02/06, not 09). The Phase 2 expansion does not alter the exclusion set.

**Updated:**
- `src/lib/tariff-calculator.ts` — updated `EXCLUDED_ENTRY_TYPES` comment and `FilingMethod` JSDoc to reference Phase 2; updated excluded_type reason string to name the deferred types explicitly per CSMS #69035485.

## Change 2: CIT Invalidated Section 122 Tariffs (May 7, 2026)

**Source:** Court of International Trade, Slip Op. 26-47  
**Cases:** Oregon v. United States and Burlap and Barrel, Inc. v. United States  
**Reference:** https://www.cit.uscourts.gov/sites/cit/files/26-47.pdf

### What happened

- **May 7, 2026** — Divided CIT three-judge panel ruled Proclamation 11012 (the Section 122 10% global surcharge) unlawful. Held that Section 122 requires identification of "balance-of-payments deficits" measured by specific metrics Congress contemplated in 1974. The proclamation failed to do so.
- Injunction is **party-specific only** — named plaintiffs: Burlap and Barrel Inc., Basic Fun, and State of Washington (University of Washington direct imports). 23 other state plaintiffs dismissed for lack of standing (indirect passthrough costs too speculative).
- **May 12, 2026** — U.S. Court of Appeals for the Federal Circuit entered an **administrative stay**, suspending the CIT judgment while it considers the government's motion for a full stay pending appeal.
- **May 20, 2026** — CIT denied the government's separate stay motion. CAFC administrative stay remains in effect.

### Practical effect on calculator

The Section 122 10% tariff **continues to be collected from all importers except the direct named plaintiffs**. The rate has not changed. The tariff expires July 24, 2026 regardless of the appeal's outcome.

**No rate change to `S122-01`.** Rate remains 10%, `endDate` remains `2026-07-23`.
