### 2. Section 122 — Rate confirmed 10%; CIT struck down but CAFC stayed (NEW CONTEXT)

**Rate:** CBP confirmed on Feb 23, 2026 that the Section 122 surcharge is **10%**, not 15%. While President Trump announced 15% via social media on Feb 22, no formal proclamation issued a rate change. The seed data entry `S122-01` correctly reflects `"rate": 0.1`. The statutory maximum under Section 122 is 15%, and the current operative rate is 10%.

**May 7, 2026 CIT ruling:** A divided 2–1 panel of the Court of International Trade held that President Trump's invocation of Section 122 exceeded statutory authority and entered summary judgment for three plaintiff importers, issuing a permanent injunction against collection of Section 122 duties from those three plaintiffs.
- Source: [Gibson Dunn — Section 122 Global Tariffs Invalidated by CIT](https://www.gibsondunn.com/section-122-global-tariffs-invalidated-by-the-court-of-international-trade-ruling-and-next-steps/)

**May 12, 2026 CAFC stay:** The Court of Appeals for the Federal Circuit entered an administrative stay suspending the CIT's May 7 order while the government's appeal proceeds. Section 122 tariffs continue to be collected from all importers, including the three plaintiff importers, pending the appeal outcome.
- Source: [Ward and Smith — CIT Rejects Section 122](https://www.wardandsmith.com/article/court-of-international-trade-rejects-10-section-122-tariff-what-businesses-should-know-while-the-appeal-proceeds)
- Source: [International Trade Insights — CIT Rules Section 122 Unlawful](https://www.internationaltradeinsights.com/2026/05/the-court-of-international-trade-rules-section-122-tariffs-unlawful-but-limited-relief-to-exclusive-importers/)

**Calculator impact: None at this time.** The TIE calculator handles *IEEPA* refunds only; Section 122 duties are not within CAPE Phase 1 scope and are not refundable via the current program. If the CAFC ultimately affirms the CIT and Section 122 is struck down nationwide, a second refund program analogous to CAPE would be required — but this would be a new triggering event.

**Watch:** If CAFC rules (or SCOTUS takes the case), revisit. Section 122 expiry date is July 24, 2026 — if not extended by Congress, collections cease regardless.
