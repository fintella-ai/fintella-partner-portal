**Source**: [Section 122 expiration explainer](https://statt.com/blog/section-122-tariff-expiration-what-businesses-need-to-know/) | [Trade Law Counsel sunset analysis](https://www.tradelawcounsel.com/insights-news/2026/7/4/section-122-surcharge-sunsets-july-24-what-importers-should-do-beforeand-afterthe-150-day-clock-runs-out) | [Carra Globe Section 301 replacement](https://carraglobe.com/section-122-tariff-2026/)

Section 122's 150-day clock expired at 12:01 AM ET July 24, 2026. Congress did not extend. Simultaneously, new Section 301 tariffs of 10%–12.5% on 60 economies (HTS 9903.05.20–9903.05.84) took effect, imposed by USTR on forced-labor grounds. These Section 301 duties are:

- Open-ended (no statutory expiration)
- Applied to same 60 economies that had IEEPA reciprocal tariffs

The `S122-01` seed entry had `supersededBy: null`. This has been corrected to note the Section 301 replacement.
