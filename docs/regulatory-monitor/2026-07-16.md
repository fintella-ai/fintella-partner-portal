## 1. CAPE warehouse-entry exclusion (CODE CHANGE — DEPLOYED)

**Source**: CBP CSMS #69127837 — "UPDATE – Consolidated Administration and Processing of Entries (CAPE) for IEEPA Refunds – Warehouse Entries"
**Effective**: July 7, 2026

CBP reversed its earlier position on warehouse entries in CAPE:

- **Entry Types 21 and 22** (warehouse entry / re-warehouse entry) are now **excluded** from CAPE as of July 7, 2026. CBP accepted them between April 20 and July 6 in error; any filer who submitted a warehouse entry on a CAPE declaration without a corresponding warehouse withdrawal will not receive an IEEPA refund on that entry and must re-file under the withdrawal entry.
- **Entry Types 31, 32, 34, 38** (warehouse withdrawals) **remain eligible** — IEEPA duties were paid at withdrawal, not at warehousing.

**Code change**: `src/lib/tariff-calculator.ts`
- Added `"21"` and `"22"` to `EXCLUDED_ENTRY_TYPES`
- Updated exclusion-reason string for types 21/22 to explain the withdrawal-vs-warehouse distinction and cite CSMS #69127837

**Why it matters**: Without this fix, the calculator incorrectly told filers with warehouse entries they had an eligible CAPE refund path. They could have filed incorrectly and forfeited their refund.

## 4. CAPE Phase 2 live since June 29 (eligibility logic note)

**Source**: CBP CSMS #69035485 (June 23) + CSMS #69066837 (June 29, deployed)

Phase 2 of CAPE launched June 29, 2026. It covers **entries flagged for reconciliation** (Types 01, 02, 06 where the corresponding Type 09 reconciliation entry has NOT yet been filed) — limited to unliquidated entries or within 80 days of liquidation.

**Current calculator handling**: The existing `checkEligibility()` already excludes Type 09 (the reconciliation entry itself, correctly). Standard Types 01/02/06 were already eligible under Phase 1. The Phase 2 nuance is that previously, entries *flagged for* reconciliation were ineligible for CAPE (because the later reconciliation entry would interact with IEEPA calculations). Phase 2 removes that barrier.

**Outstanding gap**: The `EntryForEligibility` interface has no `isFlaggedForReconciliation` field. The calculator cannot currently distinguish "Type 01, flagged for reconciliation, no recon filed yet (Phase 2 eligible)" from "Type 01, flagged for reconciliation, recon already filed (CAPE rejected)." A future PR should add this field to the interface and update `checkEligibility()`.

**Critical filing note**: If a filer files the Type 09 reconciliation entry BEFORE filing the CAPE declaration, the underlying entries are locked out of CAPE for this phase. The calculator should warn about this.
