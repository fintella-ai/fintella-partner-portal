### 4. CIT docket — Section 122 ruling; no CAPE impact ⚠️ WATCH ITEM
**May 7, 2026:** The Court of International Trade issued a 2-1 decision holding that the Section 122 tariffs (9903.03.xx, 10% global surcharge) exceed the President's statutory authority under 19 U.S.C. §2132. The court found a balance-of-payments deficit ≠ the statutory "large and serious" imbalance required by Section 122. A permanent injunction was issued — but only against collection from the three plaintiff-importers (Burlap & Barrel, Basic Fun, Inc., State of Washington).

**May 12, 2026:** The Court of Appeals for the Federal Circuit (CAFC) entered an administrative stay, suspending the CIT injunction pending appeal. Section 122 tariffs **continue to be collected from all non-plaintiff importers.**

**Impact on IEEPA refund calculator:**
- CAPE handles IEEPA refunds only (Feb 2025 – Feb 2026 period). Section 122 is a different statutory authority and a different refund mechanism would be needed if the Section 122 ruling is upheld.
- `S122-01` in the seed (10% rate, Feb 24 – Jul 23, 2026) correctly reflects the current collection status.
- The `checkEligibility()` function is unchanged — IEEPA entry date window (Feb 1, 2025 – Feb 23, 2026) is unaffected.
- The Section 122 tariffs expire July 24, 2026 unless Congress acts. As of today, no extension legislation pending.

**No calculator change needed. Watch for CAFC ruling.**

### 6. IRS Q3 2026 Interest Rate — ACTIONABLE ✅
**Source:** IRS Internal Revenue Bulletin 2026-22 (published week of May 18, 2026); corresponding CBP Federal Register notice 2026-09871 (May 18, 2026).

| Quarter | Period | Non-Corporate Rate | Corporate Rate | Fed Short-Term |
|---------|--------|--------------------|----------------|----------------|
| Q1 2026 | Jan 1 – Mar 31 | 7% | 6% | 4% | ← already in seed |
| Q2 2026 | Apr 1 – Jun 30 | 6% | 5% | 3% | ← already in seed |
| **Q3 2026** | **Jul 1 – Sep 30** | **7%** | **6%** | **4%** | ← **ADDED this run** |

Q3 2026 reverts to the Q1 2026 rate (7%/6%) because the federal short-term rate for April 2026 was 4% (same as January 2026). Q2 had briefly dipped to 6%/5% when the federal short-term rate was 3% in January.

**Why this matters:** IEEPA refund interest accrues from the duty deposit date to the refund date. Any CAPE refund processed on or after July 1, 2026 that spans the Q3 window requires this rate. Without it, interest calculations for entries being processed between July–September 2026 would truncate at June 30.
