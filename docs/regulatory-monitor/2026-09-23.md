## Findings

### 1. Section 122 Expired July 24, 2026 ✅ (seed data already correct)

The 10% Section 122 global surcharge expired at its 150-day statutory limit on July 24, 2026. Congress did not extend it.

**Additional context added to seed data notes:**
- CIT ruled Section 122 unlawful on May 7, 2026 (2-1 decision: conditions for Section 122 not met)
- CAFC entered administrative stay May 12 — tariff remained collectible through expiration
- Federal Circuit appeal of the CIT ruling is still pending

The seed data's `endDate: "2026-07-23"` and `supersededBy: null` are correct.

### 2. NEW Section 301 Forced Labor Tariffs (July 24, 2026) — NOT in scope for calculator

USTR finalized Section 301 forced-labor tariffs on 60 economies, effective July 24, 2026 (replacing Section 122):
- **10%** on countries with forced-labor import prohibitions (Canada, Ecuador, EU, Indonesia, Mexico, Pakistan, and others)
- **12.5%** on countries without such prohibitions

**Source:** Federal Register Vol. 91, No. 147, July 28, 2026; USTR fact sheet https://ustr.gov/about/policy-offices/press-office/fact-sheets/2026/july/fact-sheet-ustr-section-301-action-response-failure-60-economies-ban-imports-produced-forced-labor

**Impact on TIE:** These tariffs are under Section 301 authority (not IEEPA), are NOT retroactive, and are NOT refundable via CAPE. The calculator correctly handles only IEEPA/Section 122 tariffs for refund purposes. **No calculator rate or eligibility changes required**, but these are now the active tariff regime for importers going forward.

### 3. CAPE Phase 2 Launched June 29, 2026 ⚡ CODE UPDATED

Per CBP CSMS 69035485 (June 23, 2026), CAPE Phase 2 accepts entries **flagged for reconciliation** (entry types 01, 02, 06 where the reconciliation entry type 09 has NOT yet been filed), limited to unliquidated entries or entries within 80 days of liquidation.

**What changed in code:**
- Added `cape_phase2` to `FilingMethod` type in `tariff-calculator.ts`
- Added `hasReconciliationFlag?: boolean` field to `EntryForEligibility` interface
- `checkEligibility()` now routes reconciliation-flagged unliquidated/within-80-day entries to `filingMethod: "cape_phase2"` instead of `"cape_phase1"`
- Added `cape_phase2: 5` risk score to `FILING_RISK` in `tariff-risk-score.ts` (slightly elevated vs. Phase 1 to reflect more complex process)

**Note:** Entry type 09 (reconciliation entries themselves) and type 47 (drawback) remain in `EXCLUDED_ENTRY_TYPES` — Phase 2 covers *original* entries with pending reconciliation, not the reconciliation entries themselves. A future CAPE phase is expected to cover type 09 and type 47.

### 4. CAPE Phase 3 Ordered by CIT July 17, 2026 ⚡ REASON TEXT UPDATED

Senior Judge Eaton issued an order on July 17, 2026 in *Freestyle World, Inc. v. United States* (1:26-cv-01088 — the new lead case after Euro-Notions voluntarily dismissed) directing CBP to reliquidate finally liquidated entries for **importers who filed CIT suits**.

**Status:** Government is appealing to the Federal Circuit. CBP programming was ready in late July 2026 but refunds are only being processed for CIT plaintiffs.

**What changed in code:**
- Updated `excluded_expired` reason text in `checkEligibility()` to note CAPE Phase 3 is available via filing a CIT suit, and that the Federal Circuit appeal is pending
- `filingMethod: "litigation"` is still correct (you must file CIT suit to access Phase 3)

### 5. Euro-Notions → Freestyle World Lead Case Change — DOCUMENTATION ONLY

Euro-Notions Florida, Inc. voluntarily dismissed its case. The new lead IEEPA refund case is **Freestyle World, Inc. v. United States** (1:26-cv-01088). Updated `excluded_expired` reason message accordingly.
