## Finding 1 — ACTIONABLE: CIT Holds Section 122 Tariff Invalid (May 7, 2026)

**Case:** *Oregon v. Trump* & *Burlap and Barrel, Inc. v. Trump*, Slip Op. 26-47, Court Nos. 26-01472 & 26-01606 (Ct. Int'l Trade May 7, 2026)

**What happened:** The U.S. Court of International Trade held that the 10% global Section 122 surcharge (Proclamation 11012, effective Feb 24, 2026) is **unlawful**. The court found the proclamation failed to identify the type of "balance-of-payments deficits" required by 19 U.S.C. § 2132.

**Scope of relief:** The CIT issued a **limited** permanent injunction — only covering named plaintiffs (Burlap & Barrel Inc., Basic Fun!, State of Washington). The court expressly declined to issue a nationwide injunction.

**CAFC stay (May 12, 2026):** The U.S. Court of Appeals for the Federal Circuit entered an **administrative stay** on May 12, 2026, suspending even the plaintiff-limited injunction while the appeal proceeds.

**Current status:**
- CBP is collecting Section 122 duties at **10% from all importers** (including plaintiffs, due to the CAFC stay)
- Rate is **unchanged at 10%**
- July 24, 2026 expiration date is **unchanged**
- No presidential proclamation to extend/replace the tariff found

**Action taken:** Updated `S122-01` notes in `prisma/seed-data/ieepa-tariff-rates.json` to document the ruling, the limited injunction scope, the CAFC stay, and current collection status. Also updated `_metadata` version and sources. **Rate and dates not changed.**
