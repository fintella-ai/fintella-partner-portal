### 3. ⚠️ NEW: Section 338 Canada Tariffs + Import Exclusion (July–September 2026)

**a) Proclamation 11055 (July 23, 2026) — effective July 24, 2026:**
- 50% additional duties on specified Canadian goods: motor vehicles, dairy, alcoholic beverages
- Federal Register 2026-14997
- No USMCA exemption under this Section 338 action

- Modifies scope: removes rock salt and cement (effective Sept 15), adds ATVs and additional dairy

- This is a Federal Register document published TODAY (2026-09-14): [FR 2026-18837](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/09/14/2026-18837/)

### 4. ⚠️ NEW: CAPE Phase 2 Launched (June 29, 2026)

CBP expanded CAPE eligibility for reconciliation-flagged entries.

- **What's new:** Entries of types 01, 02, or 06 that are flagged for reconciliation (i.e., the Type 09 reconciliation entry has **not yet been filed**) are now eligible for CAPE Phase 2.
- **Critical ordering rule:** File CAPE **before** filing the Type 09. Once the Type 09 is filed, the underlying entries are permanently locked out of CAPE.
- **Type 47 drawback:** Still excluded. CBP indicated Phase 3 may cover it (timeline unknown).
- **Type 09 itself:** Still excluded from CAPE.
- **Processing status (as of Aug 21, 2026):** $132.5B accepted, $106.6B processed and sent to Treasury. Phase 3 temporarily delayed for additional CBP validations.
- **Action taken:** Added `capePhase2` section to seed data; added `cape_phase2` to `FilingMethod` type; updated `EXCLUDED_ENTRY_TYPES` comment; added `isReconciliationFlagged` field to `EntryForEligibility`; `checkEligibility()` now routes reconciliation-flagged entries to `cape_phase2` method.
- **Sources:**
  - [CSMS #69035485 — CBP CAPE Phase 2 Guidance (June 23, 2026)](https://content.govdelivery.com/accounts/USDHSCBP/bulletins/41d65dd)
  - [CBP Issues Guidance on June 29, 2026 CAPE Expansion — International Trade Insights](https://www.internationaltradeinsights.com/2026/06/cbp-issues-guidance-on-june-29-2026-cape-expansion/)
