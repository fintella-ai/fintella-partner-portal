### 1. CIT Rules Section 122 Tariffs Unlawful — CAFC Stay Entered (HIGH WATCH)

**What happened:**
- **May 7, 2026**: The U.S. Court of International Trade issued a 2–1 ruling in consolidated cases *State of Oregon v. Trump* and *Burlap & Barrel, Inc. v. Trump* (Chief Judge Barnett + Judge Kelly, majority; one dissent) that the 10% global Section 122 surcharge is unlawful. The Court held the administration failed to demonstrate a qualifying balance-of-payments deficit as the statute requires.
- The injunction is **limited to the three prevailing plaintiffs only** — Burlap & Barrel, Inc., Basic Fun, Inc., and the State of Washington. It is **not a nationwide injunction**.
- **May 8, 2026**: DOJ filed a notice of appeal to the U.S. Court of Appeals for the Federal Circuit (CAFC).
- **May 11, 2026**: DOJ filed an emergency motion for a stay pending appeal.
- **May 12, 2026**: CAFC granted an administrative stay, suspending the CIT's injunction while it considers the stay motion. **Section 122 tariffs (9903.03.01 at 10%) remain in full effect for all importers who are not parties to the litigation.**

**Current status of the calculator:**  
`S122-01` in `ieepa-tariff-rates.json` correctly records the 10% rate as active through July 24, 2026. No change needed today. The CAFC stay means CBP continues collection for non-plaintiff importers. Rate remains 10% (the Trump Truth Social announcement of 15% on Feb 22 was never formalized in a proclamation — `S122-01` notes field already reflects this).

**Trigger for code action:**
- If CAFC **lifts the stay** → Section 122 may stop being collected → update `S122-01.endDate` to the effective termination date and add a new rate entry with `rate: 0.0` and a `legalBasis` note
- If CAFC **upholds** the stay and sends case back → no change, monitor expiry at July 24

### 2. Euro-Notions IEEPA Refund — Government Appeal Deadline Approaching (MEDIUM WATCH)

**What happened:**
- *Euro-Notions Florida, Inc. v. United States* (Case No. 1:25-cv-00595-RKE, Judge Eaton) remains the lead case overseeing IEEPA tariff refunds following the designation after Atmus Filtration was voluntarily dismissed April 6.
- Judge Eaton's April 7, 2026 order directed CBP to refund all IEEPA duties regardless of liquidation status (including historically liquidated entries). This goes beyond CAPE Phase 1 scope.
- **Government's appeal deadline for the Euro-Notions IEEPA refund order runs through early June 2026.**
- If the government appeals and obtains a stay, CAPE processing for historically liquidated entries could be blocked.
