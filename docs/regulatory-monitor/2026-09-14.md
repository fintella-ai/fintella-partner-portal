# TIE Regulatory Monitor — 2026-09-14

**Run date**: September 14, 2026  
**Monitor scope**: IEEPA tariff law, CAPE system updates, CIT rulings affecting the tariff refund calculator

---

## Summary

Two actionable changes were detected and applied in this PR:

1. **CAPE Phase 2 eligibility** — Type 09 and Type 47 entries are now eligible for IEEPA refunds via CAPE Phase 2 (launched June 29, 2026). The calculator was incorrectly marking them as `excluded_type/none`.
2. **Section 122 → Section 301 succession** — Section 122 expired July 23, 2026. Section 301 Forced Labor tariffs (10% / 12.5%) replaced it effective July 24, 2026. The seed data has been updated to reflect the succession chain and document the new regime.

## 2. CBP Bulletins / CSMS Updates

**Source checked**: [cbp.gov/trade/programs-administration/trade-remedies/ieepa-duty-refunds](https://www.cbp.gov/trade/programs-administration/trade-remedies/ieepa-duty-refunds)

### CAPE Phase 2 — Launched June 29, 2026 ✅ ACTIONABLE

- **CSMS reference**: CSMS #69326983 (Section 301 Forced Labor Guidance, Jul 23, 2026)
- **CBP Trade Information Notice**: [26_0723_trade_information_notice_CAPE.pdf](https://www.cbp.gov/sites/default/files/2026-07/26_0723_trade_information_notice_CAPE.pdf)
- **What changed**: CBP CAPE Phase 2 expanded eligibility to include:
  - **Type 09** (Reconciliation entries) — now eligible regardless of liquidation status
  - **Type 47** (Drawback entries) — now eligible regardless of liquidation status
- **Prior calculator behavior**: Both types were in `EXCLUDED_ENTRY_TYPES` and returned `status: "excluded_type"`, `filingMethod: "none"`
- **New correct behavior**: Route to `filingMethod: "cape_phase2"` with `status: "eligible"`
- **Progress**: As of August 21, 2026, ~$132.5B in IEEPA refunds accepted, $106.6B completed and sent to Treasury
- **Code fix applied**: `src/lib/tariff-calculator.ts` — split `EXCLUDED_ENTRY_TYPES` into Phase-1/2 excluded (`["08", "23"]`) and new `CAPE_PHASE2_ENTRY_TYPES` (`["09", "47"]`); added `cape_phase2` to `FilingMethod` type; updated `checkEligibility()` step 4

## 3. USTR Announcements

**Source checked**: [ustr.gov/presidential-tariff-actions](https://ustr.gov/trade-topics/presidential-tariff-actions), [ustr.gov press releases (2026)](https://ustr.gov/about/policy-offices/press-office/press-releases)

### Section 301 Forced Labor Tariffs — Effective July 24, 2026 ✅ ACTIONABLE (data)

- **USTR Press Release**: [USTR Takes Action in Forced Labor Section 301 Investigations](https://ustr.gov/about/policy-offices/press-office/press-releases/2026/july/ustr-takes-action-forced-labor-section-301-investigations)
- **White House Action**: [actions-by-the-united-states-in-the-investigations...](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2026/07/actions-by-the-united-states-in-the-investigations-under-section-301-of-the-trade-act-of-1974-of-the-acts-policies-and-practices-of-60-economies-related-to-the-failure-of-each-economy-to-impose-and/)
- **Background**: Section 122's 150-day statutory maximum expired July 23, 2026. USTR had simultaneously pursued Section 301 investigations (initiated March 12, 2026; findings June 2, 2026) against 60 economies for failure to prohibit forced-labor goods imports.
- **New tariff structure**:
  | Category | Rate | HTS Range |
  |---|---|---|
  | Economies WITH forced-labor import bans | 10% | 9903.05.20–9903.05.84 |
  | Economies WITHOUT forced-labor import bans | 12.5% | 9903.05.20–9903.05.84 |
- **Exemptions**: In-transit goods, civil aircraft, pharmaceuticals, aluminum/steel/copper (Sec 232), vehicles, wood products, semiconductors, humanitarian donations, informational materials
- **Coverage**: ~60 economies representing ~99% of US imports
- **IEEPA refund impact**: ❌ None directly — these are Section 301 tariffs, not IEEPA; entries after Feb 23, 2026 are outside the refund window. Rate data added to seed file for reference/display purposes only.
- **Seed change applied**: `prisma/seed-data/ieepa-tariff-rates.json` — added `S301-FL-10` (10%) and `S301-FL-12-5` (12.5%) entries; updated `S122-01.supersededBy` from `null` to `"S301-FL-10"`
