## MATERIAL FINDING: CIT Invalidates Section 122 Tariffs (May 7, 2026)

### What happened

On **May 7, 2026**, a divided panel of the Court of International Trade (CIT) held that the Section 122 tariffs imposed by President Trump are **unlawful and unauthorized** under the Trade Act of 1974. The court concluded that Section 122's emergency authority is narrowly limited to addressing "large and serious" balance-of-payments crises, not general trade deficits.

- **Case:** State of Washington et al. v. Trump (CIT, May 7, 2026)
- **Relief granted:** Named plaintiffs only (State of Washington + two importers) — **no nationwide injunction**
- **Tariffs for all others:** Remain in effect pending appeal
- **DOJ response:** Filed notice of appeal on May 8, 2026
- **Source:** https://www.hklaw.com/en/insights/publications/2026/05/us-court-of-international-trade-invalidates-the-administrations
