## Detailed Findings

### 1. Section 122 Tariffs — EXPIRED July 24, 2026 ⚠️

**Source:** [Honigman Law — Section 122 Expires, Section 301 Replaces](https://www.honigman.com/alert-3462), [Section 122 Tariffs Expire — tariffstool.com](https://www.tariffstool.com/guides/section-122-expires-july-24-2026-importer-playbook)

Section 122 of the Trade Act of 1974 imposes a 150-day statutory maximum on any tariff imposed under it. The 10% global surcharge (HTS 9903.03.01) imposed February 24, 2026, therefore **expired automatically at 12:01 AM EDT on July 24, 2026**. No presidential action was required to end it.

The Administration replaced it the same minute with two-tier Section 301 Forced Labor tariffs:

| HTS | Tier | Rate | Coverage |
|---|---|---|---|
| 9903.05.86 | Forced-labor-compliant economies | **10%** | ~60 economies that have committed to enforcing forced-labor import prohibitions |
| 9903.05.87 | Non-compliant economies | **12.5%** | Economies that have NOT made such commitments |

Total coverage: ~99.4% of U.S. imports. Exemptions: USMCA goods, energy, potash, Section 232 goods, critical minerals.

**Code impact:**
- `prisma/seed-data/ieepa-tariff-rates.json`: `S122-01.supersededBy` updated from `null` to `"S301-FL-COMPLIANT-01"`; two new entries added (`S301-FL-COMPLIANT-01`, `S301-FL-NONCOMPLIANT-01`)
- These Section 301 entries are **NOT within the IEEPA refund eligibility period** and do not change calculator outputs for refund claims. They are added to maintain an accurate rate timeline for context.

### 2. CAPE Phase 2 — Operational Since June 29, 2026

**Source:** [CAPE Phase II Is Live — Diaz Trade Law](https://diaztradelaw.com/cape-ieepa-duty-refunds/), [CSMS #69035485](https://content.govdelivery.com/accounts/USDHSCBP/bulletins/41d65dd)

CAPE Phase 2 launched June 29, 2026. It added coverage for:
- Entries **flagged for reconciliation** (entry types 01, 02, 06) where no Type-09 reconciliation entry has been filed
- Scope: unliquidated entries or entries liquidated within 80 days

The `EXCLUDED_ENTRY_TYPES` set in `tariff-calculator.ts` already correctly handles this (types 01/02/06 were never excluded). Comments updated to reflect Phase 2 status.

**Note on 4.36 million failed entries:** CBP disclosed approximately 4.36M entries failed CAPE Phase 2 validation, primarily due to finally-liquidated status (>80 days) — those require Phase 3.

### 3. CAPE Phase 3 — Operational as of Late July 2026

**Source:** [CIT Orders Reliquidation Ahead of CAPE Phase 3 — Willson International](https://www.willsonintl.com/news/cit-orders-reliquidation-ahead-of-cape-phase-3/), [CIT Issues Order — internationaltradeinsights.com](https://www.internationaltradeinsights.com/2026/07/cit-issues-order-with-specific-updates-related-to-cape-phase-3-finally-liquidated-entry-eligibility-for-ieepa-refunds/)

- CIT ordered reliquidation of "finally liquidated" entries on July 15-17, 2026, covering ~3,700 IEEPA cases before the court
- CAPE Phase 3 went live late July 2026, processing finally-liquidated entries **for importers who have filed CIT suits and obtained reliquidation orders**
- Importers without filed suits remain locked out of Phase 3

**CIT financial stats as of August 2026:** ~$121.75B in refunds accepted into CAPE, $86.3B sent to Treasury for disbursement.

The `checkEligibility()` function already correctly routes entries liquidated >180 days ago to `filingMethod: "litigation"`. Comments updated to clarify Phase 3 provides an automated channel for CIT litigants.
