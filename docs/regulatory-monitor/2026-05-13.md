## Findings

### 1. Section 122 Tariffs Ruled Unlawful — CIT May 7, 2026 ⚠️ ACTIONABLE

**Case:** *Oregon v. Trump / Burlap & Barrel, Inc. v. Trump*, slip op. 26-47, Ct. Nos. 26-01472 & 26-01606  
**Court:** U.S. Court of International Trade  
**Date:** May 7, 2026  
**Vote:** 2-1 (divided panel)

**Holding:** The 10% global tariff imposed under Section 122 of the Trade Act of 1974 (19 U.S.C. § 2132) is unlawful. The statutory conditions required to invoke Section 122 were not satisfied, and the proclamation reflected an overly expansive interpretation of the delegation from Congress.

**Relief granted:** Permanent injunction prohibiting collection of Section 122 duties — but **only as to the prevailing plaintiffs**:
- Burlap & Barrel, Inc.
- Basic Fun, Inc.
- State of Washington

The court declined a nationwide injunction. Most state co-plaintiffs were found to lack Article III standing (not importers of record; indirect economic harm only).

**Government response:** DOJ noticed its appeal to the U.S. Court of Appeals for the Federal Circuit on May 8, 2026. A motion for stay pending appeal is anticipated (following the same playbook used in *V.O.S. Selections* after the IEEPA ruling, where the Federal Circuit granted a stay).

**Current status as of May 13, 2026:**
- Section 122 tariffs (9903.03.01) **continue to be collected** from all non-plaintiff importers
- Rate remains 10%; statutory expiration remains July 24, 2026
- No new executive order issued in response

**Impact on TIE calculator:**
- **No rate change required.** The 10% rate in `S122-01` is unchanged.
- **No eligibility logic change required.** CAPE Phase 1 covers IEEPA-period entries (Feb 1, 2025 – Feb 23, 2026); Section 122 entries (Feb 24, 2026 onwards) are a separate track.
- **Notes updated** in `ieepa-tariff-rates.json` entry `S122-01` to reflect litigation status.
- **Watch item:** If CAFC denies stay and the injunction is expanded nationwide on appeal, Section 122 refunds could become a second wave of claims (separate from IEEPA CAPE). No code changes needed yet; monitor weekly.
