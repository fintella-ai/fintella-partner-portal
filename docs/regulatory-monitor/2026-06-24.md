## Finding 1: CAPE Phase 2 Launches June 29, 2026 — ACTIONABLE

**What changed**: CBP announced that CAPE Phase 2 will deploy on **June 29, 2026**, adding support for **Type 09 (reconciliation) entries** where the reconciliation has not yet been filed.

**Phase 2 eligibility conditions** (both must be met):
1. Entry type is 09 (reconciliation-flagged)
2. The Type 09 reconciliation entry has **not yet been filed**
3. Underlying entry is unliquidated OR liquidated within 80 days of the Phase 2 filing date

**Scope**: Approximately 2.8 million reconciliation-flagged entries, representing ~$28.7 billion in potential refunds. Combined with Phase 1, this brings total coverage to ~$130 billion of $166 billion in IEEPA duties paid.

**Type 47 (drawback) remains excluded** — CAPE cannot process drawback entries due to double-refund risk.

**Source**: 
- https://www.ghy.com/trade-compliance/cbp-cape-reconciliation-ieepa-tariff-refunds-june-29/
- https://www.thompsonhinesmartrade.com/2026/06/cbp-announces-phases-2-and-3-of-the-ieepa-tariff-refund-process/ (blocked by paywall)

**Code changes made**:
- Removed "09" from `EXCLUDED_ENTRY_TYPES` in `src/lib/tariff-calculator.ts`
- Added `"cape_phase2"` to the `FilingMethod` type
- Added `reconciliationFiled?: boolean` field to `EntryForEligibility` interface
- Added Type 09 specific logic in `checkEligibility()`:
  - `reconciliationFiled === true` → `excluded_recon_filed` (separate pathway exists)
  - `reconciliationFiled === false` → eligible via `cape_phase2` (80-day window applied)
  - `reconciliationFiled === undefined` → falls through with `needsReview: true` flag

## Finding 2: CAPE Phase 3 Expected End of July 2026 — MONITOR, NO CODE CHANGE YET

**What changed**: CBP has announced Phase 3, targeting **end of July 2026**, which would cover **historically liquidated entries** (liquidated >80 days before the CAPE processing date).

**Significant caveat**: Phase 3 refunds will only be processed for importers who have **filed CIT lawsuits**. DOJ continues to argue CBP lacks authority to reliquidate finally liquidated entries without an importer-specific court judgment. Federal Circuit appeal is ongoing.

**Type 47 (drawback)** is not confirmed for Phase 3.

**Source**: https://www.greenworldwide.com/cape-phase-3-for-ieepa-tariff-refunds-on-track-for-end-of-july-as-federal-circuit-appeal-continues/

**Code changes made**: None — Phase 3 has not launched. The existing `filingMethod: "litigation"` path already covers this scenario. Will update when Phase 3 launches or when CBP CSMS guidance issues.

## Finding 3: Section 122 Tariff — CIT Ruled Unlawful, Federal Circuit Stay In Effect

**What changed (May 7, 2026 — prior to this run)**:
- The Court of International Trade ruled Section 122 tariffs **unlawful** (May 7, 2026), on grounds that the statutory "balance of payments deficit" condition was not satisfied.
- The U.S. Court of Appeals for the Federal Circuit granted a **temporary stay** of the CIT judgment pending further consideration on appeal.
- Section 122 tariffs are therefore **still in effect** as of June 24, 2026.

**July 24, 2026 expiration**: Section 122 expires in 30 days. Only Congress can extend; the President cannot. No Congressional action has been announced.

**Sources**:
- https://www.swlaw.com/publication/tariffs-redux-what-importers-should-know-about-ieepa-refunds-and-section-122/
- https://taxpolicycenter.org/taxvox/how-supreme-courts-ieepa-ruling-and-new-section-122-tariffs-reshape-costs-across-industries/

**Data changes made**: Updated `S122-01.notes` in `prisma/seed-data/ieepa-tariff-rates.json` to reflect the CIT ruling, Federal Circuit stay, and the statutory expiration constraint. No rate or date change — tariff remains 10% through July 23.

**Action required**: Monitor for Federal Circuit ruling or Congressional action before July 24. If tariff expires without extension, no data change needed (endDate already set to 2026-07-23). If extended, add a new `S122-02` entry.
