### 1. CIT Class Certification — *Freestyle World v. United States* (OCTOBER 8, 2026) — NOTEWORTHY

**Source:** https://www.steptoe.com/en/news-publications/global-trade-and-investment-law-blog/relief-in-sight-cit-certifies-class-for-ieepa-tariff-refunds-for-certain-importers.html  
https://www.dwt.com/insights/2026/10/cit-certifies-ieepa-duty-refund-importer-class  
https://www.thompsonhinesmartrade.com/2026/10/cit-certifies-a-tariff-refund-class/

Judge Richard K. Eaton of the Court of International Trade certified a class action on October 8, 2026, in *Freestyle World v. United States*. The class covers **all importers of record who paid IEEPA duties between February 1, 2025 and February 19, 2026**, excluding any entity that separately filed its own action.

Key details:
- **Class certification only** — not a refund order. Class members receive refunds only if the court enters a class-wide injunction and CBP processes their entries.
- **Scope gaps**: Does not include IEEPA tariffs on Brazilian or Indian goods.
- **Government appeal risk**: Decision is not final; subject to Federal Circuit review.
- **Status conference**: October 13, 2026. Scheduling order due October 22, 2026.
- **CAPE context**: As of early October, CBP's CAPE system had processed approximately $126 billion in duties plus interest.
- Source: https://www.vinetur.com/en/amp/20261017109177/trade-group-warns-importers-that-ieepa-tariff-ruling-does-not-guarantee-refunds.html

**Impact on calculator**: No rate change. The `litigation` FilingMethod remains the correct path for entries with expired protest windows. Importers in the `litigation` bucket should be aware the class action may provide a route without individual CIT filings — this is advisory, not a code change.

### 2. CAPE Phase 2 — Launched June 29, 2026 — CODE UPDATED

**Source:** https://www.tariffstool.com/guides/cape-phase-2-launch-date-whats-coming-may-2026  
https://gingercontrol.com/blog/cape-phase-2-scope-timeline-eligibility  
https://www.cbp.gov/sites/default/files/2026-07/26_0723_trade_information_notice_CAPE.pdf

CBP deployed CAPE Phase 2 in ACE on **June 29, 2026**. Phase 2 expands CAPE refund eligibility to:

- **Type 01, 02, and 06 entries** that carry a CBP reconciliation flag AND have **not yet had a Type 09 reconciliation entry filed**.
- Sequencing note: if the Type 09 deadline is less than 30 days away, file the reconciliation first. If more than 30 days out, file the CAPE declaration first.
- Type 09 entries themselves remain excluded from CAPE (the reconciliation entry is a separate filing).
- **Type 47 (drawback)**: Still excluded. CBP requires CAPE to be filed before any drawback claim on IEEPA-affected entries to avoid double-refund.

**Action taken**: Updated `src/lib/tariff-calculator.ts`:
- Added `cape_phase2` to the `FilingMethod` type union.
- Added `isReconciliationFlagged?: boolean` field to `EntryForEligibility`.
- Added Phase 2 eligibility path in `checkEligibility()` for reconciliation-flagged Type 01/02/06 entries with no Type 09 filed.
- Added `CAPE_PHASE2_ENTRY_TYPES` set and updated comments.

### 3. Section 122 Expired July 24, 2026 — SEED DATA UPDATED

**Source:** https://taxnews.ey.com/news/2026-1603-ustr-takes-final-action-imposing-tariffs-on-60-trading-partners-as-section-122-tariffs-expire  
https://zonos.com/us-tariff-updates

The Section 122 10% global surcharge expired by its own statutory 150-day cap on July 23/24, 2026. No congressional extension was enacted.

**Replacement**: USTR imposed Section 301 "forced labor" additional duties effective July 24, 2026:
- 10% rate on 17 economies (Canada, Mexico, India, UK, and others)
- 12.5% rate on all other investigated economies (China, Vietnam, Thailand, etc.)
- Covers top 60 U.S. trade partners (99.4% of imports)
- Authority: Section 301 of the Trade Act of 1974, not IEEPA or Section 122

**Impact on IEEPA refund calculator**: Section 122 refunds are separate from IEEPA refunds and outside TIE's current scope. The Section 301 tariffs are new duties, not subject to IEEPA refunds. The seed data entry `S122-01` already has the correct `endDate: "2026-07-23"`. Updated `supersededBy` to reference the Section 301 action.
