## Findings

### 1. Federal Register — No new IEEPA activity
No executive orders or presidential proclamations mentioning IEEPA tariffs were found for November 2026. The most recent relevant Federal Register item was a CBP information-collection notice dated October 5, 2026 covering court-ordered IEEPA refund worksheets ([2026-20228](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/10/05/2026-20228/agency-information-collection-activities-court-ordered-refunds-under-the-international-emergency)).

### 2. CBP / CAPE — Phase 1 stable, Phase 2 still unannounced
CAPE Phase 1 (launched April 20, 2026) continues to process IEEPA refunds. The most recent CBP CAPE Trade Information Notice is dated July 23, 2026 ([link](https://www.cbp.gov/sites/default/files/2026-07/26_0723_trade_information_notice_CAPE.pdf)).

Phase 2 scope (Type 09 reconciliation summaries and Type 47 drawback entries) remains excluded from CAPE Declarations but is listed as a future deployment with no date announced. As of October 2026, entry types 08, 09, 23, and 47 still return errors on CAPE Declarations.

No new CSMS messages dated November 2026 were found. Prior relevant CSMS: #69035485 and #69127837.

**No change needed** to `checkEligibility()` EXCLUDED_ENTRY_TYPES array.

### 3. USTR — No IEEPA/Section 122 actions
USTR's November 2, 2026 press releases covered AGOA (Rwanda/South Africa termination) and a U.S.-Mexico MOU — neither relates to IEEPA tariffs or the tariff refund calculator.

### 4. CIT — No new rulings
No new Court of International Trade rulings in the Learning Resources v. Trump / Euro-Notions Florida v. United States line were found for November 2026. The most recent CIT action identified was Slip Op. 26-123 (class-wide applicability of the Learning Resources holding). The CAPE refund process continues as the operational implementation of the March 4, 2026 CIT refund order.

### 5. Section 122 (9903.03.xx) — Expired, no extension
The 10% Section 122 replacement tariff expired on its statutory 150-day limit on **July 24, 2026**. No extension or replacement has been announced. A CIT ruling on May 7, 2026 found the proclamation unlawful (for 3 plaintiffs); the Federal Circuit stayed that relief on June 11, 2026. The 9903.03.xx codes have no active rate as of this writing.

**No change needed** to `ieepa-tariff-rates.json` or the calculator.

### 6. IRS Overpayment Interest Rate — No new quarterly update found
No Q4 2026 IRS overpayment interest rate change was surfaced by searches. The next IRS quarterly rate announcement would typically be in late November for Q1 2027.
