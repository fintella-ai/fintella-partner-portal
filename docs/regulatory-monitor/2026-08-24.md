## 1. Section 122 Tariffs Expired — Replaced by Section 301 Forced Labor Tariffs

**Status**: RATE DATA UPDATED

**What happened**: The Section 122 global 10% surcharge (9903.03.01), which replaced IEEPA tariffs on Feb 24, 2026, expired on July 24, 2026 under its 150-day statutory maximum. The Trump administration simultaneously imposed new Section 301 tariffs on 60 economies effective 12:01 AM July 24, 2026.

**New tariff structure**:
- Authority: Section 301 of the Trade Act of 1974 (USTR investigation into forced labor enforcement failures)
- Rates: 10% or 12.5% per economy (based on forced labor enforcement compliance)
- HTS codes: 9903.05.20–9903.05.84 (per-economy codes)
- Scope: 60 trading partners representing >99% of U.S. imports
- Pharma exemption: 9903.04.60–9903.04.66 effective July 31, 2026 (one week after main action)

**Note on CIT challenge to Section 122**: CIT also struck down Section 122 as unlawful on May 7, 2026, but the government appealed and the Federal Circuit entered an administrative stay, so Section 122 continued collecting through its July 24 statutory expiry. The Section 122 end date (2026-07-23) was already correctly recorded in the seed file.

## 2. CAPE Phase 2 Launched June 29, 2026

**Status**: ELIGIBILITY LOGIC UPDATED

**What happened**: CBP deployed CAPE Phase 2 on June 29, 2026 (CSMS # 69066837). Phase 2 covers entries flagged for reconciliation (entry types 01, 02, and 06) for which the corresponding Type 09 reconciliation entry has NOT yet been filed. This directly addresses the eligibility gap for importers who use post-entry reconciliation.

**Key Phase 2 rules**:
- The CAPE declaration MUST be submitted BEFORE the Type 09 reconciliation entry is filed
- If the Type 09 recon is filed first, the entry is locked out of CAPE and must use the protest track
- Unliquidated entries or entries liquidated within the 80-day window apply
- Entry types 01, 02, 06 with the reconciliation flag qualify

**Changes made**:
- `src/lib/tariff-calculator.ts`: Added `"cape_phase2"` to `FilingMethod` type
- Added `isReconFlagged?: boolean` and `reconFiled?: boolean` to `EntryForEligibility` interface
- Updated `checkEligibility()` to route recon-flagged entries to `cape_phase2` when Type 09 not filed; to `excluded_recon_filed` status (with protest path) when already filed
- Added `"excluded_recon_filed"` to `EligibilityResult.status` union comment
- `src/lib/tariff-risk-score.ts`: Added `cape_phase2: 5` to `FILING_RISK` record

- 75,000+ CAPE declarations submitted as of July 31, 2026
- ~$128.68 billion in refunds accepted for processing

The Trump administration had earlier appealed a June 2026 CIT order to refund duties on finally liquidated entries. The government's appeal to the Federal Circuit is pending.

**Current calculator impact**: The `"litigation"` filing method correctly captures "finally liquidated" entries (protest window expired). If Phase 3 launches and opens a new CAPE channel for finally-liquidated entries, the calculator will need:
- New `FilingMethod`: `"cape_phase3"`

**Action needed**: Monitor for Phase 3 launch announcement from CBP (likely a new CSMS message). Watch for Federal Circuit ruling on the government's appeal of the CIT reliquidation order.
