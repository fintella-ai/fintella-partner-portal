### 4. CAPE Phase 3 — launched October 6, 2026 ⚠️ CODE UPDATED

CBP launched CAPE Phase 3 on October 6, 2026, covering **finally liquidated entries** for importers who:
1. Filed a CIT case challenging IEEPA tariffs, AND
2. Received a court-ordered reliquidation (CIT Senior Judge Eaton order, July 2026), AND
3. Submitted their importer of record number to CBP by July 30, 2026.

This is NOT a universal expansion — only CIT plaintiffs qualify. The estimated scope is ~$11.4 billion
in finally liquidated IEEPA duties. CBP Executive Director Brandon Lord confirmed the October 6 deployment
in a court declaration in *Freestyle World v. United States*.

**Impact on calculator:** Previously, entries with expired protest windows (liquidated > 180 days ago)
were always classified as `filingMethod: "litigation"`. CIT plaintiffs in this group now have a
`"cape_phase3"` path.

**Changes made to `src/lib/tariff-calculator.ts`:**
- Added `"cape_phase3"` to the `FilingMethod` union type
- Added `isCitPlaintiff?: boolean` field to `EntryForEligibility`
- Updated `checkEligibility()`: when `daysRemaining < 0` and `entry.isCitPlaintiff === true`,
  returns `filingMethod: "cape_phase3"` instead of `"litigation"`
- Updated `EXCLUDED_ENTRY_TYPES` comment to document Phase 2 behavior
