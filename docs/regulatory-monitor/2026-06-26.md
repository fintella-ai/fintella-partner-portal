### 1. CAPE Phase 2 — LAUNCHES JUNE 29, 2026 (3 days) 🔴 ACTION TAKEN

**Source**: CBP CSMS #69035485; thompsonhinesmartrade.com/2026/06/cbp-confirms-june-29-2026-ieepa-tariff-refund-process-phase-2-launch/

**What changed**: CBP confirmed CAPE Phase 2 deploys June 29, 2026. Phase 2 covers entries **flagged for reconciliation** (Types 01, 02, 06) where a Type 09 reconciliation summary has **not yet been filed**. Same 80-day liquidation window as Phase 1. ~2.8 million entries, ~$28.7 billion in IEEPA duties.

**Key distinction from Phase 1**: Entries where Type 09 has already been filed remain excluded. Type 47 (drawback) remains excluded from Phase 2 as well.

**CBP guidance**: Hold Type 09 filings unless the reconciliation deadline is within 30 days — file CAPE Phase 2 declaration first.

**Code changes made**:
- `src/lib/tariff-calculator.ts`: Added `cape_phase2` and `cape_phase3` to `FilingMethod` union type

- `prisma/seed-data/ieepa-tariff-rates.json`: Updated Type 09 exclusion notes, eligibilityCriteria, added `capePhase2` and `capePhase3` documentation blocks

### 3. CIT Invalidated Section 122 Tariffs (May 7, 2026) — STAYED 🟡 INFORMATIONAL UPDATE

**Source**: skadden.com/insights/publications/2026/05/us-trade-court-strikes-down-section-122-tariffs; hklaw.com/en/insights/publications/2026/05; cooley.com/news/insight/2026/2026-05-21-trade-court-rejects-section-122-tariffs-appeal-pending; troutman.com "Federal Circuit Hits Pause"

**What happened**: On May 7, 2026, the CIT struck down the Section 122 10% global surcharge in a 2-1 decision (*Oregon v. United States* and *Burlap and Barrel, Inc. v. United States*). The court held the proclamation did not meet the statutory criteria required to invoke Section 122.

**Critical**: The CIT injunction applies only to the 3 importer plaintiffs — not universally. On May 12, 2026, the U.S. Court of Appeals for the Federal Circuit issued an **administrative stay** of the CIT ruling pending the government's appeal. Section 122 tariffs continue to be **collected from all other importers**.

**Calculator impact**: The Section 122 10% surcharge remains in effect for virtually all importers through July 24, 2026. No rate data changes needed. Updated notes on `S122-01` to reflect the CIT ruling and CAFC stay.

**Code changes made**:
- `prisma/seed-data/ieepa-tariff-rates.json`: Updated S122-01 `notes` field to document the CIT ruling, CAFC stay, and July 24 expiry

### 4. Government Appeals Euro-Notions CIT Refund Order (June 3, 2026) 🟡 INFORMATIONAL

**Source**: hklaw.com/en/insights/publications/2026/06/ieepa-tariff-refund-update-government-appeals; dwt.com/insights/2026/06/appeal-of-trade-court-ieepa-duty-refund-order; morganlewis.com/pubs/2026/06/tariff-refund-battle-continues-government-appeals-order

**What happened**: On June 3, 2026, DOJ filed notices of appeal in the Federal Circuit challenging the CIT's April 7, 2026 order in *Euro-Notions Florida v. United States* (Case No. 25-595), which ordered refunds on finally-liquidated entries. The government argues the CIT cannot order refunds to non-plaintiffs.

**CAPE status**: Despite the appeal, CAPE Phase 1 is processing refunds. ~$90B in claims accepted; ~$23B approved and transmitted to Treasury for refund. Phase 1 not affected by the appeal.

**Calculator impact**: No code changes needed. The `filingMethod: "litigation"` path for finally-liquidated entries (>180 days) remains correct for non-CIT plaintiffs. Phase 3 (late July) will be limited to CIT plaintiffs.
