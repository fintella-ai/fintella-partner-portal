## 1. Brazil Section 301 Tariff (25%) — EFFECTIVE JULY 22, 2026

**Source:** USTR Notice of Action, Federal Register (~Jul 15, 2026)
- https://ustr.gov/about/policy-offices/press-office/press-releases/2026/july/ustr-section-301-action-brazils-unreasonable-acts-policies-and-practices

**What changed:** USTR took final action under Section 301 of the Trade Act of 1974, imposing a 25% ad valorem tariff on most imports from Brazil, citing Brazil's unreasonable trade practices. Effective 12:01 AM ET July 22, 2026.

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| HTS Code | 9903.05.01 |
| Rate | 25% ad valorem |
| Country | Brazil (BR) |
| Effective Date | 2026-07-22 |
| Legal Authority | Section 301, Trade Act of 1974 |
| In-transit HTS | 9903.05.02 (0%, for shipments loaded pre-Jul 22 entered pre-Jul 29) |

**Key exemptions:** antiques/collectibles/art, certain pharmaceuticals, certain seafood, certain wood products, iron/steel scrap and waste, organic honey, pig iron, unflavored instant coffee, used clothing, aluminum hydroxide, ash containing precious metals, animal hides/furskins/leather.

**Impact on calculator:** This is a FORWARD Section 301 tariff, not an IEEPA refund-eligible period. Added as informational rate entry `BR-S301-01` in seed data. No change to `checkEligibility()` or IEEPA date range (Feb 1, 2025 – Feb 23, 2026).

**Code change:** `prisma/seed-data/ieepa-tariff-rates.json` — added `BR-S301-01` entry.

## 2. Section 122 Tariff Expiry — JULY 24, 2026 (IN 4 DAYS)

**Sources:**
- CIT ruling: https://www.skadden.com/insights/publications/2026/05/us-trade-court-strikes-down-section-122-tariffs
- Federal Circuit stay: https://www.troutman.com/insights/federal-circuit-hits-pause-on-cits-section-122-tariff-ruling/
- Expiry timeline: https://www.tradelawcounsel.com/insights-news/2026/7/4/section-122-surcharge-sunsets-july-24-what-importers-should-do-beforeand-afterthe-150-day-clock-runs-out

**What changed:**
- **May 7, 2026:** CIT (Oregon v. United States / Burlap and Barrel v. United States) struck down the 10% Section 122 tariff 3-0.
- **June 11, 2026:** Federal Circuit stayed the CIT injunction pending appeal (found government likely to succeed on merits). CBP continues collecting.
- **July 24, 2026 (4 days):** Section 122 expires by statute (max 150 days from Feb 24, 2026). No extension possible without Congress. The tariff ends regardless of the pending appeal.

**Planned replacement — NOT YET FINALIZED:**
USTR proposed 10%/12.5% Section 301 forced labor tariffs on 60 countries (June 2, 2026). Public comments closed July 6; hearings July 7; post-hearing rebuttals July 12; USTR completion deadline TODAY (July 20). Final action not yet announced as of this run. Expected to be finalized by ~July 24 to minimize the tariff gap.
- Source: https://www.internationaltradeinsights.com/2026/06/ustr-proposes-301-tariffs-on-60-countries-following-forced-labor-findings-requests-comments-before-july-7th-hearing/

**Proposed rates:**
- 10% for 15 countries that have committed to forced labor import prohibitions
- 12.5% for 45 other countries (including China, India, Vietnam, Japan, EU countries, etc.)

**Impact on calculator:** The S122-01 seed entry already has `endDate: "2026-07-23"` (correct). Updated the notes in S122-01 to reflect the CIT ruling, Federal Circuit stay, and pending replacement. **A new seed entry will be needed once the forced labor Section 301 is formally announced.** Monitor should check again in the next 1–4 days.

**Code change:** `prisma/seed-data/ieepa-tariff-rates.json` — updated `S122-01` notes, set `supersededBy: "S301-FORCED-LABOR-PENDING"` as placeholder.

## 3. CAPE Phase 2 (Launched June 29, 2026) — CODE COMMENT UPDATE

**Sources:**
- https://www.thompsonhinesmartrade.com/2026/06/cbp-confirms-june-29-2026-ieepa-tariff-refund-process-phase-2-launch/
- CSMS 69035485 (CBP, June 23, 2026)

**What changed:** CBP deployed CAPE Phase 2 on June 29, 2026, expanding coverage to entries flagged for reconciliation (entry types 01, 02, 06 where the Type 09 reconciliation entry has NOT yet been filed). Phase 2 estimate: ~2.8M additional entries, ~$28.7B in additional refunds, bringing combined Phase 1+2 to ~$130B of $166B total.

**Still excluded from ALL CAPE phases:**
- Type 08 (Duty Deferral)
- Type 09 (Reconciliation entry itself)
- Type 23 (TIB)
- Type 47 (Drawback) — deferred to a future Phase 3 (timeline unknown)

**Impact on calculator:** The `EXCLUDED_ENTRY_TYPES` set `["08", "09", "23", "47"]` remains correct — these are excluded from Phase 1 AND Phase 2. However, comments in `tariff-calculator.ts` referred to "CAPE Phase 1" which is now stale.

**Code changes:** `src/lib/tariff-calculator.ts`:

- Updated eligibility reason string from "excluded from CAPE Phase 1" → "excluded from CAPE (all phases)"
