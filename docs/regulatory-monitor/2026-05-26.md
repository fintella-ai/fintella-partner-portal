## Actionable Finding: CIT Section 122 Ruling + CAFC Stay

### What happened

1. **May 7, 2026** — The U.S. Court of International Trade issued a 2-1 decision in *Burlap & Barrel Inc. v. United States* holding Section 122 tariffs (the 10% global surcharge currently at HTS 9903.03.01) **unlawful**. The court found the statutory conditions required to invoke Section 122 of the Trade Act of 1974 (19 U.S.C. 2132) were not satisfied, and that the presidential proclamation represented an overly expansive interpretation of the delegated authority.

2. **Relief scope** — The CIT's permanent injunction prohibits collection **only as to the three named plaintiffs**: Burlap & Barrel Inc., Basic Fun Inc., and the State of Washington.

3. **May 8, 2026** — The government appealed to the U.S. Court of Appeals for the Federal Circuit (CAFC). USTR Greer stated confidence the government would prevail.

4. **May 12, 2026** — The CAFC entered an **administrative stay** suspending the CIT's permanent injunction while the court considers a full stay pending appeal.

5. **May 20, 2026** — The CIT denied the government's separate motion for a stay of enforcement of its judgment pending appeal. However, the CAFC's administrative stay remains controlling.

### Current practical status

Section 122 tariffs are **still being collected** from all importers (10%, HTS 9903.03.01) except the three named plaintiffs. The CAFC stay means CBP has not changed collection procedures. The tariff expires July 24, 2026 unless Congress acts (considered unlikely). The Federal Circuit appeal outcome remains uncertain.

### What was updated in the code

- `prisma/seed-data/ieepa-tariff-rates.json` — Updated the `notes` field of rate record `S122-01` to document the CIT ruling, the limited injunction scope, the CAFC administrative stay, and the practical collection status. Rate (10%), `effectiveDate`, and `endDate` (2026-07-23) are **unchanged** because collection continues for all importers except the three named plaintiffs.
- `_metadata.generatedDate` bumped from `2026-04-29` to `2026-05-26`.
