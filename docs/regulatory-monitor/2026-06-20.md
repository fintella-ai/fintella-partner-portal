  Sources: https://www.skadden.com/insights/publications/2026/05/us-trade-court-strikes-down-section-122-tariffs, https://www.bdo.com/insights/tax/u-s-court-of-international-trade-invalidates-section-122-tariffs

  Sources: https://reason.com/volokh/2026/06/11/federal-circuit-stays-enforcement-of-ruling-against-trumps-section-122-tariffs/, https://diaztradelaw.com/appeals-court-keeps-section-122-tariffs-in-place-for-now-what-importers-need-to-know/

### 5. Section 122 Expiration — Upcoming Risk (July 24, 2026)

Section 122 of the Trade Act of 1974 caps surcharges at 150 days. The February 24, 2026 effective date makes July 24, 2026 the statutory expiration. The President cannot extend unilaterally; only Congress can extend (considered politically unlikely).

**No replacement mechanism has been announced as of June 20, 2026.**

Likely successor vehicles being watched:
- **Section 301** investigations (launched March 11, 2026 against 16 economies for excess manufacturing capacity, 60+ economies for forced labor enforcement). No cap, no time limit. Federal Register notice with proposed tariff actions would be the trigger.
- **Pharma Section 232** — scheduled to take effect July 31, 2026. Sector-specific, not a general replacement.
- **Semiconductor/MedTech Section 232** — investigations in progress.  
  Sources: https://www.peacocktariffconsulting.com/section-122-expiration-tracker/, https://www.tariffstool.com/guides/section-301-replacing-section-122

**Calculator action needed when:** A new presidential proclamation or Federal Register notice establishes a post-July-24 tariff mechanism. At that point, add new rate entries and update `S122-01.supersededBy`.
