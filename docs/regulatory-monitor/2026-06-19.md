## Developments Requiring Attention

### 1. Section 122 Expires in 35 Days (July 24, 2026) — URGENT

The current replacement tariff — Proclamation 11012, 10% global surcharge under 19 U.S.C. §2132, HTS 9903.03.01 — expires at 12:01 AM ET on **July 24, 2026** (the statutory 150-day maximum). Congress has not moved to extend it. Executive action cannot extend it unilaterally.

The Administration is actively preparing successors:
- **Section 301 (primary):** USTR launched 60 Section 301 investigations in June 2026 on "failures to take action on forced labor goods." Proposed tariff action hearing dates in July closely track the S122 expiration, suggesting a deliberate handoff. Rates and HTS codes not yet announced.
- **Section 232 (secondary):** Active investigations into pharmaceuticals and medical devices; could produce new national-security tariffs that stack on Section 301.

**Impact on calculator:**
- The `S122-01` entry in `ieepa-tariff-rates.json` already has `endDate: "2026-07-23"` — correct as-is.
- If a Section 301 successor lands before July 24 with new HTS chapter 99 codes, a new rate entry will be needed immediately.
- The `section122` rateType and `9903.03.01` HTS code should be retired post-July 24.
- **Action needed:** Monitor USTR for proposed Section 301 tariff order in early July. This monitor will flag it.

### 2. Federal Circuit Granted Full Stay of CIT Section 122 Invalidation (June 11, 2026)

On **May 7, 2026**, the CIT (2-1 panel) struck down Proclamation 11012 as exceeding statutory authority (*State of Oregon v. United States* and *Burlap and Barrel, Inc. v. Trump*, Slip Op. 26-47). The court found that current economic conditions do not constitute the "large and serious balance-of-payments deficits" required by the statute.

On **June 11, 2026**, the **U.S. Court of Appeals for the Federal Circuit (CAFC) granted a full stay pending appeal**, pausing the CIT injunction. Section 122 tariffs at 10% are currently being collected as if the CIT ruling never happened.

**Impact on calculator:**
- No code change needed today — tariffs are being collected.
- However, if the CAFC rules for plaintiffs on the merits (likely Q3/Q4 2026), Section 122 tariffs could be retroactively invalidated, creating a second wave of refund eligibility similar to IEEPA.
- **Action needed:** Note this legal context in user-facing calculator UI. The notes field on `S122-01` should eventually be updated to reflect the legal saga.

### 3. Government Appealed IEEPA Refund Order (June 2, 2026)

On **June 2, 2026**, the DOJ formally appealed to the Federal Circuit Judge Eaton's Euro-Notions refund order (requiring CBP to refund ~$166B in IEEPA tariffs to all importers). The government argues the order constitutes an impermissible universal injunction.

Key facts:
- **CAPE processing continues** despite the appeal — no stay was granted on refund processing.
- CBP has already paid out approximately **$85 billion** in IEEPA refunds as of mid-June 2026.
- The appeal is about whether the refund order extends to importers who did NOT file protective actions at CIT.

**Impact on calculator:**
- No code change needed. CAPE Phase 1 eligibility logic (`checkEligibility()`) is unaffected.
- If the CAFC rules that the universal refund order is invalid, some importers' refund paths could narrow to protest or litigation only. The `FilingMethod` enum already covers these paths.

### 4. CAPE Phase 2 — Still No Announced Date

CBP has not announced a CAPE Phase 2 launch date. Trade press consensus points to Q3–Q4 2026. Phase 2 would add coverage for:
- **Type 09** (reconciliation entries)
- **Type 47** (drawback entries)
- Historically liquidated entries beyond the current 80-day CAPE Phase 1 window

Interim CBP guidance: hold reconciliation filings (Type 09) unless the deadline is <30 days away, so CAPE declarations can be processed first.

The `EXCLUDED_ENTRY_TYPES` set in `tariff-calculator.ts:209` currently includes `"08"`, `"09"`, `"23"`, `"47"` — correct for Phase 1.

**Impact on calculator:**
- When Phase 2 launches, `"09"` and `"47"` should be removed from `EXCLUDED_ENTRY_TYPES`.
- The `FilingMethod` logic may also need a new `cape_phase2` path.
- **Action needed:** Monitor CBP for CSMS announcement. This routine will flag it.
