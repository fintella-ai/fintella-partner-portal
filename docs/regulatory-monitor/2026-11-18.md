## Summary of landscape as of 2026-11-18

- **IEEPA tariffs**: Terminated Feb 24, 2026 by SCOTUS ruling in *Learning Resources, Inc. v. Trump*. No new IEEPA executive orders since EO 14389 (Feb 20, 2026).
- **CAPE Phase 1**: Active since April 20, 2026. Covers unliquidated and liquidated-within-80-days entries (Types 01, 02, 06).
- **CAPE Phase 2**: Launched June 29, 2026. Extends to Type 06 FTZ warehousing and reconciliation-flagged entries; CAPE declaration must precede Type 09 reconciliation filing.
- **CAPE Phase 3**: Launched October 6, 2026. Covers finally liquidated entries, **plaintiff importers only** (must have a pending CIT case with a reliquidation order and have submitted importer number to CBP by July 30, 2026).
- **Section 122 tariffs**: Expired July 23–24, 2026 (150-day statutory limit). Replaced by Section 301 forced-labor tariffs at 10% or 12.5% depending on country (HTS 9903.05.xx), effective July 24, 2026.
- **China Section 301 exclusions**: Extended November 6/10, 2026 through January 9, 2027 (maritime/shipbuilding Section 301 suspension also extended 2 months). No effect on IEEPA refund calculations.
