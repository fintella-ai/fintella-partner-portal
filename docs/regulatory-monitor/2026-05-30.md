### 4. Court of International Trade — New Rulings

**Result: ONE ACTIONABLE CHANGE — updated S122-01 notes.**

#### Section 122 / Proclamation 11012 Invalidated — May 7, 2026

- **Case:** State of Washington, Burlap and Barrel Inc., and Basic Fun Inc. v. United States (CIT)
- **Date:** May 7, 2026
- **Ruling:** CIT declared Presidential Proclamation 11012 (the Section 122 10% global import surcharge) invalid as contrary to law. The court held that the proclamation failed to identify the type of balance-of-payments deficit (a current account imbalance, not a general trade deficit) required by 19 U.S.C. § 2132.
- **Scope of relief:** Limited — injunction and refund order apply only to the three named plaintiffs. CBP continues collecting the 10% surcharge from all other importers.
- **May 12, 2026:** CAFC issued an administrative stay of the CIT's order while it considers motions for a more durable stay pending appeal.
- **May 20, 2026:** CIT denied the government's motion for a stay pending appeal (finding no likelihood of success on merits, no irreparable harm, and public interest did not favor stay).
- **Current status:** CAFC appeal is proceeding. The 10% Section 122 surcharge is still being collected from the general importer population while the appeal is pending.
- **Implication for calculator:** Section 122 duties are NOT currently refundable via CAPE. CAPE covers IEEPA duties only (Feb 4, 2025–Feb 23, 2026). A final CAFC ruling in favor of plaintiffs could open a new refund pathway for Section 122 duties, but that has not happened yet. Importers paying Section 122 should monitor the CAFC outcome and preserve refund rights (protest within 180 days of liquidation if concerned).
- Sources: [Gibson Dunn](https://www.gibsondunn.com/section-122-global-tariffs-invalidated-by-the-court-of-international-trade-ruling-and-next-steps/) | [Skadden](https://www.skadden.com/insights/publications/2026/05/us-trade-court-strikes-down-section-122-tariffs) | [Holland & Knight](https://www.hklaw.com/en/insights/publications/2026/05/us-court-of-international-trade-invalidates-the-administrations) | [Norton Rose Fulbright](https://www.nortonrosefulbright.com/en/knowledge/publications/199bf568/cit-rules-section-122-tariffs-invalid-limited-injunction-and-likely-appeal)
