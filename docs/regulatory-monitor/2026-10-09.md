## 1. Section 122 Expiration + Section 301 Replacement

**What changed:** The 10% Section 122 global surcharge lapsed at 12:01 AM ET on July 24, 2026, after its 150-day statutory maximum. Congress never passed an extension. Simultaneously, USTR's Section 301 "Forced Labor" tariffs — based on a final determination issued June 2, 2026 following investigations launched March 2026 — took effect:

- **10%** — countries with a forced-labor import ban that is not effectively enforced
- **12.5%** — countries with no forced-labor import ban (includes China, Vietnam, Thailand)

HTS Chapter 99 headings: action codes 9903.05.01–9903.05.09; country provisions 9903.05.20–9903.05.84; in-transit relief 9903.05.85. CBP implementing guidance: CSMS #69326983.

**Also:** A separate Section 301 Brazil tariff of 25% took effect July 22, 2026, two days prior.

**Unlike Section 122**, Section 301 has no fixed sunset — it terminates only on USTR four-year review under 19 U.S.C. 2417(c).

## 2. CAPE Phase 2 — Launched June 29, 2026

**What changed:** CBP launched CAPE Phase 2 on June 29, 2026 per CSMS #69035485 (issued June 23, 2026).

**New coverage:** Entries of types 01, 02, and 06 that are **flagged for reconciliation**, provided:
- The corresponding type-09 reconciliation entry has **not yet been filed**
- The entry is unliquidated **or** liquidated within the 80-day CAPE window

**Mechanics:** CAPE Phase 2 strips IEEPA duties from reconciliation-flagged entries before the importer files the type-09. This lets the reconciliation calculation proceed without the IEEPA component.

**Still excluded (all phases):**
- Type 08 (Duty Deferral)
- Type 09 (Reconciliation Summary) — the underlying flagged entries are eligible, not the 09 itself
- Type 23 (Temporary Importation under Bond)
- Type 47 (Drawback)
- Finally liquidated entries — planned for Phase 3 (no launch date announced)

**Phase 3 preview:** CBP confirmed Phase 3 is in development. It is expected to cover finally liquidated entries and entries under open protest. No timeline.

**Impact on calculator:** `src/lib/tariff-calculator.ts` was updated:

- `EntryForEligibility` gains `isReconFlagged?: boolean` field
- `checkEligibility()` routes reconciliation-flagged eligible entries to `filingMethod: "cape_phase2"` with CSMS citation
- Type-09 exclusion reason now hints that underlying 01/02/06 entries may be eligible via Phase 2
- New `getFilingMethodLabel()` export for UI display
