### 2. CBP / CSMS Bulletins
**No new CSMS messages since the last monitor run.** CAPE Phase 2 launched June 29, 2026 (covering reconciliation-flagged Type 01/02/06 entries where no Type 09 has yet been filed). **Phase 3 is confirmed in development** per Thompson Hine SmartTrade (June 2026) but no launch date announced. Phase 3 will add coverage for historically liquidated entries and Type 47 drawback entries.

**Calculator impact:** None. Phase 2 entries (reconciliation-flagged Types 01/02/06) were not excluded under Phase 1 logic and correctly route through `checkEligibility()` as `cape_phase1`. The `EXCLUDED_ENTRY_TYPES` set (`["08", "09", "23", "47"]`) remains correct. When Phase 3 launches, the exclusion of Type 47 (`isDrawback`) will need revisiting.

### 3. USTR
**No new tariff modifications.** However, a significant **non-IEEPA** deadline is approaching: China Section 301 product exclusions expire 11:59 PM EST on November 9–10, 2026 (5 days from today). These are Section 301 exclusions, not IEEPA tariffs, and do not affect the IEEPA refund calculator.

### 4. CIT Rulings
**No new CIT rulings found for November 2026.** The operative precedent remains *Learning Resources, Inc. v. Trump*, 607 U.S. ___ (2026) (decided Feb 20, 2026). The Euro-Notions Florida broad-relief order (April 7, 2026) is the basis for the CAPE process and is unchanged.

### 5. Section 122 Tariffs (9903.03.xx codes)
**Expired as expected on July 24, 2026** (150-day statutory cap under 19 U.S.C. §2132). This is already correctly reflected in `prisma/seed-data/ieepa-tariff-rates.json` as entry `S122-01` with `"endDate": "2026-07-23"`. No extension was issued. The administration transitioned to a Section 301 forced labor framework after expiry — Section 301 rates are not IEEPA and are not refundable via CAPE.

### 6. CAPE Phase 2
**Confirmed live since June 29, 2026.** Scope: reconciliation-flagged entries (Types 01, 02, 06) where no Type 09 has been filed. CBP guidance last updated July 10, 2026. No new announcements expanding Phase 2 scope. Phase 3 (Type 47 drawback + historically liquidated entries) remains "in development" with no timeline published.
