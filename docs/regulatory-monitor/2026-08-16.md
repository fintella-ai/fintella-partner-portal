## Finding 1: IRS Overpayment Rate — Q3 2026 Updated to 7%

**What changed:** The IRS quarterly overpayment interest rate rose from 6% (Q2 2026) back to 7% for Q3 2026 (July 1–September 30, 2026). This rate applies to customs duty refund interest under 19 U.S.C. §1505(c) via IRC 6621/6622, compounded daily.

**Source:** IRS Internal Revenue Bulletin 2026-22; confirmed by [Greenback Tax Services report](https://www.greenbacktaxservices.com/blog/irs-q3-interest-rates-rise-7-percent/) and [Optima Tax Relief](https://optimataxrelief.com/blog/irs-interest-rates-rise-for-q3-2026-what-taxpayers-need-to-know/).

**Rates for Q3 2026:**
- Individual overpayment: **7%** (up from 6%)
- Corporate overpayment (standard): **6%** (up from 5%)
- Large corporate overpayment (>$10k): **4.5%** (up from 3.5%)
- Underpayment: **7%** (up from 6%)
- Large corporate underpayment: **9%** (up from 8%)
- Federal short-term rate: **4%** (up from 3%)

**Why it matters:** Every IEEPA refund calculation includes compound daily interest from the duty deposit date to the refund date. With Q3 2026 absent from the seed data, any calculator run today would understate interest for entries whose refund process crosses into July 2026.

**Calculator impact:** CAPE processing target is 45 days. Importers who submitted CAPE declarations in late May/early June 2026 may be receiving refunds in July/August 2026 — the Q3 7% rate applies to the final weeks of their accrual period.

**Code change:** Added Q3 2026 entry to `prisma/seed-data/ieepa-tariff-rates.json` under `irsInterestRates.quarters`.

## Finding 2: CAPE Phase 2 Launched June 29, 2026

**What changed:** CBP deployed CAPE Phase 2 on June 29, 2026, expanding the automated refund program to cover entries **flagged for reconciliation** (entry types 01, 02, 06) in CBP ACE where the reconciliation summary entry (Type 09) has **not yet been filed**.

**Source:**
- [M.E. Dey & Co. — CAPE Phase 2 Is Live](https://www.medey.com/cape-phase-2-is-live-here-is-what-that-means-for-ieepa-refunds/)
- [Customs & International Trade Law Blog — CAPE Now Accepts Reconciliation-Flagged Entries](https://customsandinternationaltradelaw.com/2026/06/26/cape-reconciliation-refunds/)
- [Diaz Trade Law — CAPE Phase II Is Live](https://diaztradelaw.com/cape-ieepa-duty-refunds/)

**Key rules:**
1. Same 80-day liquidation window as Phase 1 (unliquidated or liquidated within 80 days)
2. **CRITICAL ORDERING REQUIREMENT**: CAPE declaration must be submitted **BEFORE** filing the reconciliation entry (Type 09). Filing Type 09 first permanently bars the underlying entries from the CAPE automated path.
3. Exception: if reconciliation deadline is within 30 days, CBP advises prioritizing the reconciliation filing.
4. Type 09 entries themselves, Type 47 drawback, surety-paid entries, and historically liquidated entries remain excluded.

**CAPE progress (as of July 31, 2026):** 75,000+ CAPE declarations submitted; 17.69M entries validated; $128.68B in potential/certified refunds accepted.

**Code changes:**
- `src/lib/tariff-calculator.ts`:
  - Added `"cape_phase2"` to `FilingMethod` union type
  - Added `isFlaggedForReconciliation?: boolean` field to `EntryForEligibility` interface
  - Updated `EXCLUDED_ENTRY_TYPES` comment to clarify the Phase 1/Phase 2 distinction
  - Added reconciliation-flagged entry handling in `checkEligibility()` (step 4a) with the critical ordering warning as `reviewNote`
- `prisma/seed-data/ieepa-tariff-rates.json`:
  - Added `capePhase2` top-level block documenting Phase 2 scope, ordering rule, and still-excluded types

**What replaced it:** USTR finalized Section 301 forced-labor tariffs at 10% or 12.5% on 60 countries, effective July 24, 2026 simultaneously with Section 122's expiry (HTS codes 9903.05.20–9903.05.84, per [Honigman LLP](https://www.honigman.com/alert-3462) and [Freight Figures](https://www.freightfigures.com/articles/section-301-forced-labor-tariffs-live-july-24-2026)).

**Important:** Section 301 is NOT an IEEPA tariff and is NOT refundable via CAPE. This calculator covers only IEEPA duties (Feb 4–Feb 23, 2026) and Section 122 surcharges (Feb 24–Jul 23, 2026). Updated `S122-01.notes` and `supersededBy` in the seed data to document this clearly.
