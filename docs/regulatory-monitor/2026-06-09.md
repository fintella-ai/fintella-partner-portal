## 5. Section 122 — Status Update (9903.03.xx codes)

**Current status:** 10% global surcharge still being collected from all importers except 3 named plaintiffs.

**Key legal development:** The CIT struck down Section 122 tariffs as unlawful on May 7, 2026 (2-1 panel; *Burlap & Barrel et al. v. United States*), holding that the statutory balance-of-payments prerequisites were not satisfied. The injunction is **limited to the 3 plaintiff importers only**. The CAFC entered an administrative stay on May 12, 2026 — tariff collection continues for all other importers pending the government's appeal.

**Rate:** Confirmed 10% per Presidential Proclamation 11012 (signed Feb 20, 2026). The 15% figure referenced in some sources is the statutory maximum under Section 122, not the rate actually imposed.

**Expiration:** July 24, 2026 at 12:01 AM ET (150-day statutory limit). Congress can extend; the President cannot. No extension legislation has been introduced.

**Section 301 successor strategy:** USTR launched 60 Section 301 investigations targeting forced labor in June 2026, explicitly designed to establish replacement tariff authority before Section 122 expires July 24.

**Impact on calculator:** S122-01 notes corrected in this update (see Changes Made below).
