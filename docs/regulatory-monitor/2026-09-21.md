## Other Developments Noted (Not Requiring Calculator Changes)

### Section 338 Tariffs on Canada
- President Trump invoked Section 338 of the Tariff Act of 1930 (July 20, 2026). 50% tariff on certain Canadian goods effective **August 22, 2026** (a 3-day suspension delayed the August 19 effective date).
- **Not an IEEPA tariff** — not refundable via CAPE. Section 338 authority is entirely separate.
- USMCA origination does NOT exempt covered goods from Section 338 (unlike IEEPA fentanyl tariffs).
- The calculator's `IEEPA_END_DATE` (Feb 23, 2026) already gates out all post-Feb 2026 entries as "excluded_date". No calculator change needed.
- Sources:

## Risk Flags

1. **Federal Circuit appeal** — Government's appeal of the universal IEEPA refund order (_Euro-Notions_ lead case) is still pending. If the Federal Circuit reverses the CIT's universal reliquidation order, Phase 3 (and possibly Phase 2) processing for non-plaintiff importers could be halted. CAPE pipeline risk remains.

2. **Phase 3 IOR deadline already passed** — July 30, 2026 was the deadline for IOR submission to CBP to qualify for Phase 3. Importers who missed this deadline are likely limited to the litigation path unless CBP extends the window. Calculator now surfaces this as `needsReview`.

4. **Section 338 Canada tariffs** — Not IEEPA, not refundable. If any clients ask why August 2026 Canadian imports show "excluded_date" in the calculator, that is correct behavior.
