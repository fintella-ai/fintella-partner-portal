### 3. USTR — Trade Policy Announcements

**Result: Section 301 proposals found (November 25, 2026). Not relevant to IEEPA refund calculator.**

USTR announced affirmative determinations on November 25, 2026, in 14 of 16 Section 301 "structural excess capacity" investigations, with proposed tariffs of up to 35% on 14 trading partners (China up to 20%, EU/Japan/South Korea/Switzerland/Taiwan at 15%). These are **proposed** Section 301 tariffs, not in effect; public comment period open (January 5, 2027 deadline). The Brazil notice proposes a sugar/aircraft modification (comments due December 30, 2026).

**Impact on calculator: None.** Section 301 tariffs are not covered by CAPE, are not refundable via the IEEPA refund process, and do not affect any eligibility logic in `tariff-calculator.ts`. The calculator correctly notes (line 249–252) that Section 301 duties are not refundable — only the IEEPA portion is recoverable.

### 6. CAPE Phase 2 — Type 09 / Type 47 Drawback

**Result: Type 09 and Type 47 remain excluded. No Phase 2 expansion announced.**

CBP's Phase 2 (live June 29, 2026) covers reconciliation-flagged entries of types 01/02/06 where no Type 09 has yet been filed. Once a Type 09 is on file, the underlying entry is excluded. Type 47 drawback entries are permanently excluded from CAPE ("ENTRY ON DRAWBACK").

No CBP announcement of Phase 2 expansion to cover Type 09 or Type 47 was found in this monitoring window. `EXCLUDED_ENTRY_TYPES` in `tariff-calculator.ts` correctly reflects CBP's eligibility rules; no update needed.
