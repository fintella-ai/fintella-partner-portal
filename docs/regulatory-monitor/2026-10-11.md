## Key background (already reflected in codebase)

### IEEPA termination (Feb 20, 2026)
- Supreme Court ruled 6-3 in *Learning Resources, Inc. v. Trump* that IEEPA does not authorize tariffs
- EO 14389 "Ending Certain Tariff Actions" terminated all IEEPA duties, effective Feb 24, 2026
- All IEEPA entries in `ieepa-tariff-rates.json` correctly set `endDate: "2026-02-23"` and `supersededBy: "TERMINATED-SCOTUS"`

### Section 122 replacement (Feb 24 – Jul 23, 2026)
- 10% global surcharge under Section 122 of Trade Act of 1974 replaced IEEPA tariffs
- HTS codes 9903.03.01 through 9903.03.11
- Expired at statutory 150-day limit: July 23, 2026 at 11:59 PM ET
- **Already reflected in `S122-01` entry:** `endDate: "2026-07-23"`, `supersededBy: null`
- Note: CIT ruled Section 122 (Proclamation 11012) invalid on May 7, 2026 (plaintiffs only); Federal Circuit issued administrative stay May 12; tariffs expired July 23 before appeal resolved
- Sources: [HK Law](https://www.hklaw.com/en/insights/publications/2026/05/us-court-of-international-trade-invalidates-the-administrations), [Gibson Dunn](https://www.gibsondunn.com/section-122-global-tariffs-invalidated-by-the-court-of-international-trade-ruling-and-next-steps/)

### Section 301 forced-labor tariffs (Jul 24, 2026 – present)
- Replaced Section 122 overnight on Jul 24, 2026 with no gap
- New HTS codes 9903.05.20 through 9903.05.84 (10% for ~17 countries; 12.5% for ~38 countries + EU)
- Exemptions at 9903.05.85 through 9903.06.21; Section 232 goods exempt
- **NOT in the rate database** — correctly omitted: these are not IEEPA duties and are not refundable via CAPE
- Sources: [FreightFigures](https://www.freightfigures.com/articles/section-301-forced-labor-tariffs-live-july-24-2026), [HTS Hero](https://htshero.com/blog/2026-hts-revision-13)
