### 1. Section 122 Tariff — EXPIRED July 24, 2026 ✅

**What changed:** The Section 122 global surcharge (10%) expired by operation of law on July 24, 2026 — it hit the 150-day statutory maximum under 19 U.S.C. §2132. No new proclamation was issued to extend or replace it under Section 122.

**Rate clarification confirmed:** The rate remained **10% throughout** (Feb 24 – Jul 23, 2026). Trump announced intent to raise to 15% on Feb 22, 2026 via social media, but no formal proclamation was ever issued. The seed data note has been updated to be explicit on this point.

**Update made:**

### 2. Section 301 Forced-Labor Tariffs — NEW, Effective July 24, 2026 ✅

**What changed:** USTR's Section 301 forced-labor tariffs took effect July 24, 2026 — the same day Section 122 expired. These are NOT IEEPA tariffs and are NOT refundable via CAPE. They are tracked in the rate database for completeness.

- **12.5% rate** (HTS 9903.05.20–9903.05.84): ~38 economies that do not prohibit or enforce bans on imports made with forced labor (China, Brazil, Vietnam, Russia, etc.)
- **10% rate** (HTS 9903.05.20–9903.05.84): 17 economies that ban forced-labor imports or committed to such a ban (Canada, Mexico, India, UK, Argentina, Bangladesh, Cambodia, Ecuador, El Salvador, Guatemala, Honduras, Indonesia, Jordan, Malaysia, Pakistan, Sri Lanka, Trinidad and Tobago)

**Update made:**
- `prisma/seed-data/ieepa-tariff-rates.json` — Added two new entries: `S301-FL-HIGH-01` (12.5%) and `S301-FL-LOW-01` (10%)

### 3. CAPE Phase 3 — CIT Reliquidation Order (July 17, 2026) ✅

**What changed:** On July 17, 2026, CIT Senior Judge Richard Eaton issued an order directing CBP to reliquidate "any and all" finally-liquidated IEEPA-duty entries (those liquidated >80 days ago where the protest window has expired). This order covers the ~3,700 cases before the CIT.

Previously, the calculator routed entries with expired protest windows (>180 days since liquidation) to `filingMethod: "litigation"`. These entries now have a CAPE Phase 3 pathway.

**Key caveats:**
- Phase 3 was not yet live as of Aug 8, 2026 (CBP progress report due Aug 4 to the CIT)
- The reliquidation order directly covers active CIT litigants
- Non-litigants may access Phase 3 via the **Freestyle World v. United States** class action (Court No. 26-01088, formerly Euro-Notions Florida which dismissed July 16, 2026). Class certification oral argument is scheduled for **August 19, 2026**
- Closed settlement conference was scheduled for August 5, 2026
- CNBC reported ~$100 billion in "liberation day" tariff refunds disbursed as of August 5, 2026

**Update made:**
- `src/lib/tariff-calculator.ts` — `FilingMethod` type now includes `"cape_phase2"` and `"cape_phase3"`
- `checkEligibility()` — Finally-liquidated entries (>180 days) now return `filingMethod: "cape_phase3"` with `needsReview: true` and a note about Phase 3 status and the Freestyle World class action. Previously returned `filingMethod: "litigation"`.
