### 3. CBP / CAPE — No new bulletins; Phase 2 status unchanged

No new November 2026 CSMS messages found. Status as of last known bulletin (CSMS #69127837, July 2026):

- **Phase 1** (launched Apr 20, 2026): Types 01, 02, 03, 06 — active, no restrictions
- **Phase 2** (launched Jun 29, 2026): Reconciliation-flagged entries (types 01/02/06 with recon flag) accepted **if no Type 09 has been filed first**; sequencing is critical — CAPE declaration must precede the Type 09 reconciliation filing or the pathway is permanently closed
- **Type 47 drawback**: Still excluded. Filing a drawback claim converts an entry to Type 47 and closes the CAPE pathway permanently
- **Phase 3 (warehouse, FTZ, other)**: Timeline unknown
- ~$126B refunded through CAPE as of Oct 2, 2026

### 4. CIT — Slip Op. 26-123 (Oct 8, 2026) class certification — no new rulings

The CIT certified a nationwide importer class in *Freestyle World v. United States* (Slip Op. 26-123, Oct 8, 2026), covering all importers of record who paid IEEPA duties from Feb 1, 2025 – Feb 19, 2026, under Rule 23(b)(2). Importers with their own pending CIT suits are excluded.

**Impact on calculator:** None yet. Class certification ≠ refund order. As of Oct 21, 2026, CBP still processes CAPE refunds only for importers who are active plaintiffs with a reliquidation order.
