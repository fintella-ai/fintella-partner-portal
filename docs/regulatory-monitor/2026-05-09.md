## 1. CIT Strikes Down Section 122 Tariffs (May 7, 2026) ⚠️

**Source:** Multiple law firm alerts and news reports dated May 7–8, 2026.

### What happened

The U.S. Court of International Trade issued its ruling in consolidated cases:

- **Oregon v. Trump**, No. 26-01472 (State of Oregon + ~20 other states)
- **Burlap & Barrel, Inc. v. Trump**, No. 26-01606 (private importer plaintiffs)

The court held that the Administration's 10% global import surcharge imposed under **Section 122 of the Trade Act of 1974 (19 U.S.C. § 2132)** is **unlawful**. Section 122 authorizes a maximum 150-day "temporary import surcharge" to address balance-of-payments emergencies — the court found the Administration's invocation exceeded the statute's scope or procedural requirements.

### Injunction scope — NARROW

The permanent injunction issued by the CIT **applies only to the named plaintiffs**:
- Burlap & Barrel, Inc.
- Basic Fun!, Inc.
- State of Washington

The court **declined to issue a nationwide/universal injunction** and **dismissed claims from 20+ other states** for lack of standing (states alleged only indirect economic harm, which the court found insufficient).

**Practical effect:** Section 122 tariffs (9903.03.01, 10% rate) are **still being collected from all other importers** as of May 9, 2026.

### Appeal status

The Administration filed a notice of appeal to the **U.S. Court of Appeals for the Federal Circuit** immediately following the ruling. No stay has been sought or granted as of May 9, 2026.

### Impact on the TIE calculator

**No rate change needed.** The 10% Section 122 rate in `S122-01` remains accurate for the vast majority of importers. The `endDate` of 2026-07-23 (statutory 150-day max) is unchanged.

**Important distinction:** Section 122 tariffs (Feb 24, 2026 – Jul 23, 2026) are **prospective liabilities**, not IEEPA refunds. The CAPE process covers only IEEPA duties paid before Feb 24, 2026. The Section 122 CIT ruling does **not** affect the IEEPA refund calculator's eligibility logic or rate data.

**Action taken:** Updated `S122-01.notes` in `ieepa-tariff-rates.json` to document the CIT ruling, docket numbers, injunction scope, and appeal status.
