## Findings

### 1. Section 122 Confirmed Expired — No Surprise

- **Status**: Expected, no new action needed beyond what was already in the seed file.
- **Detail**: The Section 122 10% global surcharge (HTS 9903.03.01) expired at 12:01 AM ET July 24, 2026 by operation of law (150-day statutory maximum). The `endDate: "2026-07-23"` in `S122-01` was correct.
- **Rate never changed**: Trump announced intent to raise to 15% on Feb 22 via social media; no formal proclamation was ever issued. Rate remained 10% through expiration.

### 2. ⚠️ Section 301 Replacement Tariff — NEW ENTRY ADDED

- **Status**: Material change — new entry `S301-01` added to seed file.
- **Authority**: Proclamation 11012 (signed Jul 20, 2026; effective Jul 24, 2026)
- **Legal basis**: Section 301 of the Trade Act of 1974 (19 U.S.C. 2411) — first time Section 301 used in this way as a blanket global tariff.
- **Rate**: Two-tier structure:
  - **10%** on countries with qualifying forced-labor import prohibitions
  - **12.5%** on all other countries
- **Coverage**: ~60 economies, ~99.4% of US imports
- **Exclusions** (HTS 9903.03.02 through 9903.03.11): critical minerals, energy, pharmaceuticals, electronics, vehicles/parts, aerospace, certain agricultural goods, USMCA-qualifying CA/MX goods, CAFTA-DR textiles/apparel, goods subject to Section 232
- **No statutory expiration**: Unlike Section 122's 150-day cap, Section 301 has no automatic sunset.
- **Legal challenge pending**: Liberty Justice Center filed suit shortly after Jul 24 effective date.
- **⚠️ CAPE impact**: Section 301 duties are NOT refundable via CAPE. The IEEPA refund program applies only to IEEPA tariffs (terminated Feb 24, 2026) and to Section 122 duties (expired Jul 24, 2026). Section 301 is a new, separate tariff program.
- **Sources**: 
  - https://www.tariffstool.com/guides/section-301-replacing-section-122
  - https://www.tariffstool.com/guides/tariff-news-2026
  - https://carraglobe.com/section-122-tariff-2026/

### 3. ⚠️ Section 338 Canada Tariffs — Effective Today

- **Status**: New tariff effective August 19, 2026 — NOT in IEEPA refund scope, no seed file change needed.
- **Authority**: Section 338 of the Tariff Act of 1930 — first modern use of this authority.
- **Rate**: 50% additional ad valorem tariff on broad range of Canadian products
- **Scope**: Motor vehicles, wine/alcohol, dairy, cement, furniture, and many other products
- **Source**: Three separate proclamations signed Jul 20, 2026; effective 12:01 AM ET August 19, 2026.
- **⚠️ CAPE impact**: None. These are Section 338 tariffs, not IEEPA tariffs. Not refundable via CAPE.
- **Sources**:

### 6. CAPE Phase 3 — Finally-Liquidated Entries (CIT Order Jul 17, 2026)

- **Status**: No code change — monitoring closely.
