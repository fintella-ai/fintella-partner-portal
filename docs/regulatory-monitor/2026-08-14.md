## 2. CAPE Phase 2 Confirmed — Type 09 Reconciliation-Flagged Entries Now Accepted

### What happened
CAPE Phase 2 launched June 29, 2026 (CSMS #68340863). CBP now accepts entries of types 01, 02, and 06 that are *flagged for reconciliation* (i.e., a Type 09 reconciliation summary is expected but not yet filed). This is **not** a change to which entry types are excluded — the Type 09 reconciliation summary itself remains excluded; Phase 2 expands access for the underlying consumption entries.

### Sources
- [CBP IEEPA Duty Refunds page](https://www.cbp.gov/trade/programs-administration/trade-remedies/ieepa-duty-refunds)
- CBP CSMS #68340863 (UPDATE — CAPE Phase 1 deployment, effective June 29, 2026)

## 3. CAPE Phase 3 — CIT-Ordered Reliquidation of Finally-Liquidated Entries (Pending)

### What happened
On July 17, 2026, CIT Senior Judge Eaton issued an order directing CBP to "reliquidate, without regard to IEEPA duties, any and all of Plaintiffs' entries that have been liquidated for more than 80 days" across the ~3,700 consolidated cases. This is "CAPE Phase 3" for finally-liquidated entries.

CBP filed a progress declaration on August 4, 2026 (Brandon Lord), reporting:
- 75,000+ CAPE declarations submitted
- 17.69 million entries validated and liquidated without IEEPA tariffs
- ~$128.68 billion in potential/certified refunds accepted for processing

## 4. Section 301 Forced Labor Tariffs — New Tariff Regime (Not IEEPA-Refundable)

### What happened
USTR final action July 23, 2026 imposed Section 301 forced-labor tariffs effective July 24, 2026:
- **10%** on imports from economies with qualifying forced-labor import prohibitions
- **12.5%** on imports from ~46 economies that do not meet the threshold
- HTS codes `9903.05.20`–`9903.05.84` (one code per economy)
- Exemptions at `9903.05.85`–`9903.06.21` (USMCA/CAFTA-DR, civil aircraft, pharma, humanitarian goods, Section 232 steel/aluminum/vehicles)
- CBP guidance: CSMS #69326983

**Active litigation:** 25 states (coalition) filed CIT suit August 3, 2026 to block enforcement. No injunction granted as of this run.

### Sources
- [C.H. Robinson: New Section 301 Forced Labor Tariffs Now In Effect (Jul 24)](https://www.chrobinson.com/en-us/resources/insights-and-advisories/client-advisories/2026q3/07-24-26-client-advisory-new-section-301-forced-labor-tariffs-now-in-effect/)
- [Troutman Pepper Locke: Forced Labor, Meet Section 301](https://www.troutman.com/insights/forced-labor-meet-section-301-new-tariffs-target-60-of-americas-biggest-trading-partners/)
- [CBP CSMS #69326983 Guidance](https://content.govdelivery.com/accounts/USDHSCBP/bulletins/421d887)
- [GingerControl: New Section 301 forced-labor tariffs (10–12.5%)](https://gingercontrol.com/briefings/new-section-301-forced-labor-tariffs-10-12-5-52cfe5d8)

### Impact on calculator
**Zero impact on refund calculations** — these tariffs are not IEEPA duties and are not refundable via CAPE. Documented in `S301-FL-01` seed entry for historical reference only.
