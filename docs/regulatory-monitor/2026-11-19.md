## Findings

### 1. IEEPA Executive Orders — No New Activity
No new IEEPA executive orders or presidential proclamations in the last 24 hours or recent weeks. Consistent with the Supreme Court's Feb 20, 2026 ruling in *Learning Resources, Inc. v. Trump* (607 U.S. 24-1287) that IEEPA does not authorize tariffs. The seed data reflects all historical IEEPA rates correctly with `supersededBy: "TERMINATED-SCOTUS"`.

### 2. Section 122 Tariffs — Expired as Expected (No Code Change Needed)
The 10% Section 122 surcharge (HTS 9903.03.01–9903.03.11) expired at 12:01 a.m. July 24, 2026, as already reflected in the seed data (`S122-01` entry has `endDate: "2026-07-23"`). Replaced by Section 301 forced-labor duties under new HTS headings 9903.05.20–9903.05.84 (12.5% for ~38 countries, 10% for ~17 countries per HTS Revision 13). These Section 301 replacement duties are **outside the scope of the IEEPA refund calculator** — they are forward-looking tariffs, not IEEPA duties eligible for refund. No seed data or calculator changes needed.

Source: https://htshero.com/blog/2026-hts-revision-13, https://www.tariffstool.com/guides/section-122-tariff-rates-2026

### 3. CAPE Phase 2 — Already Live (Previously Known)
CAPE Phase 2 launched June 29, 2026 (CSMS ~69035485/69066837). It covers entry types 01, 02, and 06 flagged for reconciliation (Type 09), **not** Type 47 drawback entries. No new Phase 2 announcements today. CAPE Phase 3 reportedly rolling out in late July 2026 per secondary sources, but no CBP formal announcement found. The calculator's `FilingMethod` type only includes `cape_phase1` — if Phase 2/3 scope expansion needs to be surfaced to users, that is a product decision for John rather than a data/eligibility logic change today.

Source: https://www.medey.com/cape-phase-2-is-live-here-is-what-that-means-for-ieepa-refunds/, https://iab-sd.com/cape-phase-2-filing-requirements/

### 4. CIT Slip Op. 26-123 — Class-Wide Reliquidation Order (Flag for Review)
The Court of International Trade issued Slip Op. 26-123, a class-wide resolution stating "the Supreme Court's holding necessarily requires the liquidation or reliquidation of all entries without IEEPA duties." The exact date and scope are unclear from search results. **This could affect the calculator's eligibility and filing-method logic** if CBP is now auto-reliquidating entries without requiring CAPE or protest. However, it may also be in-scope with the existing CAPE process. This warrants John's legal review before any code change.

Source: https://www.cit.uscourts.gov/sites/cit/files/26-123.pdf
