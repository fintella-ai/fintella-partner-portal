### 1. CAPE Phase 2 — launched June 29, 2026 ⚠️ ACTIONABLE

**Source:** CSMS # 69066837 / CSMS # 69035485 ([govdelivery — blocked by proxy](https://content.govdelivery.com/accounts/USDHSCBP/bulletins/41de055)); [GHY article](https://www.ghy.com/trade-compliance/cbp-cape-reconciliation-ieepa-tariff-refunds-june-29/); [Thompson Hine SmarTrade](https://www.thompsonhinesmartrade.com/2026/06/cbp-confirms-june-29-2026-ieepa-tariff-refund-process-phase-2-launch/); [CBP Trade Information Notice July 23](https://www.cbp.gov/sites/default/files/2026-07/26_0723_trade_information_notice_CAPE.pdf)

**What changed:**  
Effective June 29, 2026, CAPE now accepts entries of type 01, 02, and 06 that are **flagged for reconciliation in ACE** (i.e., a Type 09 reconciliation entry has been promised but not yet filed). Phase 2 is still limited to the same 80-day liquidation window as Phase 1.

**Critical filing sequence:** The importer MUST submit the CAPE declaration BEFORE filing the Type 09 reconciliation entry. Once Type 09 is filed, the underlying flagged entries are locked out of CAPE Phase 2. Filing in the wrong order forfeits the refund.

**Codebase impact:**  
- `EntryForEligibility` had no `isFlaggedForReconciliation` field  
- `FilingMethod` had no `cape_phase2_recon` value  
- `checkEligibility()` gave no Phase 2 guidance  
- **→ Fixed in this PR**

### 2. CAPE Phase 3 — CIT order July 15, 2026 ⚠️ ACTIONABLE

**Source:** [CIT July 15 order via Thompson Hine](https://www.thompsonhinesmartrade.com/2026/07/cit-orders-cbp-to-process-ieepa-tariff-refunds-for-phase-3-finally-liquidated-entries/); [Green Worldwide article on Phase 3 timeline](https://www.greenworldwide.com/cape-phase-3-for-ieepa-tariff-refunds-on-track-for-end-of-july-as-federal-circuit-appeal-continues/); CBP progress report filed August 4, 2026 (75,000+ declarations, 17.69M entries processed)

**What changed:**  
On July 15, 2026, Judge Richard K. Eaton issued an order directing CBP to reliquidate **finally liquidated entries** (entries past the 80-day CAPE Phase 1 window) under a procedure to be specified in a forthcoming court order. The order applies to ~3,700 IEEPA cases currently before the CIT. CBP confirmed at the June 9 hearing that Phase 3 programming would be ready by late July 2026. Phase 3 covers approximately $11.4 billion (7%) of total IEEPA tariff revenue.

**What this means for entries past 180 days:**  
Previously, entries liquidated more than 180 days ago were `excluded_expired` with `filingMethod: "litigation"` (protest window closed, CIT only). Under Phase 3, CBP will reliquidate these entries through the CAPE system — making litigation the fallback rather than the primary path.

**Note:** The exact procedure is still being established per the July 15 "forthcoming order" language. Phase 3 entries still warrant professional assistance (routing stays `legal_required`), but the correct filing method is now `cape_phase3` rather than `litigation`.

**Codebase impact:**  
- `FilingMethod` had no `cape_phase3` value  
- `checkEligibility()` returned `filingMethod: "litigation"` for >180-day entries  
- Reason text didn't mention Phase 3  
- **→ Fixed in this PR**

### 3. Section 122 tariffs expired July 24, 2026 — minor seed data update

**Source:** [GHY Section 122 article](https://www.ghy.com/trade-compliance/us-10-section-122-tariff-in-effect-feb-24-ieepa-tariffs-directed-to-wind-down-de-minimis-suspension-continues/); [Section 122 tariffs chart](https://tariffschart.com/tariffs/section-122-tariffs); [FreightFigures article](https://www.freightfigures.com/articles/section-301-forced-labor-tariffs-live-july-24-2026)

**What changed:**  
Section 122 tariffs (HTS 9903.03.01–9903.03.11) reached their 150-day statutory maximum and expired at 12:01 AM EDT July 24, 2026. No congressional extension was enacted.

Replacement: On July 24, 2026, USTR imposed **Section 301 forced-labor tariffs** of 10% or 12.5% on imports from 60 economies (HTS 9903.05.20–9903.05.84). These are a **different legal authority** (Trade Act of 1974 § 301, not § 122) and are **not related to the IEEPA refund program**.

**Codebase impact:**  
- `S122-01` in `ieepa-tariff-rates.json` had `supersededBy: null`  
- Notes didn't reflect the expiry or Section 301 successor  
- **→ Fixed in this PR** (note: Section 301 rates not added to the seed — they are outside the IEEPA refund calculator's scope)
