## 2. CBP Bulletins (CSMS)

**Sources checked:**
- https://www.cbp.gov/trade/programs-administration/trade-remedies/ieepa-duty-refunds
- CBP Trade Information Notice dated July 23, 2026

**Finding: CAPE Phase 2 now live as of June 29, 2026**

CBP's July 23, 2026 Trade Information Notice confirms that effective June 29, 2026, CAPE accepts entries of Types 01, 02, and 06 that are **flagged for reconciliation** (where the Type 09 reconciliation entry has not yet been filed). This was a Phase 2 expansion. Importers should:
- File the CAPE declaration first to obtain the IEEPA refund
- File the Type 09 reconciliation entry afterward
- CBP separates the IEEPA refund from the reconciliation calculation

Type 47 drawback entries remain excluded from CAPE. Type 09 entries themselves (the reconciliation summary) remain excluded.

## 5. Section 122 Updates (9903.03.xx codes)

**Sources checked:**
- https://www.honigman.com/alert-3462
- https://www.z2data.com/insights/how-the-termination-of-section-122-tariffs-impacts-your-supply-chain
- https://gingercontrol.com/blog/section-122-tariffs-explained
- https://usecervo.com/resources/section-122-tariffs-explained

**Finding: Section 122 expired July 24, 2026; replaced by Section 301 forced-labor tariffs**

The 150-day Section 122 global 10% surcharge expired at 12:01 AM ET on July 24, 2026 (hard statutory ceiling — no extension possible without Act of Congress). Simultaneously, USTR's Section 301 forced-labor tariffs took effect at the same moment:

- **Rate:** 10% or 12.5% depending on country
- **Coverage:** ~60 economies found by USTR to have inadequate forced-labor enforcement
- **HTS codes:** 9903.05.20 through 9903.05.84 (country-specific headings)
- **Exemptions:** USMCA/CAFTA-DR goods, civil aircraft, pharmaceuticals, Chapter 98 entries (9903.05.85–9903.06.21)
- **Refundability:** NOT refundable via CAPE — completely separate legal authority

Additional context: A May 7, 2026 CIT ruling found Section 122 exceeded presidential authority (moot given the expiration).

**IMPORTANT for the calculator:** Section 122 duties (Feb 24 – Jul 23, 2026) are NOT refundable via CAPE. CAPE only covers IEEPA duties from Feb 4, 2025 – Feb 23, 2026. Section 301 duties (Jul 24, 2026+) are also NOT refundable via CAPE.

**Action taken:**
- Updated `S122-01` in seed file: `supersededBy` set to `"S301-01"`, notes updated to document expiration and Section 301 replacement
- Added `S301-01` informational entry to seed file marking Section 301 as NOT CAPE-eligible
