## 1. Section 122 Expired July 24, 2026 — Replaced by Section 301 Forced Labor Tariffs

**Status: DATA GAP FIXED in this run**

The Section 122 10% global surcharge expired by statute at 12:01 AM EDT on July 24, 2026 (150-day statutory maximum under 19 U.S.C. § 2132). Congress did not extend it. The `S122-01` seed entry had `supersededBy: null`, which was incorrect.

**What replaced it:** USTR Jamieson Greer announced final Section 301 action under forced-labor investigations on July 23, 2026. Effective the same minute Section 122 expired (12:01 AM EDT July 24, 2026), new Section 301 Forced Labor tariffs took effect on 60 economies:

- **Tier 1 — 12.5%** on 46 high-risk economies including China, Vietnam, Thailand, and others. HTS: 9903.05.20–9903.05.84
- **Tier 2 — 10%** on remaining 14 economies. HTS: same range, different subheadings

Key exemptions: USMCA-qualifying goods from Canada and Mexico; CAFTA-DR textile and apparel goods; 471 additional product subheadings per public comment.

**Impact on calculator:** These are NOT IEEPA tariffs and are NOT refundable via CAPE. The Section 301 tariffs apply to entries made on or after July 24, 2026 — outside the IEEPA refund period (Feb 4, 2025 – Feb 23, 2026). No change to `checkEligibility()` logic required.

**Changes made:**
- `prisma/seed-data/ieepa-tariff-rates.json`: Updated `S122-01.supersededBy = "S301-FL-2026"` and updated `notes` to document the expiration
- `prisma/seed-data/ieepa-tariff-rates.json`: Added new `S301-FL-2026` entry documenting the replacement tariff for context

## 2. CAPE Phase 2 Launched June 29, 2026

**Status: CODE UPDATED in this run**

CBP deployed Phase 2 of CAPE within ACE on June 29, 2026. Phase 2 expands eligibility to entry types 01, 02, and 06 that are **flagged for reconciliation**, provided the Type 09 reconciliation entry has NOT yet been filed. The 80-day liquidation window is unchanged.

**Types still excluded (all phases):**
- Type 09 (Reconciliation Summary) — cannot be the subject of a CAPE declaration; if the Type 09 was already filed, the underlying entries are also ineligible
- Type 47 (Drawback) — remains excluded; expected in a future phase
- Types 08, 23 — remain excluded
