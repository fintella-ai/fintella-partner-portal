## 4. CIT Docket — Finally Liquidated Entries ⚠️ WATCH

**CIT issued orders July 15 and July 21, 2026** directing CBP to reliquidate — without regard to IEEPA duties — certain finally liquidated entries in the consolidated V.O.S. Selections-related refund cases (~3,700 related lawsuits before Judge Richard K. Eaton).

**Key caveat:** Per Holland & Knight's analysis, the CIT orders confirm that **only importers who have already filed suit** will receive full refunds for finally liquidated entries through this path. Importers who did not sue cannot use this CIT order to compel reliquidation.

**CBP's position (May 2026 reversal):** CBP argued in May 2026 that it lacks authority to issue IEEPA refunds on finally liquidated entries without an importer-specific court judgment. This position was rejected by CIT for litigants, but non-litigants face an administrative dead end for entries liquidated more than 180 days ago.

**Class certification hearing:** On August 6, 2026, the CIT heard oral argument on a Rule 23(b)(2) class certification motion in *V.O.S. Selections, Inc. v. United States* (Liberty Justice Center). No ruling yet as of this scan. If granted, it could extend refund access to importers who have not individually sued.

**Calculator impact:** The `filingMethod: "litigation"` status for entries liquidated >180 days ago remains correct for non-litigants. No code changes made for this item pending the class certification outcome. Will monitor.
