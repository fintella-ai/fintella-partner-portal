## Prior-Quarter Gaps Fixed in This Run

### 1. IRS Interest Rates Q3 and Q4 2026 — CRITICAL DATA GAP

**What changed:** The seed file (`prisma/seed-data/ieepa-tariff-rates.json`) had IRS quarterly overpayment rates through Q2 2026 only. Without Q3/Q4 rates, `calculateInterest()` returns $0 interest for any calculation period running into July–December 2026 — understating refund estimates by 5+ months of interest.

| Quarter | Period | Rate (Individuals) | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q3 2026 | Jul 1 – Sep 30, 2026 | **7%** (up from Q2's 6%) | Rev. Rul. 2026-10; IRB 2026-22 |
| Q4 2026 | Oct 1 – Dec 31, 2026 | **7%** (unchanged) | Rev. Rul. 2026-15; IRB 2026-36; IRS newsroom |

Corporate rates: 6% (overpayment), 4.5% (>$10k corporate), 9% (large corporate underpayment). Federal short-term rate: 4%.

**What was updated:** Added Q3 2026 and Q4 2026 entries to `irsInterestRates.quarters[]` in the seed file.

### 2. CAPE Phase 2 — Launched June 29, 2026

**What happened:** CBP deployed CAPE Phase 2 on June 29, 2026, extending the CAPE automated refund path to entries of types 01, 02, 06 that are flagged for reconciliation but whose Type 09 reconciliation summary entry has not yet been filed.

**Calculator impact:** None — `EXCLUDED_ENTRY_TYPES` ("08", "09", "23", "47") refers to the Type 09 reconciliation summary entry itself. Underlying 01/02/06 entries that are reconciliation-flagged are already eligible via Phase 1/Phase 2. No code change needed.

**What was updated:** Added `capePhase2` documentation section to seed file.

---

### 3. CAPE Phase 3 — Launched October 6, 2026

**What happened:** CBP deployed CAPE Phase 3 on October 6, 2026, extending the CAPE automated path to finally-liquidated entries — but ONLY for importers who are plaintiffs in CIT cases where the court ordered reliquidation and who submitted their IOR number to CBP by July 30, 2026.

**CIT clarification (early November 2026):** Judge Eaton (CIT) clarified that CIT plaintiffs can file Phase 3 CAPE declarations without first withdrawing their open protests. Protests and CAPE can proceed in parallel.

**Calculator impact:** `checkEligibility()` returns `filingMethod: "litigation"` for finally-liquidated entries — this remains correct for the general case (non-plaintiff importers). The calculator cannot detect plaintiff status from entry data alone. No code change needed.

**Future:** Importers are petitioning the court to order a Phase 4 covering AD/CVD entries; no timeline announced.

**What was updated:** Added `capePhase3` documentation section to seed file.
