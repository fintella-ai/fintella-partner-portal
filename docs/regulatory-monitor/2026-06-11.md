## Key Findings

### 1. CIT Struck Down Section 122 Tariffs — BUT Federal Circuit Stayed the Ruling
**Status: Monitored — no calculator change**

On **May 7, 2026**, a divided CIT three-judge panel ruled 2–1 that the Trump administration's invocation of Section 122 of the Trade Act of 1974 did not meet the statutory criteria (the balance-of-payments emergency standard was not satisfied). The court granted summary judgment to three named importer plaintiffs — the **State of Washington, Burlap and Barrel Inc., and Basic Fun Inc.** — and entered a permanent injunction against collection of Section 122 duties from those three parties only.

On **May 12, 2026**, the Federal Circuit (CAFC) entered an **administrative stay**, suspending the CIT order while the government's appeal proceeds. CBP continues to collect the 10% Section 122 tariff from all other importers. The CIT denied the government's separate motion for a stay on May 20, but the CAFC's administrative stay supersedes that denial.

**Practical effect:** 
- The 10% Section 122 tariff is still being collected from all importers except the three named plaintiffs.
- The appeal is pending; if CAFC affirms the CIT, broader relief could follow but is not guaranteed.
- Importers who are **not** plaintiffs should preserve refund rights by protesting if applicable.

**Calculator impact:** None. `S122-01` rate (10%, endDate 2026-07-23) remains correct. The CAPE eligibility logic is unaffected — Section 122 duties are not part of the IEEPA refund program.

Sources:

### 2. Section 122 Expiration — July 24, 2026 (43 days)
**Status: WATCH — potential calculator-adjacent event**

The Section 122 10% global surcharge expires at 12:01 AM EDT on **July 24, 2026** — the statutory 150-day maximum. Congress can extend it but would need 60 Senate votes (unlikely). The administration may pivot to a replacement mechanism before expiration:

- **Section 301**: USTR is conducting Section 301 investigations (60 economies, forced labor findings announced June 2, 2026) expected to conclude before July 24.
- **New legislation**: Possible but no bill introduced as of this date.
- **Simple expiration**: If nothing happens, the 10% surcharge disappears overnight.

The seed data entry `S122-01` correctly has `endDate: "2026-07-23"`. If a replacement mechanism or extension is announced, a new entry must be added.

**Next action:** Re-run monitor in week of July 14–18 to catch any replacement proclamation before the deadline.
