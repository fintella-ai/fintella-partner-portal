## 1. ⚠️ Section 122 Tariffs: CIT Struck Down — But CAFC Stay Keeps Them In Effect

**Status: Notes updated in seed data. No rate change — rate still 0.10.**

### What happened
On **May 7, 2026**, a divided 2-1 panel of the U.S. Court of International Trade (CIT) held that the 10% global tariff imposed under Section 122 of the Trade Act of 1974 is **unlawful** because the statutory prerequisites for invoking Section 122 were not satisfied.

However, the court issued only a **narrow permanent injunction** — applicable solely to the three prevailing plaintiffs:
- Burlap & Barrel, Inc.
- Basic Fun, Inc.
- State of Washington

The court explicitly declined to issue a nationwide injunction, finding that other state plaintiffs lacked Article III standing (not importers of record; only indirect economic harm).

On **May 12, 2026**, the U.S. Court of Appeals for the Federal Circuit (CAFC) issued an **administrative stay** of the CIT's injunction, pending the CAFC's consideration of the government's motion for a full stay pending appeal. An expedited briefing schedule is in effect.

### Practical effect on the calculator
- CBP continues to collect 10% Section 122 duties on ALL imports from ALL importers
- The `S122-01` rate entry (0.10) remains correct for current import calculations
- The Section 122 tariffs are still scheduled to expire on **July 24, 2026** regardless of court proceedings (150-day statutory maximum)
- **No rate change needed** until CAFC issues a nationwide order or the government changes the rate

### What was updated
The `notes` field on `S122-01` in `prisma/seed-data/ieepa-tariff-rates.json` was updated to document the May 7 CIT ruling, the limited injunction scope, and the May 12 CAFC stay.
