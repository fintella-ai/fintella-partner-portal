## Detail

### 1. Federal Register 2026-20228 (Oct 5, 2026 pre-publication)

**Title**: "Agency Information Collection Activities; Court-Ordered Refunds Under the International Emergency Economic Powers Act Worksheet"
**URL**: https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/10/05/2026-20228/agency-information-collection-activities-court-ordered-refunds-under-the-international-emergency

**What it is**: A PRA/OMB information collection notice. CBP is finalizing a formal worksheet for importers who need court-ordered IEEPA refunds through channels *other* than CAPE (e.g., entries from excluded types that went through CIT litigation). Purely procedural.

**Calculator impact**: None. This is a CBP administrative form for CIT-litigated refunds, not a change to CAPE eligibility rules or refundable rates. The `litigation` filing path in `checkEligibility()` is unchanged.

### 2. Section 122 Tariffs — Expired July 24, 2026

Section 122 (9903.03.01, 10% global surcharge, Proclamation 11012) expired at its 150-day statutory limit on July 24, 2026. It was replaced by a Section 301 forced-labor tariff at 10%/12.5% depending on country (effective July 25, 2026).

**Calculator impact**: None. The seed data at `prisma/seed-data/ieepa-tariff-rates.json` correctly reflects the Section 122 entry (`id: "S122-01"`) with `endDate: "2026-07-23"` and `supersededBy: null`. The IEEPA refund calculator only covers the IEEPA period (Feb 1, 2025 – Feb 23, 2026); Section 122 and Section 301 are separate programs and are not refundable via CAPE.
