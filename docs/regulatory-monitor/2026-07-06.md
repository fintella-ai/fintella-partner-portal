## Findings

### 1. CRITICAL — Section 122 expires in 18 days (July 24, 2026)

The 10% global surcharge under Section 122 of the Trade Act of 1974 expires at 12:01 AM EDT on **July 24, 2026**, by operation of statute. This is the 150-day maximum allowed without congressional extension. No extension legislation is pending; the statutory clock cannot be stopped by executive action.

### 2. MAJOR — CIT invalidated Section 122 tariffs (May 7, 2026); CAFC stay in place

A 2-1 CIT panel in **Oregon v. United States + Burlap and Barrel, Inc. v. United States** (May 7, 2026) ruled that the President's use of Section 122 did not satisfy the statutory criteria. The court granted summary judgment and issued a permanent injunction against collection from three named plaintiffs only: the State of Washington, Burlap and Barrel, Inc., and Basic Fun, Inc.

- Government appealed to the Federal Circuit on **May 8, 2026**
- CAFC issued an **administrative stay on May 12, 2026** — Section 122 collection continues for all other importers
- CIT denied the government's separate stay motion on May 20, 2026; CAFC administrative stay supersedes
- As of July 6, 2026, the CAFC administrative stay is still in place and the merits briefing is ongoing

**Effect on calculator:** None — Section 122 rates are still being collected from essentially all importers. The `S122-01` rate entry is unchanged. Notes updated.

### 3. ACTIONABLE — CAPE Phase 2 deployed June 29, 2026 (CSMS #69035485)

CBP deployed a Phase 2 expansion of CAPE on June 29, 2026. The Phase 2 scope:

- **New:** Accepts entry types 01, 02, 06 that are **flagged for reconciliation** (i.e., the Type-09 reconciliation entry has NOT yet been filed)
- **Scope limitation:** Only unliquidated entries OR entries liquidated within 80 days — the standard CAPE Phase 1 window
- **Still excluded:** Entries with the Type-09 already on file (reserved for a future phase); type 09 itself; types 08, 23, 47
- **Process:** CAPE removes the IEEPA duties from the flagged entry before the reconciliation entry is filed, separating the IEEPA refund from reconciliation calculations

**Effect on `EXCLUDED_ENTRY_TYPES` in `tariff-calculator.ts`:** The set `["08", "09", "23", "47"]` remains **correct** — Phase 2 accepts types 01/02/06 (already not excluded), not Type 09 itself. However, the comment was outdated and has been updated to describe all phases.

### 4. INFORMATIONAL — CAPE Phase 3 announced, end-of-July 2026

CBP confirmed at a June 9, 2026 CIT hearing that programming for **CAPE Phase 3** will be ready by the end of July 2026. Phase 3 scope:

- **Covers:** Finally-liquidated entries (liquidated >80 days ago, including those past the 180-day protest window)
- **Restriction:** The government's current position is that Phase 3 refunds will be processed **only for importers who have filed suit at the CIT**
- **Controversy:** Government has appealed the Euro-Notions universal refund order to the CAFC, arguing it need not refund finally-liquidated entries to non-CIT filers; approximately 4.36 million entries reportedly failed in Phase 2 reconciliation-flagged processing
- **Effect on calculator:** `excluded_expired` / `filingMethod: "litigation"` remains the correct output for finally-liquidated entries. Phase 3 will provide a CAPE channel for CIT filers, but non-CIT filers still need litigation. The `reason` text in `checkEligibility()` has been updated to mention Phase 3.

### 5. MONITORING — Section 301 investigations to replace Section 122

USTR self-initiated 60 Section 301 investigations on March 12, 2026, targeting 76 tariff determinations across ~60 major trading partners. Hearings are scheduled for **July 7, 2026** (tomorrow). Final determinations are expected before July 24 to provide seamless tariff coverage after Section 122 expires.

- Section 301 has no rate cap, no 150-day expiry, and a legal track record that has survived thousands of court challenges
- No HTS Chapter 99 codes (9903.xx.xx) have been assigned yet
- Expected to roughly match Section 122's ~$60B annual cost

**No code change yet** — rates are not finalized. Will require a new rate database entry once USTR publishes final determinations.
