## Findings

### 1. ⚠️ CAPE Phase 3 — LAUNCHED (October 6, 2026)  
**Actionable: YES — `src/lib/tariff-calculator.ts` updated in this PR**

CBP deployed Phase 3 of the Consolidated Administration and Processing of Entries (CAPE) on **October 6, 2026**.

- **Scope:** Entries that have been liquidated for more than 80 days ("finally liquidated")
- **Mechanism:** CIT-ordered reliquidation (not purely automated like Phase 1); importers submit via CAPE ACE portal but refunds require a pending CIT order
- **Condition:** Phase 3 is linked to litigation — importers must have an active CIT case OR be a certified class member. The importer class was certified on **October 8, 2026** (*Atmus Filtration* class action), covering importers who paid IEEPA duties Feb 1, 2025 – Feb 19, 2026 and have not filed separately.
- **Important caveat:** As of October 19, 2026, CBP has not entered a classwide Phase 3 order for class members who have not filed their own CIT suits. The government's appeal on refunds for non-litigant importers remains pending. Importers with large Phase 3 exposure should still consider individual CIT filings.
- **Type exclusions unchanged:** Entry types 08, 09, 23, 47 remain excluded from CAPE Phase 3.

**Sources:**
- Thompson Hine: https://www.thompsonhinesmartrade.com/2026/06/cbp-announces-phases-2-and-3-of-the-ieepa-tariff-refund-process/
- Thompson Hine (Phase 3 launch confirmed): https://www.thompsonhine.com/insights/cbp-launches-phase-2-of-the-ieepa-tariff-refund-process-and-confirms-development-of-phase-3/
- DWT (class cert + Phase 3 caveat): https://www.dwt.com/insights/2026/10/cit-certifies-ieepa-duty-refund-importer-class
- Customs & International Trade Law Blog: https://customsandinternationaltradelaw.com/2026/10/19/ieepa-class-phase-3-refunds/

**Code change made:**
- Added `"cape_phase3"` to the `FilingMethod` union type
- Updated JSDoc for `FilingMethod`
- Entries liquidated 80–180 days ago: `filingMethod` changed from `"protest"` to `"cape_phase3"`, with `needsReview: true` noting the CIT case/class membership requirement and that formal protest remains available as a fallback
- Entries liquidated > 180 days (protest expired): `filingMethod` changed from `"litigation"` to `"cape_phase3"` with same review note; `status` remains `"excluded_expired"` so routing correctly sends to "Needs Legal Counsel"
