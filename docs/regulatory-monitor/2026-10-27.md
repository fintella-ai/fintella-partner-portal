## 3. Section 301 Forced Labor Tariffs Replaced Section 122 — July 24, 2026 ✅ ACTIONED

**What changed:** Section 122 expired by its 150-day statutory limit on July 24, 2026. USTR issued final Section 301 Forced Labor tariffs effective 12:01 AM ET July 24, replacing it:
- **10% tier (17 economies):** Argentina, Bangladesh, Cambodia, Canada, Ecuador, El Salvador, Guatemala, Honduras, India, Indonesia, Jordan, Malaysia, Mexico, Pakistan, Sri Lanka, Trinidad and Tobago, UK
- **12.5% tier (38+ economies):** China, Vietnam, Brazil, Thailand, Australia, Singapore, Philippines, Israel, Saudi Arabia, and others. EU/Taiwan capped at 10% total; Japan/South Korea/Switzerland capped at 12.5% total (net-of-MFN basis).
- **HTS headings:** 9903.05.20–9903.05.84

**Important:** These are NOT refundable IEEPA tariffs — they are a new, ongoing Section 301 regime. The calculator's refund logic is unaffected. These entries are added to the seed data for informational completeness.

**Litigation:** 25 states filed CIT suit seeking to block the Section 301 Forced Labor tariffs — outcome TBD.

**What changed:** The seed data ended at Q2 2026 (6%). IRS held rates **steady at 7%** for both Q3 2026 (Jul–Sep) and Q4 2026 (Oct–Dec), per IR-2026-98 announced August 21, 2026. This is an increase from Q2 2026's 6%.
