## Key Developments This Period

### 1. Section 122 CIT Ruling + Federal Circuit Stay ⚠️ WATCH

**Case:** *Burlap & Barrel, Inc., Basic Fun, Inc., and State of Washington v. United States*  
**CIT ruling:** May 7, 2026 — 2-1 majority held Section 122 tariffs **unlawful**  
**Basis:** The court rejected the Administration's argument that the U.S. trade deficit satisfies the "balance-of-payments" statutory predicate under 19 U.S.C. § 2132; a long-running trade imbalance is not a "fundamental international payments problem."  
**Injunction scope:** **Plaintiff-specific only.** Permanent injunction prohibited collection from Burlap & Barrel, Basic Fun, and the State of Washington. Nationwide injunction denied — most state co-plaintiffs lacked Article III standing as non-importers of record.

**Federal Circuit stay:** May 12, 2026 — Federal Circuit **immediately stayed** the CIT ruling and injunction pending appeal. Section 122 tariffs are being collected from **all importers** (including the three named plaintiffs) while appeal proceeds. Expedited briefing schedule ordered.

**Impact on calculator:** NONE at this time. Section 122 at 10% through July 24, 2026 remains the operative rule for all importers. Our rate database entry is accurate.

**What to watch:** If the Federal Circuit denies a longer-lasting stay or issues a merits ruling, this could expand relief or trigger legislative action. July 24, 2026 statutory expiration is also approaching — monitor for Congressional extension legislation or Presidential proclamation.
