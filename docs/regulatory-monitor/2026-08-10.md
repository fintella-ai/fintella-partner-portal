## 3. USTR Announcements

### ACTIONABLE: Section 301 Forced-Labor Tariffs (July 24, 2026)

- **Source:** [Baker Donelson alert](https://www.bakerdonelson.com/forced-labor-section-301-tariffs-imposed-to-replace-expiring-section-122-tariffs-what-importers-need-to-know)
- **What changed:** Section 122 tariffs expired at 12:01 AM ET July 24, 2026 (150-day statutory max, no congressional extension). USTR's Section 301 forced-labor investigation determination took effect the same minute — **no gap**.
- **Rate:** Two-tier: **10%** for countries with partial forced-labor protections; **12.5%** for non-compliant economies
- **Coverage:** ~60 economies representing ~99.4% of all US imports
- **HTS codes:** `9903.05.20` through `9903.05.84` (per new U.S. Note 52 to Subchapter III of Chapter 99)
- **Exemptions:** USMCA goods, civil aircraft, pharmaceuticals, semiconductors, and specific HTS line exclusions per Note 52
- **Calculator impact:** NOT an IEEPA tariff. No CAPE refund path for duties collected under Section 301. Documented in seed data as context only. `S122-01.supersededBy` updated to `S301-GLOBAL-01`.

### Canada Section 338 Tariffs (50%, effective August 19, 2026)

- **Source:** [Crane Worldwide advisory](https://www.craneww.com/knowledge-center/trade-advisory-notices/trade-advisory-new-section-338-tariffs-add-50-duties-on-certain-canadian-imports-effective-august-19-2026/), [White & Case alert](https://www.whitecase.com/insight-alert/trump-administration-imposes-50-tariffs-certain-canadian-products-first-use-section)
- **What changed:** President Trump signed three proclamations July 20, 2026, invoking Section 338 of the Tariff Act of 1930 (19 USC 1338) — **first modern use of this authority**. 50% tariff on specific Canadian goods effective August 19, 2026.
- **New HTS codes in 9903.03.xx space:**
  - `9903.03.12` — Alcoholic beverages (beer 2203, wine 2204, spirits 2208, vermouth 2205, other fermented beverages 2206) + certain wood + hockey articles
  - `9903.03.13` — Dairy products (milk, cream, butter, cheese, yogurt — selected Chapter 4 lines)
  - `9903.03.14` — Motor vehicles, cement, furniture, clothing, seeds, fishing rods, wigs, other consumer goods (hundreds of HTS lines)
- **USMCA note:** Provides **no relief** — applies regardless of USMCA preference status
- **Scope:** ~$20B in annual Canadian imports (~5% of all Canadian imports)
- **Effective:** August 19, 2026 at 12:01 AM ET — **9 days from this report**
- **Calculator impact:** NOT IEEPA. No CAPE refund path. Added to seed data as context. New codes added to `htsCodeReference.section338Codes`.
