### 2. CAPE Phase 3 (Finally Liquidated Entries) — Launched Late July 2026

CBP's CAPE Phase 3 opened access to IEEPA duty refunds for entries whose liquidation became **final** (past the 180-day protest window). This is approximately $11.4 billion in IEEPA duties.

- **CIT Order:** July 15, 2026 — CIT Judge Eaton issued individual refund orders. DOJ will not voluntarily reliquidate without a court order; Phase 3 payments therefore flow **only** to importers who filed individual CIT protective actions.
- **Phase 3 launch:** Late July 2026 (CBP ACE deployment).
- **Key constraint:** Importers without a pending CIT case have **no confirmed mechanism** for finally-liquidated-entry refunds. They must file a CIT protective action to access Phase 3.
- **Appeal risk:** Government appealed to the Federal Circuit. Phase 3 refunds **may be subject to clawback** if the Federal Circuit reverses. Counsel should advise clients accordingly.

### 3. Section 122 Tariff Expired July 24, 2026 — Data Record Closed

The 10% Section 122 global surcharge expired at midnight July 24, 2026 (last effective day: July 23). Its 150-day statutory maximum ran from Feb 24 to July 24, 2026.

- **Replaced by:** USTR Section 301 (forced-labor) tariffs at 10%/12.5% on ~60 countries, effective July 24, 2026. These are NOT IEEPA duties and are NOT refundable via CAPE.
- **CIT invalidation (context):** CIT struck down Section 122 on May 7, 2026 (2-1 ruling, Burlap & Barrel et al.). Federal Circuit stayed the ruling May 12, 2026. Tariff remained in effect until its natural expiration — the CIT ruling was ultimately moot as the tariff expired before the appeal resolved.
