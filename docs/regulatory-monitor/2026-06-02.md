## Findings

### 1. IRS Q3 2026 Interest Rate — ACTIONABLE ✅

**Source**: IRS Internal Revenue Bulletin 2026-22 (published late May 2026), Revenue Ruling 2026-22  
**URL**: https://www.irs.gov/irb/2026-22_IRB

The IRS published Q3 2026 (July 1 – September 30, 2026) overpayment interest rates. The rate **increased from 6% back to 7%** for non-corporate taxpayers.

| Quarter | Non-corporate overpayment | Corporate overpayment | Fed short-term |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q1 2026 | 7% | 6% | 4% |
| Q2 2026 | 6% | 5% | 3% |
| Q3 2026 (new) | **7%** | **6%** | **4%** |

**Why this matters**: CAPE refunds issued in July–September 2026 accrue interest through the refund date. Any importer whose CAPE declaration is accepted in Q2 but refunded in Q3 will accrue at 7% for the Q3 days — the higher rate benefits claimants. Without this entry, the calculator's `calculateInterest()` function has no rate to apply for days after June 30, 2026, producing an understated interest estimate for any refund projected past that date.

**Action taken**: Added Q3 2026 entry to `irsInterestRates.quarters` in `prisma/seed-data/ieepa-tariff-rates.json`. The seeder upserts by `quarter` key, so the next build/seed run will insert this row into the `InterestRate` table.

### 2. Section 122 CIT Ruling + CAFC Stay — INFORMATIONAL ONLY

**Source**: Multiple law firm alerts  
**URLs**:
- https://www.skadden.com/insights/publications/2026/05/us-trade-court-strikes-down-section-122-tariffs
- https://www.nortonrosefulbright.com/en/knowledge/publications/199bf568/cit-rules-section-122-tariffs-invalid-limited-injunction-and-likely-appeal
- https://www.troutman.com/insights/federal-circuit-hits-pause-on-cits-section-122-tariff-ruling/

**Timeline**:
- **May 7, 2026**: CIT 3-judge panel (2-1) ruled Section 122 tariffs unlawful — conditions for invoking §122 not satisfied. Permanent injunction issued for 3 named plaintiffs only (Burlap & Barrel, Basic Fun, State of WA).
- **May 8, 2026**: Government appealed to Federal Circuit (CAFC).
- **May 12, 2026**: CAFC issued administrative stay of the CIT injunction pending appeal.

**Net effect on our calculator**: None. Section 122 tariffs (10%, 9903.03.01) remain in force for all importers except the 3 named plaintiffs while the CAFC appeal proceeds. The July 24, 2026 statutory expiration date is unchanged. Section 122 tariffs are not IEEPA duties and are not refundable via CAPE — they are the replacement tariff post-SCOTUS termination. Our `S122-01` rate entry (10% through Jul 23, 2026) remains accurate.

**Action taken**: Updated `S122-01` notes field to document the ruling and stay for reference.
