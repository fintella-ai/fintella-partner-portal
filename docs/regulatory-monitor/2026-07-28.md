## 2. Section 301 Forced Labor Tariffs — NEW, Effective July 24, 2026 ⚠️ SIGNIFICANT

**Status:** New tariff regime took effect at 12:01 AM EDT July 24, 2026.

USTR imposed Section 301 tariffs under the Trade Act of 1974 §301 based on findings that 60 economies failed to prohibit or enforce bans on imports made with forced labor (investigations docketed USTR-2026-0265 and USTR-2026-0266; initiated March 12, 2026; final FR notice July 23, 2026).

**Rate tiers:**
| Rate | Economies |
|------|-----------|
| 10% | ~14 economies with trade agreement forced labor commitments: Argentina, Bangladesh, Cambodia, El Salvador, Guatemala, Malaysia, Taiwan, UK + ~6 with partial enforcement |
| 12.5% | Remaining ~46 economies including China, EU, Japan, South Korea, Vietnam, Canada, Mexico, India, Switzerland |

**HTS implementation:**
- Duties: `9903.05.20` through `9903.05.84` (per-country headings)
- Exemptions: `9903.05.85`–`9903.05.99` and `9903.06.01`–`9903.06.21` (product-specific exemptions for 13 economies)

**Exemptions (similar structure to Section 122):**
- USMCA-compliant goods from Canada and Mexico
- CAFTA-DR textile and apparel
- Section 232 articles
- Civil aircraft, pharmaceutical articles, informational materials, donations

**CBP filing guidance:** CSMS #69326983 (July 24, 2026)

**Critical note for the TIE calculator:** These are Section 301 tariffs under a separate legal authority. They are **NOT IEEPA tariffs** and are **NOT refundable via CAPE**. The TIE calculator eligibility logic is unchanged — `IEEPA_END_DATE` remains February 23, 2026. Section 301 duties paid after July 24, 2026 are not within the calculator's scope.

## 3. CAPE Phase 3 — Launching Late July 2026 ⚠️ ELIGIBILITY LOGIC CHANGE

**Status:** Phase 3 confirmed on track for late July 2026; CIT order issued July 17, 2026.

CAPE Phase 3 covers **finally liquidated entries** — entries that were liquidated more than 80 days before the CAPE declaration period, previously beyond the automated CAPE Phase 1 window and potentially beyond the 180-day protest deadline.

**CIT Order (July 17, 2026):** Senior Judge Richard K. Eaton directed CBP to "reliquidate, without regard to IEEPA duties, any and all of Plaintiffs' entries that have been liquidated for more than 80 days and on which Plaintiffs made estimated deposits pursuant to IEEPA" — applying to ~3,700 IEEPA cases currently before the court.

**Critical eligibility restriction:** The government's stated position is that Phase 3 refunds process **only for importers who filed a protective action at the CIT**. Importers who have not filed suit will not receive Phase 3 refunds through CAPE (government is appealing universal-refund orders to the Federal Circuit; Federal Circuit appeal is pending).

**Impact on calculator's `checkEligibility()` function:**
- Before Phase 3: entries liquidated >180 days ago → `excluded_expired` / `filingMethod: "litigation"` — end of road
- With Phase 3: same entries may be eligible for CAPE refund **if** the importer filed at CIT
