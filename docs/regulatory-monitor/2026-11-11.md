## Detailed findings

### 1. IEEPA — terminated, no new activity
All IEEPA tariffs remain terminated. The Supreme Court's 6–3 ruling in *Learning Resources, Inc. v. Trump* (Feb 20, 2026) held IEEPA does not authorize tariffs. EO 14389 ended collection effective Feb 24, 2026. No new IEEPA-related executive order, proclamation, or agency action was found in the last 24 hours.

**Source:** [Ending Certain Tariff Actions (EO 14389)](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2026/02/ending-certain-tariff-actions/)

### 2. China EO 14358 suspension — expired November 10, 2026 (moot)
EO 14358 (Nov 2025) had suspended heightened China reciprocal IEEPA tariffs until 12:01 a.m. EST November 10, 2026. That date has now passed. No U.S. White House or Federal Register notice extending this suspension was found.

**However, this is moot for the IEEPA refund calculator:** all IEEPA tariffs (including the China reciprocal rates this EO covered) were already terminated in February 2026 by the SCOTUS ruling and EO 14389. The expiry of EO 14358 has no effect on IEEPA refund eligibility or the rate database.

China extended its own side of the truce (suspension of its 24% tariff on US goods, plus port fees) for two months to January 10, 2027, citing the "Washington heads-of-state meeting." This is China's domestic action, not a U.S. tariff change.

**Sources:**
- [China extends US trade truce 2 months (Reuters/AsiaOne)](https://www.asiaone.com/china/china-extends-us-trade-truce-2-months)
- [China Extends 24% Tariff Suspension](https://bworldonline.com/world/2026/11/09/792445/china-extends-suspensions-of-24-tariff-on-us-goods-port-fees-on-us-ships-for-two-months/)

### 3. USTR Section 301 — two routine notices (Nov 6, 2026)

  - **Source:** [Federal Register notice Nov 10, 2026](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/11/10/2026-22925/notice-of-product-exclusion-extensions-chinas-acts-policies-and-practices-related-to-technology)

- **Maritime/Shipbuilding Section 301 suspension extended 2 months:** Routine extension; unrelated to IEEPA.
  - **Source:** [USTR Section 301 Maritime/Shipbuilding page](https://ustr.gov/trade-topics/enforcement/section-301-investigations/section-301-china-targeting-maritime-logistics-and-shipbuilding-sectors-dominance)

These are **not IEEPA tariffs** and are outside the scope of the IEEPA refund calculator's seed data. The Section 122 `endDate: "2026-07-23"` in the seed file is accurate.
