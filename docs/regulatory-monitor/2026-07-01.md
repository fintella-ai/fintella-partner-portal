## 1. CAPE Phase 2 — LIVE (June 29, 2026) ✅ UPDATED

**Source:** CBP CSMS #69035485; [CBP IEEPA Duty Refunds page](https://www.cbp.gov/trade/programs-administration/trade-remedies/ieepa-duty-refunds); [Diaz Trade Law](https://diaztradelaw.com/cape-ieepa-duty-refunds/); [Thompson Hine SmarTrade](https://www.thompsonhinesmartrade.com/2026/06/cbp-announces-phases-2-and-3-of-the-ieepa-tariff-refund-process/)

**What changed:** Effective June 29, 2026, CAPE now accepts entries flagged for reconciliation (entry types 01, 02, 06) where the reconciliation summary (Type 09) **has not yet been filed**. Phase 1 (launched April 20, 2026) had previously required entries to NOT be flagged for reconciliation.

**Scope:** ~2.8 million additional entries; ~$28.7 billion in additional potential refunds.

**Key mechanics:**
- Same 80-day liquidation window as Phase 1
- CAPE removes IEEPA duties from the flagged entries *prior* to the Type 09 filing, so IEEPA duty amounts are cleanly separated from reconciliation calculations
- Type 09 (reconciliation summary entry itself) remains excluded from CAPE declarations
- Type 47 (drawback) and Type 08/23 remain excluded from all CAPE phases

**Code changes made:**
- `prisma/seed-data/ieepa-tariff-rates.json`: Updated `capePhase1.description` to reference all three phases; updated Type 09 entry-type notes; updated `eligibilityCriteria.additionalRequirements` to reflect Phase 2 lifting the reconciliation flag restriction with CSMS citation
- `src/lib/tariff-calculator.ts`: Updated `EXCLUDED_ENTRY_TYPES` comment to explain the Phase 2 distinction between the Type 09 entry itself (still excluded) vs. entries merely flagged for reconciliation (now eligible); updated `CAPE_PHASE1_LIQUIDATION_WINDOW_DAYS` comment; updated reason string in `checkEligibility()` from "Phase 1" to "all phases"

## 2. CAPE Phase 3 — Expected End of July 2026 ⚠️ MONITOR

**Source:** [Green Worldwide Shipping](https://www.greenworldwide.com/cape-phase-3-for-ieepa-tariff-refunds-on-track-for-end-of-july-as-federal-circuit-appeal-continues/); [Holland & Knight](https://www.hklaw.com/en/insights/publications/2026/06/ieepa-tariff-refund-update-government-appeals)

**What:** Phase 3 will cover **finally liquidated entries** (liquidated >180 days ago, protest window closed). Technical deployment is on track for end of July 2026.

**Critical legal caveat:** The DOJ argues CBP cannot issue refunds on finally liquidated entries without an individual CIT lawsuit from each importer. The government's position: Phase 3 refunds will only be processed for importers who have **already filed lawsuits at the CIT**. This is being litigated at the Federal Circuit (appeal of Judge Eaton's universal reliquidation order).

**Impact on calculator:** The current `filingMethod: "litigation"` classification for entries liquidated >180 days ago (line 325–330 of `tariff-calculator.ts`) will eventually need a `cape_phase3` filing method once Phase 3 launches with legal clarity. **No code change yet** — waiting for actual Phase 3 deployment and resolution of the Federal Circuit appeal.

**Watch for:** CSMS message announcing Phase 3 launch date; Federal Circuit ruling on government's appeal of Judge Eaton's order.

## 3. Section 122 Tariff — CIT Ruling (May 7) + Federal Circuit Stay (June 11) ⚠️ MONITOR

**Source:** [Skadden](https://www.skadden.com/insights/publications/2026/05/us-trade-court-strikes-down-section-122-tariffs); [Subject to Inquiry (Federal Circuit stay)](https://www.subjecttoinquiry.com/2026/06/federal-circuit-stays-injunctions-against-section-122-balance-of-payments-tariffs-pending-appeal/); [Ward and Smith](https://www.wardandsmith.com/article/court-of-international-trade-rejects-10-section-122-tariff-what-businesses-should-know-while-the-appeal-proceeds)

**CIT ruling (May 7, 2026):** 2-1 decision in *State of Oregon v. Trump* (No. 26-cv-01472) and *Burlap and Barrel, Inc. v. Trump* (No. 26-cv-01606), held that the 10% Section 122 surcharge (Proclamation 11012) exceeds the President's statutory authority. Declared Proclamation "invalid as contrary to law."

**Federal Circuit stay (June 11, 2026):** Government's motion for stay pending appeal GRANTED. Section 122 tariffs are **still being collected** while the appeal proceeds on the merits.

**Impact on calculator:** Section 122 (rate entry `S122-01`) remains active at 10% for refund calculation purposes. No rate change.

**Code changes made:**
- `prisma/seed-data/ieepa-tariff-rates.json`: Updated `S122-01.notes` to document the CIT ruling, Federal Circuit stay, approaching expiration (July 24), and Section 301 replacement plans.

## 4. Section 122 Expiration July 24, 2026 — Section 301 Replacement Racing ⚠️ URGENT

**Source:** [The Conveyor](https://www.theconveyor.co/p/section-122-tariff-expires-section-301-replacement); [Dorsey](https://www.dorsey.com/newsresources/publications/client-alerts/2026/6/new-section-301-tariffs); [NTMA](https://ntma.org/section-301-tariff-changes-coming/)

**What:** Section 122 has a hard 150-day statutory cap. It expires July 24, 2026. The President **cannot extend it**; Congressional extension is considered unlikely.

**Section 301 replacement timeline:**
- USTR initiated Section 301 investigations March 11, 2026 (targeting 60+ economies for forced-labor enforcement; 16 economies for excess manufacturing capacity)
- Public comment period closed July 6, 2026
- Public hearing: July 7, 2026
- Proposed rates: **10%** for 15 trading partners; **12.5%** for 45 trading partners
- HTS Chapter 99 codes not yet announced (likely 9903.04.xx)
- Administration is aiming to finalize and publish before July 24 — but timing is uncertain

**Key difference:** Section 301 has **no statutory rate cap** and **no time limit** — unlike Section 122's 15%/150-day constraints.

**Action required:** If Section 301 duties are finalized before July 24, a new rate entry must be added to `prisma/seed-data/ieepa-tariff-rates.json` (with `rateType: "section301"`, country codes, rates, effective date, and HTS codes). Also: the `lookupCombinedRate()` and `lookupCombinedRate()` breakdown object will need a `section301` key.

**Watch for:** White House proclamation or USTR Federal Register notice announcing final Section 301 rates and effective date. Monitor federalregister.gov daily through July 24.

**No code change yet** — rates not finalized.
