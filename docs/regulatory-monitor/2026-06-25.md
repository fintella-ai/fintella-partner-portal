### 2. Section 122 Tariff — CIT Invalidated, Federal Circuit Stayed; Expiry July 24 — DATA UPDATED ✅

**CIT ruling:** State of Oregon v. Trump, Slip Op. 26-47 (Nos. 26-01472 & 26-01606, May 7, 2026)  
**URL:** https://www.cit.uscourts.gov/sites/cit/files/26-47.pdf  
**Fed Circuit stay:** Nos. 2026-1804 & 2026-1805 (June 11, 2026)  
**URL:** https://www.subjecttoinquiry.com/2026/06/federal-circuit-stays-injunctions-against-section-122-balance-of-payments-tariffs-pending-appeal/

The CIT struck down Proclamation 11012 (Section 122 10% global surcharge) in a 2–1 split opinion, holding that the Administration did not invoke one of the three recognized balance-of-payments deficit measures (liquidity, official settlements, or basic balance). A permanent injunction was entered against three plaintiff importers (State of Oregon/Washington, Burlap and Barrel Inc., Basic Fun Inc.).

**Federal Circuit stayed the injunction on June 11, 2026.** CBP continues collecting 10% from all importers including the original plaintiffs while the appeal proceeds.

**Statutory expiry:** Section 122 tariffs expire July 24, 2026 (150-day hard statutory limit under 19 U.S.C. §2132 — no presidential extension). As of June 25, 2026, no congressional extension bill has been introduced.

**What may replace it:** USTR announced Section 301 investigations in March 2026 against 60 economies for forced-labor trade failures. Hearings are scheduled July 7, 2026. If finalized before July 24, new Section 301 tariffs could partially replace Section 122 coverage. The U.S.-China Board of Trade mechanism (USTR public comment period opened June 2026) may also produce tariff modifications for China-origin goods.

**Calculator impact:** Section 122 tariffs run Feb 24 – Jul 24, 2026, which is AFTER the IEEPA period (Feb 4, 2025 – Feb 23, 2026). `checkEligibility()` already excludes these entries via the date range check. No eligibility logic change needed.

**Data updated:** `S122-01.notes` in `ieepa-tariff-rates.json` updated to include CIT ruling, Federal Circuit stay, and approaching expiry status.
