# Competitive Intelligence Report — 2026-05-06

## Summary

The IEEPA tariff refund calculator space has exploded since the February 20, 2026 Supreme Court ruling in *Learning Resources Inc. v. Trump* (striking down all IEEPA tariffs) and CBP's April 20, 2026 launch of the CAPE refund portal. At least **10 net-new entrants** were identified this scan, bringing the total tracked competitor count to 19+. Most tools remain in the "estimation only" tier — uploading an ES-003 or entering an import value and producing a dollar estimate. **No competitor has shipped TMS embedding, multi-client dashboards, or commission/referral tracking.** One new entrant (CustomsGenius) now has CAPE CSV generation with AI, making the pre-validation story Fintella's sharpest remaining differentiator on that feature.

## Changes Detected

### 1. Allied CHB — https://www.alliedchb.com/refunds
**Status: Active | No major changes detected**

- **Core feature**: Drag-and-drop CBP 7501 PDF upload (remains gold standard for PDF-first workflow).
- **No signup required**; free calculator.
- Parses all IEEPA duty types: China/HK, reciprocal, tiered, country-specific.
- Detailed breakdown per entry including total IEEPA duties paid + estimated refund.
- Data is never stored or transmitted — processing is implied server-side but data is discarded.
- Licensed US customs brokers offered to handle the full Post-Summary Correction (PSC) process.
- **Notable limitation**: 9903.01.34 (US-originating content, 20% exemption rule) flagged as non-refund-eligible.
- **CAPE CSV generation**: Not detected. Allied CHB routes to broker-managed CAPE filing, not self-serve CSV export.
- **Positioning**: Importer-facing, professional broker backing. "We handle it for you."

### 2. Flexport — https://tariffs.flexport.com/refunds
**Status: Active | Significant feature launches in Jan–Feb 2026**

- **Jan 2026**: Launched Tariff Refund Calculator (upload CBP 2025 Entry Report → estimate refund).
- **Feb 2026**: Launched "Technology to Automate Tariff Refunds" — AI agents to audit, file corrections, and process claims end-to-end.
- **AI Auditor**: Audits all past customs entries for compliance errors; can file corrections independently.
- **Bulk upload**: Yes, via entry data upload (Flexport's own report format, not ES-003 native).
- **Account required**: Free to access at tariffs.flexport.com/refunds — no login required for basic calculator. Full automation pipeline likely requires a Flexport account.
- **CAPE filing**: Flexport manages CAPE submissions for customers.
- **New (Feb 2026)**: Flexport Atlas data foundation; AI container optimization agent; real-time AI language translator launched alongside refund automation.
- **Positioning**: "Audit your customs broker" — aggressive positioning against incumbent brokers. Tech-forward, AI-first, enterprise and SMB.

### 3. UNIS / UNISCO — https://www.unisco.com/ieepa-refund-calculator
**Status: Active | Also available via ITEM marketplace**

- **ACE ES-003 CSV/TXT upload** — primary input format.
- **Client-side processing**: Explicitly confirmed — "no data is transmitted to any server."
- **Tariff classification**: Auto-classifies all lines into IEEPA (9903.01/02), Section 301 (9903.88), Section 232 (9903.80/85), Section 122 (9903.03). Filter views for each.
- **Deadline tracking**: 180-day protest deadline calculated per liquidated entry; urgent cases highlighted. Open (unliquidated) entries tracked separately.
- **"Load Sample Data" button**: Demo without uploading real data.
- **No signup required**; free.
- **CAPE CSV generation**: Not detected — routes to UNISCO brokerage team for filing.
- **New channel**: Tool is now also available via ITEM marketplace (marketplace.item.com) — distribution expansion.
- **Positioning**: Broker lead-gen funnel. "Our team reviews results and files protests before deadlines."

### 4. The Trade Lab — https://thetradelab.ai/refund-calculator
**Status: Active | AI platform context growing**

- **Inputs**: Drag-and-drop ES-003 CSV or Excel (.xlsx).
- **Processing**: Identifies all IEEPA tariff lines by Chapter 99 codes; calculates refund per line.
- **AI features**: Broader platform uses AI for HTS classification, landed cost modeling. Whether AI applies to the refund calculator specifically is unconfirmed.
- **Freemium**: Refund calculator is free with no signup; full AI platform has optional paid tier.
- **Deadline**: 180-day protest window referenced; broker recommended for formal filing.
- **CAPE CSV**: Not detected.
- **Positioning**: AI-forward customs platform. Calculator is a free entry point to paid AI subscription.

### 5. TariffClaim.ai — https://tariffclaim.ai/refund-calculator
**Status: Active | App platform detected**

- **Input**: ACE ES-003 upload.
- **Free, no signup** for the calculator.
- **App platform**: app.tariffclaim.ai exists — suggesting account-based features beyond the free calculator.
- **Notable feature**: Estimates claim value to potential investors — possible factoring/financing angle for large refund claims.
- **CAPE CSV**: Not confirmed.
- **Positioning**: Dual-audience — importers who want to self-estimate AND investors interested in acquiring refund claims. This financing angle is unique among all monitored competitors.

### 6. TariffRefundIQ — https://www.tariffrefundiq.com
**Status: Active | Content marketing expanding**

- **Core feature**: Probability-weighted NPV model with 7 scenarios (recovery rates, timing, discount rates).
- **Client-side**: All calculations run in browser.
- **Hold-vs-sell recommendation**: Computes a breakeven offer price — if a third-party offer exceeds expected value, sell; if below, hold. Unique feature not found elsewhere.
- **Content marketing**: ACE ACH setup guide published at /blog/ace-ach-setup-guide-ieepa-refund — practical CAPE prep content.
- **No signup required**; free.
- **Positioning**: Finance/CFO audience. "What is my refund claim worth in present-value terms?" Distinct from pure eligibility calculators.

### 7. TADA — https://mytada.com
**Status: Active | Enterprise positioning maintained**

- **Free tools**: Tariff Estimator (includes IEEPA recovery modeling) + HTS Lookup.
- **Enterprise platform**: SKU-level tariff risk, landed cost modeling, supplier-swap scenario planning, AI-Enabled Digital Twin.
- **IEEPA recovery**: Rate-updated for Feb 20, 2026 SCOTUS ruling and Feb 24, 2026 Section 122 surcharge.
- **195+ countries** supported.
- **AI features**: Explicitly marketed — "AI-Enabled Digital Twin," AI-powered prescriptive analytics, AI Part Master.
- **No CAPE/ES-003 tools detected**.
- **Pricing**: Free quick tools; enterprise platform is demo-only/sales-led.
- **Positioning**: Complex enterprises needing end-to-end supply chain intelligence, not just a refund calculator.

### 8. SimplyDuty — https://www.simplyduty.com/import-calculator/
**Status: Active but NOT a direct IEEPA refund competitor**

- General import duty and tax calculator (HS code + origin + destination + value → duty estimate).
- **No IEEPA refund calculator, no CAPE filing, no ES-003 analysis**.
- Data freshness issue noted: rates appeared to be from January 2026 (pre-SCOTUS ruling) per third-party reviews, with Section 232 rates showing 25% instead of current 50%.
- 100+ countries. 5 free calculations/day. Paid subscription and API available.
- **Verdict**: Not a meaningful IEEPA refund competitor — different market segment (general landed cost estimation).

### 9. Descartes CustomsInfo — https://www.descartes.com/solutions/global-trade-intelligence/product-classification-and-duty-determination
**Status: Active but NOT a direct IEEPA refund competitor**

- Enterprise product classification database — 6 million+ reference documents, cross-referenced duty rates, customs rulings, WCO materials.
- Professional classification services (human experts + technology). AI-assisted HTS classification is a named feature.
- **No IEEPA refund calculator, no CAPE filing tool detected**.
- **Verdict**: Different market segment — enterprise compliance and classification, not a refund estimation tool.

## New Entrants

Ten verifiable new entrants were identified this scan:

### NE-1. TariffsTool.com — https://www.tariffstool.com
- **Tool**: IEEPA Refund Estimator + US Tariff Rate Calculator.
- **Method**: Select country of origin + enter total IEEPA-period import value → estimated refund with statutory interest (30-second result).
- **Also has**: Country-specific tariff guides (China, India, Thailand, etc.); "Tariffs Today" summary; full tariff calculator.
- **Accuracy caveat**: Rough estimate — multiplies import value by IEEPA rate for country + ~5% interest. Real refund requires per-entry 7501 data.
- **Free, no signup**. Content-marketing heavy (multiple SEO guides).
- **Positioned**: Consumer/importer education and rough ballpark — not a professional filing tool.
- **Featured in**: Fortune (May 5, 2026) article on tariff refunds.

### NE-2. ClaimTariffRefund.com — https://www.claimtariffrefund.com
- **Tool**: Free refund calculator + step-by-step CAPE filing guide.
- **Method**: Country + import period + total import value → estimated refund.
- **Also covers**: Full CAPE action plan — ACE account setup, ES-003 data pulls, Post-Summary Corrections, protective CIT filings.
- **Free, no signup**.
- **Positioned**: Education-first, broadest-audience tool. Targets importers who don't yet know what CAPE is.

### NE-3. CAS — https://www.cas-ieepa-tariffs.com/calculator
- **Tool**: HTS-code-level IEEPA refund estimator.
- **Method**: Enter HTS code + country of origin + customs value → instant estimate.
- **Covers**: EOs 14193, 14194, 14195, 14257 (the four IEEPA executive orders).
- **Free, no signup apparent**.
- **Positioned**: Quick spot-check for a specific product/shipment. Less useful for large import portfolios.

### NE-4. TariffRefundsCalculator.com — https://tariffrefundscalculator.com/ieepa-refund-calculator
- **Tool**: Broker/ACE CSV triage and CAPE prep tool.
- **Method**: Upload ACE ES-003 or broker CSV export → estimates IEEPA refund exposure + flags missing CAPE-prep fields.
- **Unique feature**: Generates CAPE Declaration CSV template structure. Validates required fields (entry number, date, importer name, IEEPA duty amount, liquidation status).
- **Workflow**: Routes output to broker, counsel, or paid claim-prep services.
- **Positioned**: Professional broker/compliance audience. Triage before engaging counsel. Appears to have a commercial routing component.

### NE-5. CustomsGenius — https://www.customsgenius.com
- **Who**: Automated IEEPA refund recovery platform. Most technically sophisticated new entrant found.
- **AI/ML**: "Neurosymbolic AI" — cross-verifies extracted data across entry summaries, commercial invoices, and HTS codes.
- **Key features**: Automated ES-003 analysis; eligibility identification across HTS codes; **CAPE Declaration CSV generation**; per-line-item analysis reports; analyzes 12 months of import activity in under 4 minutes.
- **Pricing**: Usage-based — first 500 lines free; $0.18/line for analysis reports; $0.25/line for CAPE filing CSV; volume discounts; no platform fee.
- **Target audience**: Customs brokers and importers of record; trade professionals.
- **Launch signals**: March 2026 — operational before CAPE portal launched.
- **⚠️ Threat level: HIGH** — This is the only competitor with confirmed CAPE CSV generation AND AI-powered analysis. Usage-based pricing will appeal to brokers processing many clients. Directly overlaps with Fintella's CAPE CSV generation feature.

### NE-6. Wove — https://tariffs.wove.com/us/refunds
- **Built**: Within 72 hours of the Feb 20, 2026 SCOTUS ruling — one of the earliest entrants.
- **Features**: Spreadsheet upload (HTS codes, country of origin, customs values, entry dates); entry-level refund breakdown; multi-path recovery routing (PSC vs. Protest vs. CIT); **Tariff Calculator API for brokers and developers**.
- **API tier**: Confirmed — brokers and developers can embed or integrate Wove's calculation engine.
- **Pricing**: Free base tool; API pricing not confirmed.
- **AI**: No — rules-based engine against IEEPA tariff schedule.
- **Positioned**: Importers + brokers; developer/API tier available.
- **⚠️ Note**: The API-for-brokers angle is a potential attack on Fintella's TMS embedding differentiation.

### NE-7. ConsumerShield — https://www.consumershield.com/business-law/tariff/calculator
- **Who**: Legal consumer advocacy platform that added a tariff section post-SCOTUS.
- **Features**: Free IEEPA rebate calculator, eligibility checker, how-to-file guide.
- **Model**: Free calculator; attorney referral monetization on the backend.
- **Launched**: February–March 2026.
- **Positioned**: Businesses and consumers (not trade professionals). Legal lead-gen play.

### NE-8. Southern Star Navigation — https://southernstarnavigation.com/ieepa-tariff-refund-resources/ieepa-tariff-refund-deadline-calculator/
- **Tool**: Deadline calculator (not dollar estimator) — enter entry release dates, get PSC cutoff, expected liquidation date, protest window deadline, final liquidation date.
- **Capacity**: Up to 50 entries; PDF export.
- **Pricing**: Free.
- **Who**: Logistics firm with 30+ years at Port of Charleston.
- **Niche**: Pure deadline/timeline planning tool. Fills a gap none of the main calculators address explicitly.

### NE-9. ITEM Marketplace — https://marketplace.item.com/ieepa-refund-calculator/
- **Note**: Distinct from UNIS even though the tool is co-marketed. ITEM is an established customs/trade technology marketplace.
- **Features**: ACE ES-003 upload, local browser-side processing (data never uploaded), IEEPA vs. Section 301 classification, protest deadline tracking. Broker handoff via "JFS CHB" partner.
- **Data privacy**: Explicitly local — differentiator for compliance-sensitive importers.
- **Pricing**: Free; JFS CHB broker services on upsell.

### NE-10. Dimerco — https://ieepa.dimerco.com:39443
- **Who**: Major Asia-Pacific freight forwarder/3PL (Dimerco Express Group).
- **Tool**: IEEPA Tariff Refund Calculator accessible at a non-standard port (:39443) — likely an internal/client tool exposed publicly.

## Recommended Actions

### 1. URGENT — Publish CAPE CSV generation feature prominently and add pre-validation as a named differentiator
CustomsGenius (NE-5) launched with CAPE CSV generation in March 2026 and is charging $0.25/line for it. Fintella has this built in. **Recommended**: Surface the CAPE CSV feature explicitly on the TIE landing page with a bulleted list of the pre-validation rules (entry number CBP mod-10 check, batch size ≤9,999 enforcement, liquidation status flags, importer match check). The validation story is the differentiator — CustomsGenius has the feature, but Fintella is the only tool that validates before submitting. This directly competes with an AI-backed entrant that has a usage-based pricing model.

### 2. Counter TariffClaim.ai's investor-financing angle with the Fintella referral pipeline
TariffClaim.ai has identified a "claim value to investors" angle — likely targeting large importers who want cash now rather than wait 60–90 days. Fintella's commission/referral pipeline (Frost Law as the spoke) is structurally better positioned to deliver a full-service path: calculate → file → collect. **Recommended**: Partner-facing messaging should explicitly frame "don't sell your claim at a discount to an investor — route it through Fintella and capture the full refund" as a value proposition.

### 3. Add Section 301/232/122 classification to the ES-003 analysis
UNIS, ITEM Marketplace, and (partially) The Trade Lab all classify entries beyond IEEPA into Section 301, Section 232, and Section 122. Brokers need the full picture when advising clients — CAPE Phase 1 only covers IEEPA, but clients will ask about 301. **Recommended**: Add classification flags for 9903.88 (301), 9903.80/85 (232), and 9903.03 (122) to the ES-003 analysis output, labeled "not refundable under CAPE Phase 1." This closes UNIS's most visible differentiator and adds broker advisory value.

### 4. Monitor Wove's API tier
Wove built a Tariff Calculator API for brokers and developers within 72 hours of the SCOTUS ruling. If they gain TMS integration traction, this becomes a direct threat to Fintella's TMS embedding advantage. **Recommended**: Keep an eye on Wove's API documentation and any announcements of CargoWise or Magaya partnerships in the next 30 days.

## Market Context

- **CAPE Phase 1 went live April 20, 2026** — only covers unliquidated entries + entries within 80 days of liquidation. Phase 2 (liquidated entries via protest) is not yet scheduled.
- **CBP timeline**: Refunds expected 60–90 days after CAPE Declaration acceptance. First wave confirmed for May 12, 2026 (per CBP announcement, The Hill).
- **~$166–175B total refund pool** across 330,000+ importers and 53M shipments — not automatic, every dollar requires a CAPE filing.
- **Supreme Court ruling**: *Learning Resources Inc. v. Trump*, February 20, 2026, 6–3, all IEEPA tariffs (China, Canada, Mexico, reciprocal/Liberation Day) declared unconstitutional.
- **Section 122** (10% temporary import surcharge, effective Feb 24, 2026) replaced IEEPA tariffs and is NOT refundable — important distinction many tools gloss over.
