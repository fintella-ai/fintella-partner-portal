# Competitive Intelligence Report — 2026-08-26

> **Delta scan**: This report builds on [2026-05-06](./2026-05-06.md). It focuses on changes since that scan, new entrants, and shifts in market positioning as the CAPE refund program moves from filing into disbursement.

---

## Summary

The IEEPA tariff refund market has materially shifted since the May 6 scan: **~60% of the $175B pool has already been disbursed** (~$100B paid as of August 4), CAPE Phase 1 filing deadline passed July 24, and Phase 2 (reconciliation entries) opened June 29 with an October 16 close. The market is now in a **post-filing/disbursement phase** rather than the pre-filing rush that drove tool launches in February–May. As a result, the competitive moat is moving from "who can calculate the fastest" toward "who can manage the ongoing claim lifecycle and broker relationships." This is exactly Fintella's structural advantage. Three notable new findings: (1) **Tariff Partners** launched a direct competitor to Fintella's broker referral/commission model; (2) **GingerControl** emerged as a credible mid-tier tool with real-time policy updates and Section 122 line-item tracking; (3) **The Trade Lab** has expanded into a full AI trade compliance platform with CE training and an AI voice assistant.

The 80-day countdown is now relevant to Phase 2, not Phase 1. Tools with disbursement tracking, claim-status visibility, and Phase 2 support will capture the next wave of attention.

## New Entrants (Since May 6 Scan)

### NE-11. Tariff Partners — https://tariffpartners.com
**⚠️ THREAT LEVEL: HIGH — Direct competition to Fintella's broker referral model**

- **What it is**: A partner program for customs brokers, trade attorneys, and freight forwarders to screen importer clients, refer them for IEEPA refund claims, and earn commissions on recovered refunds.
- **How it works**: Brokers submit leads; Tariff Partners handles the full CAPE assessment for importers who lack bandwidth to run multi-thousand-entry analyses. Partner earns a referral fee on claims originated.
- **No calculator tool**: Tariff Partners is not a calculator — it is purely a referral/commission network.
- **Critical gap**: No TMS embedding, no multi-client dashboard, no integrated calculator. This is a network play without a product.
- **Positioning**: Brokers as the channel, importers as the end-beneficiary. Revenue share on claim outcomes.
- **Why it's a threat**: This is the first competitor explicitly building a broker referral network around IEEPA refunds — Fintella's same go-to-market motion. Fintella's advantage is the integrated product (calculator → referral → commission in one portal vs. Tariff Partners' manual referral + spreadsheet workflow).

### NE-13. GingerControl — https://gingercontrol.com
**New — Mid-tier tool with real-time policy updates as key differentiator**

- **Core capability**: Multi-layer tariff calculator that shows every tariff layer as a separate line item (MFN + Section 301 + 232 + Section 122 + IEEPA) with simultaneous multi-country comparison.
- **Real-time policy updates**: Date-sensitive rate logic — as rates change (EU deal at 15%, Section 122 expiration, new executive orders), calculations update automatically.
- **Section 122 specialization**: Section 122 explicitly called out as a separate line item, making it easy to audit which entries included the 10% temporary surcharge (important since Section 122 is NOT refundable under CAPE).
- **CAPE documentation**: Organizes CAPE Declarations, REV-603 disbursement details, and supporting documentation for tax teams (COGS reversal, interest income, state-level adjustments).
- **Competitive content**: Published comparison of Flexport vs. GingerControl; also published guides on Section 122 expiration and IEEPA refund taxability.
- **Pricing**: Not confirmed; calculator appears free, broader platform may be paid.
- **No TMS embedding, no broker dashboard, no commission tracking.**
- **⚠️ Note**: Real-time policy update engine is a genuine differentiator as tariff policy continues to evolve in 2026. Fintella should ensure its rate tables are also updated in real time.

## Recommended Actions

### 1. COUNTER Tariff Partners with an explicit "Fintella vs. referral networks" message
Tariff Partners is the first competitor to explicitly build a broker referral/commission network. Their weakness: **no product**. Fintella's response should emphasize that Fintella is the only network that combines the calculator tool, the broker dashboard, and the commission tracking in one portal — brokers don't have to send clients to a third party and hope for a referral fee. Suggested homepage copy: "Don't just refer — track every claim, view every client, and earn your commission in the same portal."

### 2. ADD a CAPE disbursement status / Phase 2 tracker to the TIE dashboard
TariffsTool.com has become the public reference for CAPE progress data. Fintella brokers are asking "where is my client's refund?" — and the answer isn't in the TIE product today. Adding a CAPE phase timeline, Phase 2 close date (Oct 16), and a link to the CBP ACE portal for claim status checks would make the broker dashboard the source of truth for broker-client conversations about disbursement timing.

### 3. VERIFY and promote real-time tariff rate updates
GingerControl is explicitly marketing real-time policy updates as a differentiator — rates that auto-adjust as executive orders, Federal Register notices, and USITC changes take effect. Confirm whether Fintella's rate tables are updated in real time. If yes, make it explicit in marketing. If not, this is a gap to close before it becomes a talking point in competitive comparisons.

### 4. MONITOR Wove API for CargoWise/Magaya partnerships
Wove published a dedicated API blog post. If they announce a CargoWise or Magaya integration, Fintella's TMS embedding advantage narrows significantly. Recommend checking Wove's blog and changelog monthly. If they announce a TMS partnership, Fintella should accelerate the API/webhook documentation for its own TMS embed to establish prior technical leadership.
