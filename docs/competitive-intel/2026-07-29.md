# Competitive Intelligence Report — 2026-07-29

## Summary

The IEEPA tariff refund calculator market has matured significantly since the May 6 scan. **CAPE Phase 2 went live June 29** and Phase 3 is launching end of July — the refund window is real and shrinking, which is accelerating both competitor feature development and market consolidation. CBP has authorized $104B+ in refunds and paid $71B+ as of early July. Three notable new entrants were found: **TariffGuru.com** (free AI agent + paid $97 toolkit + 300+ domain network), **Gateway Lines** (AI OCR on CF-7501 uploads), and **1st Capital Financial** (ES-003 → CAPE Declaration CSV generation, free, no signup). The **TMS embedding and multi-client broker dashboard gap remains uncontested** — no competitor has shipped either feature. CustomsGenius (the CAPE CSV threat flagged in May) has not publicly expanded; 1st Capital Financial is now the most credible free CAPE CSV alternative to Fintella. Content marketing SEO plays are saturating the space, but broker-infrastructure features remain Fintella's exclusive territory.

## New Entrants

Four verifiable new entrants identified since the May 6 scan:

### NE-11. 1st Capital Financial — https://1stcapitalfinancial.com/ieepa-tariff-refund/calculator/
- **Tool**: ES-003 upload → CAPE Declaration CSV generator. Free, no signup.
- **Method**: Reads ACE ES-003, identifies IEEPA lines (9903.01/9903.02), estimates refund + statutory interest, classifies CAPE Phase 1 eligibility, and **downloads a CAPE Declaration CSV ready for ACE upload**.
- **Client-side**: File parsed in browser; no data transmitted to their servers.
- **⚠️ Threat level: HIGH** — This is now the most accessible free CAPE CSV generator in the market alongside CustomsGenius (May 6). Unlike CustomsGenius, 1st Capital's tool is free with no per-line pricing, lowering the barrier significantly.
- **Gap vs. Fintella**: No pre-validation rules (entry mod-10 check, 9,999 line limit enforcement, liquidation flag). No broker dashboard. No TMS embed.
- **Positioning**: Capital/finance services firm (factoring, financing); CAPE CSV as a free acquisition tool.

### NE-12. Gateway Lines — https://tariff.gatewaylines.com/ieepa-refund
- **Tool**: Free CF-7501 AI OCR upload OR quick estimator.
- **AI**: "i-Sight AI" — processes uploaded customs documents to identify IEEPA-specific duties and calculate exact refunds.
- **Input method**: CF-7501 drag-and-drop (same as Allied CHB) with an AI layer for extraction.
- **Data handling**: Documents transmitted over HTTPS, processed in real-time, discarded immediately.
- **Free, no signup**.
- **Platform**: Also runs a free tariff calculator and import duty simulator at `tariff.gatewaylines.com/`.
- **⚠️ Note**: AI OCR on the CF-7501 PDF is the one feature Allied CHB doesn't have (Allied CHB appears to parse structured 7501 data, not raw OCR). If Gateway Lines' OCR is robust, they could capture users with messy or scanned entry documents.
- **Positioning**: Logistics/freight brokerage firm; refund calculator as a free lead magnet.

### NE-13. TariffGuru.com — https://tariffguru.com
- **Tool**: Free AI Recovery Agent + $97 "Federal Recovery Toolkit" + concierge data audit services.
- **AI Agent**: Trained on CBP CAPE documentation, ACE portal protocols, Federal Register interest rate tables, and CIT court orders.
- **Free tier**: AI filing agent (diagnostic, eligibility check, guide generation).
- **Paid tier**: $97 toolkit includes technical filing prep materials, ACH setup instructions, pre-filing audit checklist.
- **Domain network**: 300+ IEEPA and CAPE domain names registered — aggressive SEO moat.
- **Free diagnostic**: `/diagnostic.html` — CAPE eligibility check before paying anything.
- **Target**: Small importers (300,000 businesses) who lack access to institutional trade counsel.
- **Positioning**: "Closes the information gap between large institutional importers and small businesses."
- **⚠️ Note**: The 300+ domain network is a pure SEO defensive play — they will rank for most long-tail IEEPA refund queries and funnel to their paid toolkit. Not a technology threat but a content marketing competitor.
- **CAPE CSV generation**: Not confirmed. Guides only.

### NE-14. TariffLens.ai — https://www.tarifflens.ai
- **Core product**: AI-powered HTS classification for customs brokers. Not primarily a refund calculator.
- **IEEPA content**: Publishes substantive CAPE filing guides (e.g., "The $165 Billion Refund Window: How to File Your IEEPA Tariff Claim Before It Closes") — strong SEO content targeting customs professionals.
- **Data security**: AES-256 at rest, TLS 1.3 in transit; no client data sold/shared — differentiator for compliance-sensitive broker workflows.
- **Positioning**: Customs broker tooling (HTS classification); IEEPA content as a channel.
- **⚠️ Note**: Same dynamic as The Trade Lab — if they gain broker traction via HTS classification, they have a natural path to adding a calculator/CAPE tool. Worth monitoring.
- **CAPE CSV generation**: Not detected. ❌

## Recommended Actions

### 1. URGENT — Publish CAPE Phase 2/3 support before end of July
CAPE Phase 2 (protest/liquidated entries) went live June 29. Phase 3 (finally liquidated entries via CIT) is targeting end of July 2026. **No competitor has publicly confirmed Phase 2 or Phase 3 support in their calculator.** This is a first-mover window: if Fintella ships Phase 2 CAPE CSV generation and announces it this week, it will be the only self-serve tool covering the full refund lifecycle. The window is measured in days, not weeks.

### 2. Counter 1st Capital Financial's free CAPE CSV with the pre-validation story
1st Capital Financial now offers free CAPE Declaration CSV generation with no signup — directly matching Fintella's CAPE CSV feature on the most visible dimension. **The differentiator is Fintella's pre-validation.** Recommended: Add a visible "validation score" or "pre-flight checklist" to Fintella's CAPE CSV output that explicitly names the checks run (CBP entry number mod-10, batch size ≤9,999 lines, liquidation status flag, importer match). Frame it as "1 of 4 CBP rejection reasons caught before you upload." This converts a feature parity situation into a quality-layer advantage.

### 3. Add Section 301/232/122 classification to ES-003 analysis (carry-forward from May)
This action was recommended in the May 6 report and remains open. UNIS and ITEM Marketplace classify all entries, not just IEEPA — brokers need the full picture when advising clients. **Adding these flags (labeled "not refundable under CAPE") is a ≤1-day build** against the existing ES-003 parser, and it closes UNIS's only remaining differentiator vs. Fintella in the tool feature set.

## Market Context (Updated)

| CAPE Phase | Status | Launch Date | Covers |
|-----------|--------|-------------|--------|
| Phase 1 | Active | April 20, 2026 | Unliquidated entries + entries within 80 days of liquidation |
| Phase 2 | Active | June 29, 2026 | Liquidated entries within protest window |
| Phase 3 | Imminent | Late July 2026 | Finally liquidated entries (CIT litigants only) |

- **Refund pool progress**: ~$104.29B authorized, ~$71.06B paid out as of mid-July 2026 (including statutory interest).
- **CBP has processed 16M+ entries** under Phase 1; second wave payments via ACH ongoing.
- **Government appeal**: The administration appealed the CIT refund order to the Federal Circuit; proceedings ongoing. The CAPE process continues pending the appeal outcome per court order.
- **Deadline pressure is real and shrinking**: 80-day countdown clocks are ticking on entries near the CAPE Phase 1 cutoff. Fintella's 80-day alert is one of the few tools that makes this visible to brokers.
- **Section 122** (10% universal surcharge, effective Feb 24, 2026) is NOT refundable — still a source of confusion in the market.
