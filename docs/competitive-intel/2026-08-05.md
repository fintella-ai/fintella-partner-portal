# Competitive Intelligence Report — 2026-08-05

> **Prior report**: [2026-05-06](./2026-05-06.md) · **Scan interval**: ~90 days
>
> **Market moment**: With $86.3B paid out of the $166B IEEPA refund pool as of July 10, 2026, the space is shifting from "Phase 1 deadline rush" to "Phase 2 strategy." CAPE Phase 2 launched June 29, 2026, opening refunds for more complex liquidated-entry cases. Tools that can handle Phase 2 workflows will define the next competitive wave.

---

## Summary

The competitive landscape has continued to consolidate since the May 6 scan. The major headline is **CAPE Phase 2 launched June 29, 2026**, expanding eligible entries significantly — but no competitor has shipped Phase 2-specific tooling yet. Two new entrants of note: **1st Capital Financial** now generates CAPE Declaration CSV (joining CustomsGenius as only the second tool with self-serve CAPE CSV), and **Gateway Lines** offers CF-7501 AI document analysis with an API for ERP/TMS embedding. The Phase 1 window is largely closing (most unliquidated entries have cleared the 80-day window); tools competing for Phase 2 filers will be the next battleground. **Fintella's TMS embedding, multi-client dashboard, and commission pipeline remain unmatched** — no competitor has shipped those in the three months since the last scan.

## New Entrants Since May 6

### NE-1 (Updated). 1st Capital Financial — https://1stcapitalfinancial.com/ieepa-tariff-refund/calculator/
**⚠️ Threat level: HIGH — CAPE CSV generation confirmed**

- Financial services firm (likely asset-based lending / factoring background) that launched a tariff refund product.
- **Features**: ES-003 upload → reads 9903.01/02 IEEPA lines → estimates refund principal + statutory interest → classifies CAPE Phase 1 eligibility per entry → **generates downloadable CAPE Declaration CSV**.
- CAPE CSV generation is confirmed — making this the **second self-serve CAPE CSV generator** in the market (alongside CustomsGenius from the May scan).
- Unlike CustomsGenius (usage-based pricing), 1st Capital Financial's calculator appears to be **free with no signup** for the calculator itself.
- Revenue model: Likely refund financing (advance on the refund claim before CBP pays) — their IEEPA Refund Services page exists at /ieepa-tariff-refund/.
- **Assessment**: The combination of free CAPE CSV + implied financing product is a potent bundle. Importers who want their money faster than CBP's 60–90 day timeline could bypass broker filing entirely using this tool.

### NE-2 (Updated). Alliance Drawback Services — https://alliancechb.com/ieepa-refund-calculator/
*(Note: different company from Allied CHB — naming proximity is a potential market confusion point)*

- ES-003 upload, client-side processing (files never leave browser unless user engages their service).
- **Success-based pricing**: 2% of IEEPA refund amount (subject to minimum/maximum caps) for full CAPE submission management — handles data collection, validation, submission, tracking, and CBP advocacy.
- Calculator is free; full managed service is the paid product.
- **Limitation noted**: Does not account for liquidation status, PSC eligibility, protest deadlines, drawback claimed, or interest in the free calculator.
- **Assessment**: Standard broker-as-a-service model. The 2% success fee is consistent with market pricing (CustomsGenius charges $0.25/line for CSV, roughly comparable).

### NE-3 (Updated). Gateway Lines — https://tariff.gatewaylines.com/ieepa-refund
**⚠️ Notable — CF-7501 AI analysis + API access**

- **Two input paths**: (1) Upload CF-7501 customs documents for AI-powered analysis; (2) Quick estimator (country + import value → rough estimate).
- AI analysis: Parses 7501 documents to identify IEEPA duty lines — similar to Allied CHB's PDF approach but explicitly described as "AI-powered."
- **API access**: Gateway's tariff calculator platform offers API access for embedding in ERP/TMS — the first non-Fintella tool with an explicit ERP/TMS embedding angle.
- Data security: Documents processed in real-time, not stored.
- Free base calculator; API pricing not confirmed.
- Comprehensive tariff coverage: Section 301, 232 (50/25/15/10% tiered), Section 122, IEEPA.
- **Assessment**: The ERP/TMS API angle is the most notable competitive development in this scan relative to Fintella's TMS embedding advantage. Gateway has not announced specific TMS integrations (CargoWise, Magaya), but the direction of travel is toward embedding.

### NE-4 (Updated). Wove API — https://wove.com/refunds
*(Wove was tracked in May; the API tier is now more clearly confirmed)*

- Tariff Calculator API confirmed available for brokers and developers.
- Free first analysis; API pricing not confirmed.
- Entry-by-entry recovery mechanism routing: PSC vs. Protest vs. CIT Litigation — still the most granular recovery guidance in the market.
- Built in 72 hours of the Feb 20 SCOTUS ruling — engineering credibility signal.
- **Assessment**: If Wove announces a CargoWise or Magaya integration, this becomes a direct Fintella threat. No such announcement detected as of this scan.

### NE-5 (Confirmed from May). TariffsTool — https://www.tariffstool.com
*(More data available this scan)*

- **Live refund tracker**: Real CBP data — shows $121.75B accepted, $86.3B paid, $49B in June alone. This tracker is getting press coverage and appears to be a significant SEO/traffic driver.
- **Tools**: Free 60-second refund estimator, HTS lookup (full USITC schedule, 13,776 tariff lines), general duty calculator (36 countries, 14 sectors), country-specific tariff guides.
- **Broker partnerships**: Licensed broker + trade attorney referral network; free consultation for refunds over $100K.
- **Content marketing**: Extensive — monthly refund update posts, country guides, how-to-file guides. Featured in Fortune (May 2026).
- **Assessment**: Strong content marketing play. The live refund tracker is unique in the market and drives recurring visits. Not a professional filing tool, but a major top-of-funnel competitor for importer awareness.

## Recommended Actions

### 1. URGENT — Update deadline messaging for Phase 2 context
CAPE Phase 2 launched June 29, 2026. Fintella's 80-day countdown was built around Phase 1 (unliquidated entries). Most of those entries have now cleared or the window has closed. **Recommended**: Update the countdown logic and any landing page copy to address Phase 2 (protest window for liquidated entries — 180-day window from liquidation date, not the 80-day Phase 1 window). This is a simple copy/logic update but ensures Fintella stays relevant as Phase 1 winds down.

### 2. HIGH — Counter 1st Capital Financial's free CAPE CSV with the validation story
1st Capital Financial now offers free CAPE CSV download — joining CustomsGenius. The Fintella CAPE CSV differentiator is eroding on the "does it exist" dimension. The remaining differentiator is **pre-validation**: entry number CBP mod-10 check, batch size ≤9,999 enforcement, liquidation status flags, importer match verification. **Recommended**: Make the validation rules explicitly visible on the TIE feature page. Position as "CAPE CSV that doesn't get rejected" vs. competitors that generate CSV without validating it.

### 3. MEDIUM — Monitor Gateway Lines and Wove for TMS integrations
Both Gateway Lines (explicit ERP/TMS API) and Wove (broker/developer API) are moving toward the embedding space. Neither has announced a CargoWise or Magaya integration. **Recommended**: Check both APIs quarterly. If either announces a CargoWise partnership, escalate to immediate response — Fintella's TMS embedding is the single largest uncopied advantage in the market.

### 4. MEDIUM — Consider adding Phase 2 / protest recovery routing (a la Wove)
Wove's entry-by-entry recovery routing (PSC vs. Protest vs. CIT Litigation) is the most technically differentiated feature not yet in Fintella's stack. As Phase 2 opens liquidated-entry cases, importers will need guidance on which entries require protests (not just PSCs). **Recommended**: Add a recovery mechanism flag to the ES-003 analysis output — "Phase 1 CAPE eligible," "Phase 2 protest required," or "Ineligible / No IEEPA duty." This positions Fintella as Phase 2-ready before competitors ship it.
