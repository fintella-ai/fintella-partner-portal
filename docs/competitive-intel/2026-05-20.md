# Competitive Intelligence Report — 2026-05-20

## Summary

The IEEPA tariff refund market has rapidly densified since the February 20, 2026 Supreme Court ruling striking down ~$166 billion in IEEPA duties. At least nine distinct tools are now in the space, ranging from free single-importer calculators (Allied CHB, UNIS/ITEM, The Trade Lab, TariffRefundIQ) to AI-augmented enterprise automation (Flexport, Descartes). Fintella's multi-client, broker-native, TMS-embedded positioning is not replicated by any single competitor; however, Flexport's rapidly expanding AI customs suite and UNIS/ITEM's ES-003 native processing are the two most direct capability threats requiring close monitoring.

**What it does:** Free IEEPA tariff refund calculator operated by a licensed US customs broker (Filer Code 9AJ). Users drag-and-drop or upload CBP Form 7501 entry summary PDFs to instantly identify IEEPA refund eligibility.

**Key features observed:**

**Pricing:** Free analysis — no pricing tiers found in indexed content.

**Signup required:** No signup required — "see your potential savings in seconds."

**Target audience:** Importers who have their own CBP 7501 PDFs. Positioned as a lead-generation tool for Allied CHB brokerage services.

**AI/technology mentions:** No AI mentions found; described as a parsing engine.

**CAPE CSV / bulk upload / TMS / multi-client:** No evidence of CAPE CSV generation, bulk batch upload, TMS embedding, or multi-client/broker dashboard features. Single-importer, single-session tool.

### UNIS/ITEM (unisco.com/ieepa-refund-calculator + marketplace.item.com/ieepa-refund-calculator)

**Availability:** Page returned HTTP 403 to automated fetchers; content reconstructed from indexed page titles, Google snippets, and third-party guides citing the tool directly.

**What it does:** ACE ES-003 report analyzer. Users upload their ACE ES-003 CSV export (Entry Summary Line Tariff Details) to identify IEEPA-eligible entries, calculate duty exposure, and track protest deadlines. Available both on UNIS's own site and mirrored on the ITEM marketplace.

**Key features observed:**
- Accepts ES-003 as CSV, Excel (.xlsx), or comma-delimited TXT
- All processing runs locally in the browser — no data transmitted to any server
- Classifies every entry line by tariff type: IEEPA (9903.01/02), Section 301 (9903.88), Section 232, Section 122
- For liquidated entries: calculates the 180-day protest deadline and highlights urgent cases
- Open/unliquidated entries tracked separately
- Sample data load available to test without real data
- Required columns: Entry Summary Number, HTS Number (Full), Line Tariff Duty Amount, Line Tariff Goods Value

**Pricing:** No pricing found; appears entirely free.

**Signup required:** No signup required.

**Target audience:** Importers and customs brokers who have already pulled their ES-003 from ACE. The ITEM marketplace version targets the ITEM logistics platform user base.

**AI/technology mentions:** No AI mentions found; browser-local JavaScript processing.

**CAPE CSV / bulk upload / TMS / multi-client:** ES-003 CSV natively accepted — this is a direct capability overlap with Fintella. No evidence of CAPE CSV generation (output), TMS embedding, or multi-client dashboard. Single-session, single-file tool.

### TariffRefundIQ (tariffrefundiq.com)

**Availability:** Page returned HTTP 403 to automated fetchers; content reconstructed from Google snippets (title: "IEEPA Tariff Refund Calculator & Guide 2026 | TariffRefundIQ") and indexed blog posts including tariffrefundiq.com/blog/ace-ach-setup-guide-ieepa-refund.

**What it does:** Probability-weighted NPV model for IEEPA refund claims. Rather than raw entry-by-entry calculation, TariffRefundIQ focuses on expected value: filing status, entry liquidation, cost of capital, and seven probability-weighted legal scenarios.

- "Sell vs. hold" recommendation engine

- Pro subscriber tier: receives updates as CIT proceedings, congressional action, and CBP guidance evolve
- Blog content includes ACE/ACH setup guides (e.g., how to set up bank account for refund deposit)

**Pricing:** Free tier (in-browser calculator). Pro subscriber tier exists; no specific price found.

**Signup required:** Free calculator does not require signup; Pro tier requires subscription.

**Target audience:** Financially sophisticated importers weighing whether to hold, sell, or negotiate their claims. Finance/legal positioning rather than operational/filing.

**AI/technology mentions:** No AI mentions found; browser-local financial modeling.

**CAPE CSV / bulk upload / TMS / multi-client:** No evidence of ES-003 upload, CAPE CSV generation, bulk upload, TMS embedding, or multi-client features. Focused on financial modeling, not filing automation.

**Wove (tariffs.wove.com/us/refunds)**
Built their IEEPA refund calculator in 72 hours (per their own blog post). Key differentiator: shows the correct recovery mechanism (PSC, Protest, or CIT Litigation) per entry, with every calculation traceable to a specific tariff schedule and Federal Register notice. API available (Wove Tariff Calculator API). Free for first analysis. Targets importers and developers (API access). No CAPE CSV generation or multi-client features confirmed.

**ClaimTariffRefund (claimtariffrefund.com)**
Appeared in IEEPA calculator search results with title "IEEPA Tariff Refund Calculator & Guide (2026)." Minimal information found beyond the URL; likely a lead-generation or advisory service site.

**GingerControl (gingercontrol.com)**
AI global trade compliance infrastructure for importers, exporters, and customs brokers. Features: product classification, tariff engineering, duty calculation, policy change tracking. Positioned as "broker-grade software" with multi-client tenancy, per-client billing, ABI filer integration, and per-client policy alerts — this is the most direct new multi-client/broker-facing entrant found. ## Recommended Actions

**1. Counter GingerControl's broker-native positioning immediately.**
GingerControl is the only newly identified tool explicitly advertising multi-client tenancy, per-client billing, and ABI filer integration for customs brokers. This is Fintella's closest structural competitor for the broker-channel. A direct feature comparison landing page targeting "GingerControl alternative" search intent, plus a capabilities comparison card for broker sales conversations, should be prepared before GingerControl gains search ranking momentum in this niche.

**2. Promote CAPE CSV auto-generation as a named, standalone feature.**
Zero competitors confirmed CAPE CSV auto-generation. Every tool stops at "tell you what you're owed" — none auto-generate the submission-ready ACE CAPE Declaration CSV. This is Fintella's clearest unmet-need advantage. It should be named explicitly ("CAPE Declaration Generator"), featured in the hero section, and demonstrated with a sample output file in the docs.

**3. Accelerate TariffRefundsCalculator (tariffrefundscalculator.com) intelligence.**
This site appeared with the description "IEEPA Refund Calculator for Broker and ACE CSV Exports" — the first competitor copy that explicitly mentions both brokers and CSV exports in the same product description. A targeted follow-up scan on this specific URL is warranted to confirm whether it has a multi-client workflow or CAPE CSV output capability that would make it a more direct threat than it currently appears.
