# Competitive Intelligence Report — 2026-06-24

**Previous scan**: 2026-05-06 · **Delta period**: ~49 days

---

## Summary

The IEEPA refund space has matured since the May 6 scan. No headline new calculator has launched, but the competitive frontier has shifted in two meaningful directions: **Descartes CustomsInfo pivoted from "not a competitor" to a full IEEPA refund workflow** with batch PSC filing, auto-flagging, and TMS/API integration — the first enterprise incumbent to do so. Simultaneously, **Flexport's "Audit Your Customs Broker" AI agent is now a dedicated named product** with its own URL, explicitly framing Flexport as a replacement for incumbent CHBs (20% error rate claim). Four verifiable new entrants have emerged since May 6. Fintella's core moat — TMS embedding, multi-client broker dashboards, CAPE CSV pre-validation, and referral commission tracking — **remains unmatched by any competitor**. However, Descartes entering the TMS-integrated refund space at the enterprise tier is the strongest structural threat detected to date.

### 9. Descartes CustomsInfo — https://www.descartes.com/solutions/global-trade-intelligence/product-classification-and-duty-determination
**Status: ⚠️ SIGNIFICANT UPGRADE — Now a direct IEEPA refund competitor**

This is the most material competitive development in the delta period. Descartes was categorized in the May 6 scan as "not a direct IEEPA refund competitor." That verdict has changed.

Descartes now has a dedicated IEEPA tariff refund resource page (`descartes.com/resources/knowledge-center/tariff-refunds-ieepa-tariffs`) and a full refund workflow product:

- **Auto-flagging**: Business logic continuously monitors liquidation status and flags entries eligible for IEEPA refunds.
- **Batch PSC filing**: Thousands of entries processed simultaneously — described as "enterprise-grade."
- **TMS/API integration**: Deep integration with Descartes Forwarder TMS via robust APIs for "real-time trade data exchange and automated PSC creation." This is the feature set closest to Fintella's TMS embedding claim.
- **Multi-user access**: Centralized data management with configurable dashboards — supports large-scale third-party (broker) operations.
- **Audit trail**: Comprehensive audit trail per PSC.
- **Target audience**: Large importers and customs brokers managing many clients simultaneously.

**⚠️ Threat level: MEDIUM-HIGH.** Descartes is an established enterprise incumbent with deep TMS integration capabilities and a large broker customer base. They now compete directly with Fintella's multi-client dashboard and TMS-adjacent value proposition — but at the enterprise/sales-led tier, not as a self-serve tool. No evidence of CAPE CSV self-serve generation or direct CargoWise/Magaya widget embedding yet.

## New Entrants (since May 6, 2026)

### NE-1. Alliance Drawback Services — https://alliancechb.com/ieepa-refund-calculator/
*(Note: distinct entity from Allied CHB — similar name, different company)*

- **Input**: ACE ES-003 Excel upload; browser-based, file stays local.
- **Pricing**: Success fee model — **2% of refund, $10K minimum, $100K cap** — for full CAPE submission management. First calculator use is free.
- **Services**: Full CAPE submission workflow — data collection, validation, ACE filing, refund tracking, CBP advocacy.
- **Background**: Alliance Drawback Services is a duty drawback specialist (Section 301, drawback claims) pivoting IEEPA expertise from existing drawback relationships.
- **Positioning**: Broker-facing and importer-facing; "we manage everything" for a capped fee.
- **Threat level**: LOW — success-fee model is rational but competes with free tools. CHB relationships create direct referral conflict with Fintella's commission pipeline.

- **Pricing**: Flat-fee model (specific amount not confirmed), not percentage-based — a pricing model distinction from Alliance Drawback and others.
- **Also has**: IEEPA interest calculator blog content, refund status tracking via ACE guide.
- **Positioning**: Managed-service for importers who don't want to touch ACE themselves.
- **Threat level**: LOW — full-service managed filing, not a calculator tool. Different value chain position.

### NE-3. GingerControl — https://gingercontrol.com
- **Who**: AI-powered trade compliance infrastructure platform.
- **IEEPA content**: IEEPA tariff refund guide, ACE/CAPE process walkthrough, taxability analysis ("Is your IEEPA refund taxable income?"), Section 122 vs. IEEPA comparison.
- **Notable**: Published a head-to-head comparison of "Flexport vs. GingerControl Tariff Calculator" — signals they are actively positioning against Flexport.
- **Tools**: Tariff Calculator and Classification Researcher (quantify exposure per entry + audit entries for CAPE prep).
- **Unique angle**: The only competitor explicitly addressing tax implications of IEEPA refunds.
- **Content volume**: High — trade compliance software buyer's guide author (2026 edition), multiple IEEPA-specific posts.
- **Positioning**: AI infrastructure play; targets compliance teams at importers and brokers.
- **Threat level**: LOW direct (no CAPE CSV / broker dashboard), but HIGH content SEO — strong importer education play that could outcompete Fintella on organic discovery.

### NE-4. ES-003.com — https://es-003.com/calculator
- **Who**: Dedicated site built around the ACE ES-003 report format.
- **Tools**: IEEPA Refund Calculator + IEEPA Refund Deadlines tracker (all deadlines, updated April 20, 2026).
- **Deadline tracking**: Tracks Form 19 deadline (August 2026), Phase 1 CAPE open period, individual entry protest windows.
- **Free, no signup**.
- **Positioning**: Highly targeted — importers/brokers who already know the ES-003 and want a specialist tool.
- **Threat level**: LOW-MEDIUM for the calculator; **MEDIUM for deadline tracking** since Fintella's 80-day countdown is a named differentiator and ES-003.com has a dedicated multi-deadline page.

## Market Context Update (as of June 24, 2026)

- **CAPE Phase 1 scale**: As of May 22, 2026 — ~126K declarations submitted; ~15.9M entries accepted; ~$60B forwarded to Treasury for disbursement.
- **Phase 1 coverage**: CBP's June 2026 CIT filing confirms Phase 1 covers ~63% of all IEEPA-tariffed entries. Phase 2 (protest-based, covers liquidated entries outside 80-day window) has no confirmed launch date.
- **Refund timeline**: ~60–90 days after CAPE Declaration acceptance for standard claims. First-wave disbursements confirmed May 12, 2026.
- **Federal Circuit appeal risk**: June 7, 2026 deadline for appeal of the SCOTUS ruling was flagged by TariffsTool. The outcome of any appeal would directly affect refund program continuity.
- **Section 122 remains**: The 10% temporary import surcharge (effective Feb 24, 2026) replaced IEEPA tariffs and is NOT refundable via CAPE. Critical for calculator accuracy.
- **TariffClaim.ai investor angle**: Claim factoring/financing for large refunds is an emerging niche. Sidley Austin published analysis on "key considerations for lenders, borrowers, and claims purchasers" in April 2026 — signals this is becoming a real market.

## Recommended Actions

### 1. Directly address the Descartes enterprise threat — name Fintella's broker differentiation
Descartes now sells "multi-user centralized dashboards" and "batch PSC via API" to enterprise brokers — the same audience Fintella targets. The key differentiator Descartes cannot easily replicate is **Fintella's referral commission pipeline** (Dossier-to-Deal → Frost Law partner network → commission tracking). **Recommended**: Add a broker-specific landing page or section of the TIE that explicitly contrasts Fintella's model: "Unlike enterprise compliance platforms, Fintella is built for the broker who earns revenue from the deals they source, not just tools they pay to license."

### 2. Surface the refund tax implication gap — zero competitors address it
GingerControl is the only competitor with a "Is your IEEPA refund taxable?" article, but they offer no specific tool. IEEPA refund principal is generally taxable where the original duty was deducted through COGS; statutory interest is ordinary income. This is a compliance landmine that no calculator warns about. **Recommended**: Add a tax-implication callout (single tooltip or note) to the TIE results page: "IEEPA refund amounts may be taxable. Consult your accountant — the principal is likely ordinary income to the extent duties were expensed." This costs Fintella nothing and creates importer trust at a moment of vulnerability (they're about to receive a large payment).

### 3. Counter ES-003.com's deadline tracker with a sharper 80-day alert
ES-003.com now has a multi-deadline tracker page with Form 19 (August 2026) and Phase 1 CAPE deadlines. Fintella's 80-day countdown is listed as a named differentiator but is specific to the CAPE Phase 1 window. **Recommended**: Extend the deadline counter to surface the Form 19 (Section 19 USC §1514 protest) deadline alongside the 80-day CAPE window, so Fintella's countdown explicitly covers both paths. This closes ES-003.com's only real feature gap vs. Fintella.
