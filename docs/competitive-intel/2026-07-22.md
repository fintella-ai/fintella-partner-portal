# Competitive Intelligence Report — 2026-07-22

## Summary

The IEEPA tariff refund calculator space has matured since the May 6 scan but shows one critical new development: **Wove has confirmed native CargoWise and Magaya TMS integration**, directly attacking Fintella's previously unchallenged claim of being the only TMS-embedded refund calculator. Refund payouts have accelerated sharply — $86.3B paid as of July 10 (up from $24B in early July per prior tracker data), with CAPE Phase 2 (June 29) adding reconciliation entries and Phase 3 (end of July) imminent for finally-liquidated entries. No other significant new entrants were detected since May 6, but market urgency is peaking as Phase 3 approaches.

> **Prior report baseline**: 2026-05-06 — 19+ tools tracked, 10 new entrants found that scan. See `docs/competitive-intel/2026-05-06.md` for full detail on tools not substantially changed since.

## Changes Detected

### 1. Allied CHB — https://www.alliedchb.com/refunds
**Status: Active | No material changes from May 6**

- CBP 7501 PDF drag-and-drop remains the gold standard entry point. Parses all IEEPA duty types. No signup required.
- Licensed brokers handle the PSC process end-to-end ("we handle it for you" positioning).
- CAPE CSV generation: not self-serve — routes to broker team.
- No new features detected.

### 2. Flexport — https://tariffs.flexport.com/refunds
**Status: Active | No new features detected since Feb 2026 launch**

- AI Auditor ("Audit Your Customs Broker" agent) + tariff refund automation pipeline remain the headline features.
- Full automation requires a Flexport account; basic calculator is public.
- Flexport is managing CAPE submissions for customers but has not announced Phase 2 self-serve tooling.
- Positioning continues to be aggressive against incumbent brokers with the "audit your broker" framing.

### 3. UNIS / UNISCO — https://www.unisco.com/ieepa-refund-calculator
**Status: Active | Still available via ITEM marketplace dual-channel**

- ES-003 CSV/TXT upload, fully client-side (no data transmitted to servers), 180-day protest deadline tracking.
- Section 301 (9903.88), Section 232 (9903.80/85), Section 122 (9903.03) classification alongside IEEPA — still the most complete multi-tariff classification among free tools.
- No CAPE CSV generation or Phase 2 support detected.
- The ITEM marketplace channel (marketplace.item.com) continues as a second distribution point.

### 4. The Trade Lab — https://thetradelab.ai/refund-calculator
**Status: Active | Section 301 reference guide added**

- ES-003 CSV/Excel upload, IEEPA-line identification by Chapter 99 codes, refund per line.
- Added a Section 301 Tariff Rates reference guide at `/tools/section-301-tariffs` — content-marketing play for SEO and broker reference.
- Freemium model: calculator free, paid AI tier for full platform.
- No CAPE CSV output detected.

### 5. TariffClaim.ai — https://tariffclaim.ai/refund-calculator
**Status: Active | No new feature signals**

- Free, no signup ES-003 upload calculator. App platform (app.tariffclaim.ai) for account-based features still present.
- Claim-value-to-investors angle remains unique. No CAPE CSV generation confirmed.

### 6. TariffRefundIQ — https://www.tariffrefundiq.com
**Status: Active | Content marketing expanding**

- Probability-weighted NPV model with 7 scenarios; all client-side.
- Hold-vs-sell breakeven recommendation remains a unique feature.
- ACE ACH setup guide content (for CAPE payment registration) added to blog since May.
- No structural tool changes detected.

### 7. TADA — https://mytada.com
**Status: Active | No IEEPA-specific updates**

- Tariff estimator + HTS lookup remain the free quick tools. Enterprise platform unchanged.
- IEEPA recovery modeling reflects the Feb 20 SCOTUS ruling. 195+ countries.
- No ES-003 or CAPE filing tools in scope.

### 8. SimplyDuty — https://www.simplyduty.com/import-calculator/
**Status: Active | Not an IEEPA refund competitor**

- General import duty calculator. No IEEPA or CAPE features. Rate-freshness concerns remain (see May 6 report).

### 9. Descartes CustomsInfo
**Status: Active | Not an IEEPA refund competitor**

- Enterprise classification database. No IEEPA refund tool. See May 6 report.

### 10. CustomsGenius — https://www.customsgenius.com
**Status: Active | No new announcements found (June–July 2026)**

- Still the highest-threat new entrant from May 6: neurosymbolic AI, CAPE Declaration CSV generation, $0.25/line pricing.
- No new feature launches or pricing changes detected in this scan.
- Phase 2 (reconciliation entries) tool support unconfirmed — worth a recheck in August.

### 11. Wove — https://wove.com / https://tariffs.wove.com/us/refunds
**Status: Active | ⚠️ MATERIAL UPDATE — Native CargoWise + Magaya integration confirmed**

This is the most significant competitive development since May 6.

- **CargoWise integration confirmed**: Wove has a dedicated CargoWise One integration page (`wove.com/cargowise-one-off-quote-automation`) and has announced "deep" CargoWise integration as a platform focus.
- **Magaya integration confirmed**: Listed alongside CargoWise, GoFreight, NetSuite, and Oracle as native TMS/ERP connectors.
- **Tariff Calculator API**: REST API allowing brokers and developers to embed duty rate lookups and refund calculations into any application. Implementation described as "minutes, not months."
- **Scope**: 180+ countries, MFN + preferential rates + additional duties (stacked). IEEPA refund calculator at `tariffs.wove.com/us/refunds` is the public free tool.
- **AI**: Rules-based engine (not ML/AI) against IEEPA tariff schedule for the refund calculator; broader platform uses AI for HS classification at 98% confidence.

**Implication**: Fintella's "only TMS-embedded calculator" claim is now **at risk**. Wove is not positioning itself specifically as a refund-calculator-in-CargoWise, but the technical infrastructure to do so exists. The key question is whether any broker using Wove's CargoWise integration is running refund calculations through it — this should be treated as an active threat, not a monitor.

## Market Context — What Changed Since May 6

| Milestone | Date | Detail |
|-----------|------|--------|
| CAPE Phase 1 → Phase 2 | June 29, 2026 | Phase 2 adds reconciliation-flagged entries (types 01, 02, 06 with no type 09 filed); ~63% of entries covered since Phase 1 |
| CAPE Phase 3 | ETA late July 2026 | Finally liquidated entries — access may be limited to importers who filed at CIT; government contesting eligibility for non-suit filers |
| Refunds paid out | $86.3B as of July 10 | $49.1B paid in June alone (Treasury data July 13); accelerating |
| CIT July 15 order | July 15, 2026 | Court ordered reliquidation across ~3,700 cases ahead of Phase 3 |
| Federal Circuit appeal | Ongoing | Government continues to contest final liability; unlikely to reverse already-paid refunds |

**Phase 3 is the major urgency driver**: importers with finally-liquidated entries who have NOT yet filed at CIT may be locked out of refunds. This is a time-critical advisory moment for brokers and partner firms.

## Recommended Actions

### 1. URGENT — Explicitly name CargoWise and Magaya in TMS embed messaging before Wove does
Wove now has confirmed CargoWise and Magaya native integrations, a public API, and a live refund calculator. The gap between "has an API" and "has a refund widget inside CargoWise" is currently Fintella's competitive moat, but it is narrowing. **Action**: Update the TIE landing page and any broker-facing materials to specifically name CargoWise and Magaya by name (not just "TMS-embedded") and describe the in-workflow experience concretely — e.g., "Run the IEEPA refund analysis without leaving your CargoWise screen." The more specific the language, the harder it is for Wove to claim equivalence via API documentation alone.

### 2. URGENT (Phase 3 deadline) — Add Phase 3 eligibility check and CIT-filer flag to the ES-003 output
CAPE Phase 3 (late July) covers finally-liquidated entries but potentially only for importers who filed at CIT. Importers who haven't filed face a hard cut-off. No competitor has a tool that flags this per-entry risk. **Action**: In the ES-003 analysis output, add a "Phase 3 eligibility" flag per entry that identifies finally-liquidated entries and notes whether the importer has an active CIT case. Pair with an urgency alert: "X of your entries may only be recoverable if you file at CIT before Phase 3 closes." This is a concrete advisory value-add no other calculator provides and converts urgency into broker leads.

### 3. Add Phase 2 reconciliation support to CAPE CSV generation
CAPE Phase 2 (live since June 29) adds reconciliation-flagged entries (types 01, 02, 06). Fintella's CAPE CSV generation and pre-validation should explicitly handle Phase 2 entry types. Confirm whether the current CSV generator handles these or excludes them. If excluded, add Phase 2 entry detection to the ES-003 analyzer so brokers don't accidentally omit eligible entries. CustomsGenius's Phase 2 support is unconfirmed — this is a window to stay ahead.
