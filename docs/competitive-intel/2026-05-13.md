# Competitive Intelligence Report — 2026-05-13

## Summary

The IEEPA tariff refund calculator space has expanded significantly since the April 20, 2026 CAPE portal launch, with at least 7 new entrants identified beyond the original 9 monitored competitors. Two new tools — **CustomsGenius** and **MightyBot Duty Reclaim** — directly target the customs broker workflow with CAPE CSV generation and represent the highest competitive threat to Fintella. Fintella's TMS-embedded widget and commission-tracking Dossier-to-Deal pipeline remain **completely uncontested** in the market.

---

## Context

On February 20, 2026, the U.S. Supreme Court ruled 6–3 in *Learning Resources, Inc. v. Trump* that IEEPA does not authorize tariff imposition, voiding all IEEPA-based tariffs from Feb 4, 2025 through Feb 24, 2026. ~$166B is eligible for refund across ~330,000 importers. CBP launched the CAPE (Consolidated Administration and Processing of Entries) filing system on April 20, 2026 inside ACE, accepting CSV-format CAPE Declarations. Phase 1 covers unliquidated entries and those liquidated within 80 days of declaration (~63% of affected entries). An 80-day rolling liquidation window creates genuine urgency.

## Changes Detected (Original 9 Competitors)

### 1. Allied CHB — https://www.alliedchb.com/refunds
**Status:** Live (direct fetch 403; confirmed indexed)

- 7501 PDF drag-and-drop upload — no signup required (unchanged, gold standard)
- Full-service licensed CHB (Filer Code 9AJ) — owns both calculator and downstream CAPE filing
- Server-side OCR/parse; data not stored per their claim
- No ES-003 support; no TMS embedding; no multi-client dashboard
- No public pricing for managed filing services
- **Verdict:** No detected changes from prior position. Stable full-service importer-direct play.

---

### 2. Flexport — https://tariffs.flexport.com/refunds
**Status:** Live (direct fetch 403; confirmed via BusinessWire + LinkedIn)

- **NEW (Feb 2026):** Launched "Agentic AI" Customs Technology Suite — "Audit Your Customs Broker" agent reviews past filings, finds errors, recovers refunds
- **NEW:** Dedicated CAPE Task Force — managed end-to-end CAPE filing for Flexport brokerage customers
- Public calculator (ACE entry report upload) remains free; automation tier locked to Flexport brokerage relationship
- Extensive content marketing: Supreme Court ruling analysis, two BusinessWire press releases, Tariff Simulator Pro video tutorials
- No standalone widget for third-party TMS (CargoWise/Magaya)
- **Verdict:** Significant capability expansion. However, agentic suite is customer-locked — non-Flexport brokers cannot access it. Fintella's open broker model remains differentiated.

### 3. UNIS / ITEM — https://www.unisco.com/ieepa-refund-calculator
**Status:** Live (direct fetch 403; confirmed indexed; also at marketplace.item.com/ieepa-refund-calculator)

- ES-003 CSV upload, fully client-side (no data transmitted to any server) — privacy differentiator
- Auto-classifies by tariff type: IEEPA (9903.01/02), Section 301, 232, Section 122
- Tracks 180-day protest deadlines per entry for liquidated entries
- Second distribution channel via ITEM marketplace
- No CAPE CSV generation confirmed; no TMS embedding; no multi-client dashboard
- **Verdict:** Strong client-side privacy angle. Now distributed on ITEM marketplace — wider reach than last check.

- Broader platform: HTS lookup + landed cost + tariff calculator = full trade compliance suite

## New Entrants

### NEW — CustomsGenius — https://www.customsgenius.com
**Threat: HIGH**

B2B SaaS by eCompliance, Inc. specifically targeting IEEPA refund recovery for brokers.

- Ingests ACE ES-003 + commercial invoices; "neurosymbolic" AI cross-validates every data point across both documents
- Generates CBP-schema-compliant CAPE Declaration CSVs as explicit output
- **Pricing (fully public):** First 500 lines free → $0.18/line for analysis reports → $0.25/line for filing CSVs; volume discounts; no platform fees, no seat fees
- Per-line pricing model aligns directly to how CHBs think about their own billing
- Cross-document validation (entry summary + commercial invoice) is a deeper accuracy claim than any current competitor

**Why this matters:** CustomsGenius is the closest direct competitor to Fintella's CAPE CSV generation for brokers. Their transparent per-line pricing is a strong sales motion. Cross-document validation (using commercial invoices, not just ES-003) is a differentiation Fintella should study.

---

### NEW — MightyBot Duty Reclaim — https://www.mightybot.ai/agents/duty-reclaim/
**Threat: HIGH**

CAPE Declaration CSV preparation agent built explicitly for customs brokers.

- Connects to TMS systems OR accepts CSV exports; applies Phase 1 eligibility rules; normalizes Chapter 99 codes; reconciles duty paid against court orders
- Outputs CBP-schema-compliant CSVs with full "reasonable care" audit trail
- Queue-based broker review workflow for flagged edge cases; broker approves batch; broker uploads through their own ACE Portal account
- Explicitly preserves broker's ACE filing relationship and contingency revenue model
- Pricing: contact-only (not public)

**Why this matters:** MightyBot is the most technically sophisticated broker-facing CAPE tool identified. TMS *data ingestion* (pulling from TMS) is the closest competitor feature to Fintella's TMS-*embedded widget* (running inside TMS). Key distinction: MightyBot pulls data from TMS; Fintella runs inside TMS. The reasonable-care audit trail is a compliance differentiation worth watching.

### NEW — TariffsTool — https://www.tariffstool.com
**Threat: MEDIUM (lead aggregator)**

Content-heavy site with a 30-second free estimator; monetizes via broker referrals.

- Dominant SEO presence — appears on page 1 for nearly every IEEPA search query
- Extensive guide content: ACE portal setup, eligibility explainers, CAPE filing walkthroughs
- Explicitly routes users to licensed customs brokers and trade attorneys for filing
- No file upload; no CAPE CSV; no multi-client dashboard

**Why this matters:** TariffsTool captures top-of-funnel demand and routes it to brokers — could be a referral partner for Fintella-using brokers, or could route volume to competitors.

---

### NEW — Un-tariff — https://www.un-tariff.com
**Threat: MEDIUM-HIGH**

Freemium: free refund-band estimate + paid CAPE preparation workflow.

- Nine-step framework aligned to CBP's validation sequence
- **Key feature:** Client-side pre-validation before generating the CSV (reduces ACE Portal rejection rate)
- Coverage window: Feb 1, 2025 – Feb 7, 2026; interest at 19 CFR 24.3a (8% annualized, Q2 2026, compounded daily)
- Paid CAPE CSV preparation (price not public); free estimate
- No multi-client dashboard; no TMS embedding

**Why this matters:** Pre-submission client-side validation before CSV download is a tactically strong differentiator. Reduces rejections at the ACE Portal — a real pain point brokers face.

## Recommended Actions

### 1. Add cross-document validation (commercial invoice vs. ES-003) — Priority: HIGH
**Gap:** CustomsGenius's "neurosymbolic" cross-validation of ES-003 against commercial invoices is a genuine accuracy claim. If Fintella's CAPE CSV validation only runs against ES-003, it misses discrepancies between what CBP recorded and what the importer actually paid.
**Suggested action:** Add optional commercial invoice upload (PDF or CSV) to the CAPE calculator flow. Flag entries where duty-paid amounts diverge between the entry summary and the commercial invoice. Even a basic flag-only implementation would neutralize this competitive gap.

### 2. Publish Fintella's pre-submission validation rules — Priority: MEDIUM
**Gap:** Un-tariff's "client-side pre-validation before download" is getting search traction because it solves a real pain point: ACE Portal rejections. Fintella already runs CBP mod-10 validation — but this is not externally communicated as a feature.
**Suggested action:** Add a validation step summary to the CAPE CSV download flow ("We checked 12 validation rules before generating this file") and publish a one-page guide at `/docs/cape-validation` listing exactly what Fintella checks. This both differentiates Fintella and supports SEO for "CAPE filing errors" queries.

### 3. Consider a pricing transparency page — Priority: LOW-MEDIUM
**Gap:** CustomsGenius is the only competitor with fully public pricing, and it appears to be helping their broker sales motion. Per-line pricing aligns with how CHBs bill their own clients.
**Suggested action:** If Fintella's business model supports it, add a transparent pricing page or at minimum a "how billing works" explainer. Even if pricing requires a call, describing the model (per-line, per-importer, flat partner fee) builds trust with CHBs who are skeptical of opaque pricing in this space.
