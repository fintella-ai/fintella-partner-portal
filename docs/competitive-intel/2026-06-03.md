# Competitive Intelligence Report — 2026-06-03

> **Baseline**: Previous scan [2026-05-06](./2026-05-06.md). This report focuses on changes and new findings since that date.

---

## Summary

The IEEPA refund market has matured sharply in the 28 days since the May 6 scan. CBP reported approximately **11.2 million CAPE claims** filed — the market is in full swing. The two most significant developments this cycle are: (1) **Wove has confirmed native TMS integrations with CargoWise, Magaya, GoFreight, and Logiware** — directly attacking Fintella's TMS-embedding exclusivity claim; and (2) a **June 7, 2026 Federal Circuit appeal deadline** where the government is widely expected to challenge the SCOTUS ruling, potentially halting refund processing mid-stream — a market-moving risk that TariffsTool.com is already publishing about, while no broker tool including Fintella appears to be surfacing it prominently. CustomsGenius continues to scale (confirmed 200+ IOR broker workflows), and two new entrants were identified: **ES-003.com** (comprehensive reference + calculator) and **Alliance CHB** (separate firm from Allied CHB, distinct calculator).

### 10. CustomsGenius — https://www.customsgenius.com *(May NE-5 — now in active monitoring)*
**Status: ⚠️ ACTIVELY SCALING | Confirmed broker-at-scale workflow**

- **Confirmed broker use case**: A brokerage used CustomsGenius to scan 200+ importer-of-record (IOR) portfolios, auto-identify CAPE-eligible entries, and generate CSV CAPE declarations grouped by IOR. Multiple declarations auto-batched for IORs exceeding the 9,999-entry-per-declaration limit.

### 11. Wove — https://tariffs.wove.com/us/refunds *(May NE-6 — ESCALATED)*
**Status: ⚠️ THREAT ESCALATED | TMS integrations now confirmed**

May report flagged Wove's API tier as something to monitor. This scan confirms the threat is larger:

- **Confirmed native TMS integrations**: CargoWise, Magaya, GoFreight, and Logiware. Also integrates with NetSuite, QuickBooks, and bill.com on the accounting side. Full REST API for custom integrations.
- **CAPE CSV handling confirmed**: The platform includes an importer sub-account view with a CAPE tab where brokers can upload CAPE CSV declarations and process them.
- **Tariff Calculator API** (from May): Brokers and developers can embed or integrate Wove's calculation engine. API pricing not confirmed publicly.
- **"First analysis is free; no credit card required"** — free entry point.
- **Positioning**: "AI infrastructure for tariffs, compliance, risk, and global logistics workflows." Not just a calculator — an infrastructure play.
- **⚠️ Direct TMS conflict**: Wove is integrated into CargoWise and Magaya — the same TMS platforms Fintella's widget targets. Fintella's "only TMS-embedded calculator" claim requires verification against Wove's integration depth.

## Market Context Updates (Since May 6)

### June 7, 2026 — Federal Circuit Appeal Risk
**⚠️ CRITICAL MARKET RISK — underserved by most tools**

- The government's window to appeal Judge Eaton's IEEPA refund order to the Federal Circuit closes around **June 7, 2026**.
- Trade attorneys widely expect the government to file the appeal. If the Federal Circuit grants a stay, refund processing could halt mid-stream — affecting all in-flight CAPE declarations.
- TariffsTool.com published a dedicated guide: "Tariff Refund Appeal Risk — June 7 Federal Circuit Deadline."
- **No other calculator tool in the competitive set is surfacing this risk prominently to users.** This is an opportunity to differentiate on broker-advisory depth.

### CAPE Filing Volume
- Approximately **11.2 million CAPE claims** filed as of late May 2026 (per Abasto reporting).
- Refund processing timeline: 60–90 days from CAPE Declaration acceptance; first wave issued May 12, 2026.

### CAPE Phase 2 Timeline
- Phase 2 (liquidated entries via protest) expected **Q3–Q4 2026** — no firm CBP date published.
- Phase 2 covers edge cases: partial liquidations, reconciliation entries, FTZ admissions, complex entry types.
- Protest window (180 days from liquidation) is the parallel path — some June 2026 and August 2026 deadlines for Feb–Apr 2025 entries.

## Recommended Actions

### 1. URGENT — Audit and fortify the TMS-embed differentiation claim vs. Wove
**Priority: High — do this week**

Wove now has confirmed native integrations with CargoWise AND Magaya — the same TMS platforms Fintella targets. This is no longer a "watch" item. Before marketing "only TMS-embedded calculator," validate:
- Is the Fintella widget actually embedded inside CargoWise/Magaya UI, or is it a web embed that opens in a browser tab?
- Does Wove's CargoWise integration include the tariff refund calculator, or just their broader logistics workflow tools?
- If Wove's integration is deeper or more native, the differentiation must shift to what Wove **cannot** do: multi-client per-IOR grouping, CAPE pre-validation, commission tracking, and the Dossier-to-Deal pipeline. Those four remain uncontested.

### 2. Publish June 7 Federal Circuit appeal risk prominently — today
**Priority: High — time-sensitive**

The June 7 deadline is 4 days away. TariffsTool.com already has an SEO guide about it. No broker tool is surfacing this risk to users. Adding a banner or alert to Fintella's TIE tool that says something like "Appeal risk: government has until June 7 to challenge the SCOTUS ruling — file your CAPE Declaration before this date to lock in your submission" would:
- Position Fintella as the most legally-aware broker tool
- Drive urgency and conversion (brokers will file now rather than wait)
- Be genuinely useful to broker clients

### 3. Counter CustomsGenius's broker-scale CAPE CSV story with Fintella's pre-validation advantage
**Priority: Medium**

CustomsGenius is now confirmed working at 200+ IOR broker scale — generating and batching CAPE CSV files grouped by IOR with auto-splits for the 9,999-entry limit. Fintella has this same capability. The differentiator is **pre-validation** (CBP mod-10 check digit, liquidation status flags, importer match check) — CustomsGenius's feature has not been confirmed to validate before generating. Run a side-by-side on the Fintella TIE landing page: "We generate CAPE CSVs — and validate them before you upload so you don't get rejected by ACE."
