# Competitive Intelligence Report — 2026-06-10

## Summary

The IEEPA tariff refund market has exploded since the February 20, 2026 SCOTUS ruling in *Learning Resources v. Trump*, with 15+ tools now competing for the estimated $166–175B refund pool across 330,000+ businesses. The market bifurcates cleanly: (1) free single-importer calculators used as broker lead-gen tools (Allied CHB, UNIS, TariffClaim.ai, Trade Lab), and (2) enterprise supply-chain platforms that added IEEPA modeling as a feature (Flexport, TADA, Descartes). **Fintella TIE remains the only tool serving the broker multi-client workflow** — no competitor has a multi-importer broker dashboard, TMS-embedded widget, or commission-tracking pipeline. The CAPE Phase 1 deadline (July 24, 2026) is 44 days out; urgency messaging is underutilized across the competitive set.

## Changes Detected

### 1. Allied CHB — alliedchb.com/refunds

**Status:** Active. No significant changes detected since prior report.

**Features confirmed:**
- Drag-and-drop upload of CBP Form **7501 PDF** (not ES-003 CSV) — distinguishes from ES-003-native competitors
- Scans all IEEPA tariff codes across categories: base reciprocal (9903.01.25), tiered rates (9903.01.43–76), country-specific (9903.02.xx, 9903.96.xx)
- Per-entry breakdown: IEEPA duties paid + estimated refund
- No signup required; data claimed never stored
- Free tool; full-service PSC filing offered as follow-on (broker lead-gen model)

**Positioning:** "Upload your 7501 entry summaries to instantly identify IEEPA tariff refund eligibility." Single-importer self-service → broker engagement CTA.

**Technology:** Rule-based tariff code detection on PDF. No AI/ML. No CAPE CSV generation.

**Gaps vs. Fintella:** No multi-client dashboard, no ES-003 native support, no CAPE CSV auto-generation, no deadline tracking, no TMS embedding.

### 2. Flexport — tariffs.flexport.com/refunds

**Status:** Active. **Phase 2 launched Feb 26, 2026** — AI automation suite added on top of the Phase 1 calculator.

**Phase 1 (Jan 20, 2026):** Free public calculator — upload CBP entry data, estimate IEEPA refund by category. Launched before SCOTUS ruling, updated in real time.

**Phase 2 (Feb 26, 2026):** Full automation suite:
- **"Audit Your Customs Broker"** — AI agent reviews all past entries for compliance errors (not just IEEPA; also catches tariff stacking, filing inaccuracies)
- Automated CAPE filing prep — estimates + prepares filing-ready CSVs
- Per-IOR CSV generation (brokers can submit separate CSVs per importer)
- Powered by **Flexport Atlas** — proprietary trade data platform

**Positioning:** "AI-driven audit technology with veteran customs expertise to identify, calculate, and claim refunds." "Show what you're owed in 5 minutes." The "Audit Your Customs Broker" tool is an aggressive competitive moat builder — creates anxiety about competitors' filing accuracy.

**Technology:** Explicitly AI-powered. Server-side processing (data leaves device). Flexport Atlas data layer. Most sophisticated technology of any competitor.

**Pricing:** Free calculator (public). Full automation suite gated to Flexport logistics customers (retention play). No standalone paid tier.

**Gaps vs. Fintella:** No multi-client broker dashboard (Flexport-customer-only), no TMS embedding outside Flexport's own platform, no commission tracking, no referral pipeline.

**Notable:** Only competitor with CAPE CSV prep capability that is publicly documented. Gated, but it signals the market is moving in that direction.

### 3. UNIS (Unisco) — unisco.com/ieepa-refund-calculator

**Status:** Active. No significant changes detected since prior report.

**Features confirmed:**
- ACE **ES-003 CSV** native upload (browser-parsed, no server transmission)
- Multi-category auto-classification: IEEPA (9903.01/02), Section 301 (9903.88), Section 232, Section 122
- IEEPA entries flagged as refund-eligible with duty amounts
- **180-day protest deadline calculation** for liquidated entries + urgent-case highlighting
- Sample data load (no file required to demo)
- 100% client-side — "no data is transmitted to any server"

**Positioning:** Privacy-forward ("your data never leaves your browser"). Technically capable audience (requires ACE access to pull ES-003).

**Technology:** Client-side JavaScript, rule-based classification. No AI.

**Gaps vs. Fintella:** No multi-client dashboard, no CAPE CSV generation, no broker workflow, no TMS embedding, no commission tracking. Tracks 180-day protest deadline but not CAPE 80-day window.

**Technology:** CSV and Excel parsing. Processing details unclear. No explicit AI for refund calculator; broader platform is AI-adjacent.

**Gaps vs. Fintella:** No multi-client broker dashboard, no CAPE CSV generation, no TMS embedding, no commission tracking.

**Notable:** Excel (.xlsx) support alongside CSV is unique — no other competitor offers this. Positions well for less-technical importers who manage entry data in spreadsheets.

### 5. TariffClaim.ai — tariffclaim.ai

**Notable:** Claims-financing market context — hedge funds are offering 70–90 cents on the dollar for CAPE refund claims. TariffClaim.ai appears to bridge importers to that market. Distinct business model (claims facilitation vs. broker lead-gen).

### 6. TariffRefundIQ — tariffrefundiq.com

**Status:** Active. Most financially sophisticated tool in the market.

**Features confirmed:**
- **Probability-weighted NPV model** — expected value of claim accounting for recovery rate, timing, discount rate
- **Sensitivity analysis** across all three variables
- **"Sell vs. hold" recommendation engine** — advises whether to sell claim to a finance firm or hold for direct CBP payment
- Client-side only — "your financial data never leaves your device"
- **Pro subscription** with ongoing legal/regulatory updates (CIT proceedings, congressional action, CBP guidance)

**Positioning:** "Know your number before someone lowballs you." Targets financially sophisticated importers making monetization decisions on their CAPE claims. Anti-predatory framing vs. claims-finance firms.

**Technology:** Browser-based financial modeling (JavaScript). No AI/ML — this is financial modeling, not classification.

**Pricing:** **Free tier + Pro subscription** — only competitor with a visible recurring revenue model.

**Gaps vs. Fintella:** Not a filing tool at all — a decision-support/valuation tool. No ES-003 upload, no CAPE CSV, no multi-client dashboard.

## Recommended Actions

### 1. Add a no-signup public "quick scan" entry point (HIGH PRIORITY)

**Gap:** Every major competitor (Allied CHB, Flexport, UNIS, TariffClaim.ai, Trade Lab) offers a free, no-signup calculator. Fintella currently requires broker account creation before a partner can run a calculation. This creates friction for broker acquisition — prospective partners can't self-qualify their book of business before signing up.

**Recommendation:** Add a public `/calculator` page that accepts a single ES-003 CSV upload and returns a summary (total entries, estimated refund pool, breakdown by tariff category). No data stored. Add a "Want the full CAPE CSV + multi-client dashboard? Sign up as a broker partner" CTA. This mirrors the Allied CHB model but with ES-003 native support and a broker-recruitment angle rather than a single-importer brokerage-services CTA.

### 2. Add Excel (.xlsx) upload support to the ES-003 parser (MEDIUM PRIORITY)

**Gap:** The Trade Lab supports Excel (.xlsx) alongside CSV; no other competitor offers this. A meaningful segment of brokers and importers manages their entry data in Excel rather than raw ACE CSV exports.

**Recommendation:** Add `.xlsx` file parsing to the existing ES-003 upload handler. Libraries like `xlsx` (SheetJS) handle this client-side or server-side. This is a low-effort differentiation: it removes a friction point for non-technical importers who may have reformatted their ACE export in Excel.

### 3. Add a "claim value" module with sell vs. hold guidance (LOW PRIORITY, HIGH DIFFERENTIATION)

**Gap:** TariffRefundIQ is the only tool with a probability-weighted NPV + sell vs. hold engine. TariffClaim.ai hints at this with investor valuation language. As the Phase 1 deadline approaches, more importers will face pressure from trade-finance firms offering to buy their CAPE claims at 70–90 cents on the dollar.

**Recommendation:** Add a "Claim Value" tab or modal to the broker dashboard that, given estimated refund amount, shows: (1) face value, (2) estimated claims-financing offer range (70–90%), (3) NPV at 3 scenarios (fast/nominal/slow CBP processing), (4) simple "hold is worth X% more than selling" framing. This positions Fintella as an advisor, not just a filing tool, and increases stickiness with importers making monetization decisions. Could also open a referral-fee opportunity with claims-financing firms.
