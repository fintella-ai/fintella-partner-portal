# Competitive Intelligence Report — 2026-07-08

## Summary

The IEEPA refund calculator market exploded following the February 20, 2026 Supreme Court ruling in *Learning Resources, Inc. v. Trump* (6-3) striking down all IEEPA-based tariffs. At least 8 new entrants have appeared since the last scan. Flexport has made the most aggressive product move — 25+ new AI features including a customs AI auditor claiming a 0.2% error rate and automated CAPE filing. No competitor has matched Fintella's TMS-embedding, multi-client broker dashboard, or commission tracking. The 80-day CAPE deadline window (counting from entry liquidation) is the key urgency driver across all messaging.

> **Market context**: CAPE portal launched April 20, 2026. As of May 14, CBP had processed $35.4B of an estimated $166B owed across ~330,000 importers. CAPE Phase 2 (reconciliation entries) went live June 29, 2026. ACH refund payments began May 12, 2026.

## Changes Detected

### 1. Allied CHB — https://www.alliedchb.com/refunds
**Status: Active — no major changes detected**

- 7501 PDF drag-and-drop upload, no signup required
- Parses every line for all IEEPA duties: China/HK, reciprocal, tiered, country-specific
- Per-entry breakdown of IEEPA duties paid and estimated refund amounts
- Data not stored — processed in real-time and discarded
- Licensed CHB team handles full PSC process from start to finish
- **No CAPE CSV generation detected**
- **No multi-client broker dashboard detected**
- Positioning: importers + their brokers, white-glove service upsell

### 2. Flexport — https://tariffs.flexport.com/refunds
**Status: SIGNIFICANT NEW FEATURES — biggest market move**

Flexport launched 25+ new AI products as part of their Customs Technology Suite, including:

- **AI Auditor**: AI agent that conducts a compliance audit of all past customs entries to identify mistakes. Claims a 0.2% error rate — "10X improvement to any other broker in the world" (BusinessWire, Feb 26, 2026)
- **Automated CAPE filing**: Flexport couples their AI auditor with automated tariff refund preparation and submission
- **Tariff Refund Calculator** at `tariffs.flexport.com/refunds`: free, bulk entry upload
- **Sample report** available at `tariffs.flexport.com/refunds/sample-report`
- **Flexport Atlas**: described as "the data foundation for the future of autonomous trade"
- Positioning: existing Flexport freight customers (requires account), targeting enterprise importers

**Takeaway for Fintella**: Flexport is vertically integrating from freight → customs → refund in one AI-powered loop. Their moat is the existing customer base with entry data already on file. They are NOT a broker-facing tool.

### 3. UNIS / ITEM — https://www.unisco.com/ieepa-refund-calculator
**Status: Active — also listed on ITEM marketplace at https://marketplace.item.com/ieepa-refund-calculator/**

- ACE ES-003 CSV analyzer, client-side processing (data never leaves browser)
- Classifies every entry line by tariff type: IEEPA (9903.01/02), Section 301 (9903.88), Section 232, Section 122
- IEEPA entries flagged as refund-eligible with duty amounts calculated
- Deadline tracking (protest window tracking)
- Now cross-listed on ITEM marketplace — distribution play
- 5.9% general rate increase reflected as of January 1, 2026
- **No CAPE CSV generation detected**
- **No multi-client dashboard detected**
- Positioning: importers and brokers who have ACE ES-003 access

### 4. The Trade Lab — https://thetradelab.ai/refund-calculator
**Status: Active + platform expansion**

- IEEPA refund calculator (CSV/Excel upload, estimates by country and date range)
- Identifies IEEPA tariff line items by Chapter 99 codes
- 180-day protest deadline tracking
- **New**: AI Voice Assistant at `chat.thetradelab.ai` — customs compliance chatbot
- **New**: Customs Broker AI Training + CE Credits platform at `learn.thetradelab.ai`
- Additional tools: HTS tariff calculator, landed cost calculator, Section 301 reference
- Positioning: expanding from calculator → full customs broker AI training platform
- Target: customs brokers, trade compliance professionals

### 5. TariffClaim.ai — https://tariffclaim.ai
**Status: Active — now has separate app at https://app.tariffclaim.ai/**

- Refund calculator at `/refund-calculator`: ACE ES-003 upload, no signup required
- **App** at `app.tariffclaim.ai`: suggests a more feature-complete authenticated product now exists
- Models refund claim as an asset: "estimate what your claim might be worth to investors"
- Investor/liquidity angle is distinct positioning — refund monetization play
- **No CAPE CSV generation detected**
- Positioning: importers who want to sell or monetize their refund claim

### 6. TariffRefundIQ — https://www.tariffrefundiq.com
**Status: Active — niche differentiation maintained**

- Probability-weighted NPV model with 7 scenarios and sensitivity analysis
- Runs entirely in-browser (client-side, no data upload required — manual entry only)
- Scenarios model recovery rates, timing, and discount rates
- Methodologically rigorous — no CSV upload, purely financial modeling
- Positioning: CFOs, finance teams, sophisticated importers who want expected-value math rather than an estimate
- **No upload, no CAPE CSV, no broker features**

### 7. TADA — https://mytada.com
**Status: Active — enterprise focus deepening**

- Tariff estimator at `mytada.com/microapps/resource/quicktools/tariff-estimator`
- SKU-level tariff risk analysis and margin impact prioritization
- Landed cost modeling (freight + duty + surcharges)
- IEEPA tariff estimator (overpayment recovery modeling)
- Fortune 500 positioned, enterprise system integration
- Trusted by Fortune 500 companies — not a self-serve broker tool
- **No CAPE CSV generation, no broker dashboard detected**

### 8. SimplyDuty — https://www.simplyduty.com/import-calculator/
**Status: No IEEPA-specific features detected**

- General-purpose import duty calculator, not an IEEPA refund tool
- No evidence of IEEPA refund or CAPE support added
- Positioned for general duty calculation, likely not a direct competitor for the IEEPA refund use case

### 9. Descartes CustomsInfo
**Status: No IEEPA-specific features detected**

- Enterprise classification and duty determination platform
- No evidence of IEEPA refund calculator or CAPE CSV support in public materials
- Enterprise sales motion — not a self-serve calculator competitor

**Wove** is the most technically sophisticated new entrant: they built entry-level Chapter 99 rate calculation accounting for 35 tariff rate changes, launched an API (`wove.com/blog/wove-tariff-calculator-api`), and published a detailed engineering writeup. They could enable competitors to embed their calculation engine via API.

**KlearNow.AI** has the most novel business model: a refund buyback program that gives importers immediate liquidity while KlearNow manages CAPE filing. This is different from calculators — it's a financial product.

### Fintella's Defensible Advantages

1. **TMS-embedded widget** — no other tool is embedded in CargoWise or Magaya. This is a distribution moat. No evidence any competitor is pursuing this.
2. **Broker-first multi-client dashboard** — competitors target importers directly. Fintella's per-importer grouping is the only broker workflow tool in the market.
3. **Dossier-to-Deal pipeline** — the only tool that converts a calculator session into a trackable referral with commission. Zero competitors have this.
4. **CAPE CSV pre-validation** — Allied CHB, UNIS, and The Trade Lab don't generate CAPE-ready CSV. Fintella's mod-10 entry validation reduces ACE rejection risk.

## Recommended Actions

### 1. Activate AI Document Intake (High Priority — closes biggest gap)
Flexport's AI auditor and Gateway's "i-Sight AI" are both offering OCR/AI-powered CF-7501 analysis. Fintella has the `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` integration already built but demo-gated. **Activating the PartnerOS AI** to parse 7501 PDFs and auto-populate entry fields would match or exceed Flexport's intake capability and position Fintella as the AI-native broker tool. This is the #1 competitive gap.

### 2. Add API / Embeddable Widget Distribution Layer (Medium Priority)
Wove has an API. If competitors start licensing Wove's calculation engine to embed in their own tools, the calculation accuracy gap closes. Fintella should offer an embeddable widget (similar to TMS embed, but for freight forwarders' client portals) and consider a public API for affiliated broker networks. This extends the TMS-embed moat beyond CargoWise/Magaya.

### 3. Add Refund Claim Financing Explainer or Partner (Opportunistic)
KlearNow's refund buyback program is a novel angle — importers often have cash flow pressure while waiting the 60–90 day ACE processing window. Fintella could add an educational section or partner referral to a financing provider within the Dossier-to-Deal pipeline. No build required if treated as a referral/affiliate play.
