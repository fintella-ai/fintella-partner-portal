# Competitive Intelligence Report — 2026-09-02

## Summary

The IEEPA refund market has materially shifted since the last scan (2026-05-06). With ~$106.6B of the ~$166B refund pool already certified to Treasury (~64% disbursed), the "estimation" phase of this market is largely over and competition is now concentrated on **managed filing services** and **Phase 3 complexity**. CAPE Phase 3 (finally liquidated entries) was delayed on August 25 after programming was declared ready in late July, and critically, Phase 3 refunds appear limited to importers who filed CIT lawsuits — non-plaintiff importers have no confirmed mechanism. Three new managed-filing competitors entered the market charging 2% success fees. **Fintella's TMS embedding, multi-client broker dashboard, and commission tracking remain unmatched** — no competitor has shipped any of these features.

## Market Context (Critical Updates Since May 6 Scan)

| Milestone | Status |
|-----------|--------|
| CAPE Phase 1 (unliquidated + 80-day window) | Live since April 20, 2026 |
| CAPE Phase 2 (reconciliation entries, ~$28.7B) | Live since June 29, 2026 |
| CAPE Phase 3 (finally liquidated, ~$11.4B) | **Delayed** — CBP Aug 25 notice; CIT-plaintiff importers only |
| CAPE declarations submitted | 272,029 as of Aug 21, 2026 |
| Declarations passing file validation | 191,494 (70% pass rate) |
| Pool certified to Treasury | ~$106.6B (64% of ~$166B) |
| Refunds held — missing ACH info | $1.7B (22,170 importers affected) |
| Federal Circuit government appeal | Still pending |

**Key implication**: The market is in a "filing crunch" phase — most low-hanging fruit (Phase 1) is filed, Phase 2 is running, and Phase 3 uncertainty is adding complexity. Brokers need advisory tools more than simple calculators now.

## New Entrants (Identified Since May 6, 2026)

### NE-11. Alliance Drawback Services — https://alliancechb.com/ieepa-refund-calculator/

- **Tool**: Free browser-based IEEPA refund calculator (ES-003 upload; files stay in browser only).
- **Managed service**: Full CAPE submission management via "Apollo" platform — **2% success fee** (min $10K, max $100K cap).
- **Pricing model**: Transparent success-based. "Apollo" described as a cloud-based optimization platform built from decades of drawback experience.
- **Coverage**: All IEEPA tariff types; routes data to broker team for CAPE submission.
- **CAPE CSV generation**: Not self-serve; managed filing only.
- **Threat level: MEDIUM** — the 2% managed-service pricing model directly competes for brokers who want to outsource CAPE filing rather than manage it themselves. Fintella's commission-tracking infrastructure could be positioned as a counter — brokers earn their own margin while Fintella handles the pipeline.

## Recommended Actions

### 1. URGENT — Add Phase 2 and Phase 3 eligibility flags to the ES-003 analyzer
With CAPE Phase 2 (reconciliation entries) live since June 29 and Phase 3 (finally liquidated entries, CIT plaintiffs only) pending, the ES-003 analyzer needs to flag each entry's CAPE phase eligibility. Brokers advising clients on "can we still file?" now need Phase 2 and Phase 3 context per entry. Currently **no competitor does this** — it's an open differentiator. Specifically: flag entries as Phase 1 eligible / Phase 2 eligible / Phase 3 CIT-plaintiff-only / not eligible, and surface the count and dollar exposure in each bucket. This becomes the single most actionable broker advisory output in the market.

### 2. MODERATE — Counter 1st Capital Financial's free CAPE Declaration download by foregrounding pre-validation
1st Capital Financial (NE-12) now offers a free ACE-ready CAPE Declaration CSV without signup, which narrows one of Fintella's stated advantages. However, there is no evidence they run CBP mod-10 check digit validation, batch size ≤9,999 enforcement, or liquidation status flags. **Recommended**: Add a visible "Pre-Validation Report" output panel to the Fintella CAPE CSV generator that itemizes every validation check run before the CSV is generated. "We validate so CBP doesn't reject" is a concrete counter-message. The 30% rejection rate in CAPE (272K submitted, 191K passing) is a compelling stat to reference.

### 3. MODERATE — Build an ACH readiness guide / checklist into the partner portal
As of August 21, 2026, **$1.7 billion is stuck** due to missing ACH bank account information (22,170 importers). CustomsGenius is the only competitor providing ACH guidance. This is a high-value, zero-cost feature for Fintella to add — a simple "Make Sure You Get Paid" checklist covering ACE account ACH setup, bank account registration, and CBP notification settings. Brokers advising clients need this now. Positions Fintella as the tool that handles the full refund journey, not just the CAPE CSV.

### 4. LONGER-TERM — Consider Phase 3 CIT lawsuit tracking as a broker feature
Phase 3 refunds are limited to importers who filed CIT lawsuits. Brokers need to track which of their clients are plaintiffs vs. non-plaintiffs and communicate different guidance accordingly. No competitor is building this workflow. A simple "CIT Plaintiff flag" per importer in the multi-client dashboard — along with a reminder that Phase 3 is not available to non-plaintiffs — would differentiate the broker-facing advisory experience materially.
